---
title: Shopping lists
description: Lists filled from recipes and by hand, sorted by department, shared by invitation with other people on the instance, with server-side scaling and instance-wide department overrides
---

A signed-in person keeps any number of shopping lists, alone or shared with others on the instance, fills them from recipes (the basket on a recipe's ingredient stub) and by typing, ticks items off and sees them either by store department or by recipe. The service side is `service/internal/shopping` (pure rules, service, huma handler); the frontend is `frontend/src/routes/lists/`, `frontend/src/lib/components/shopping/`, `frontend/src/lib/shopping/` and the client `frontend/src/lib/api/shopping.ts`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Traps">

- Rounding exists twice: `service/internal/shopping/scale.go` ports `frontend/src/lib/recipe/scale.ts`, and `TestRoundScaledMatchesFrontend` carries the frontend's table; change both files and both tables ([Adding and scaling](#adding-and-scaling)).
- A demo ingredient that lands in the `other` department fails `TestDemoIngredientsAreSorted` ([Names and departments](#names-and-departments)).
- A changed `NameKey` does not rewrite the keys already stored with items ([Names and departments](#names-and-departments)).
- A buffering reverse proxy breaks the stream ([Live updates](#live-updates)).
- Every change answers with the whole list, so invitations are sent one after another, never in parallel, and the page drops an answer older than its newest change ([The list page](#the-list-page)).

</Callout>

## How it works

### Access

**Access is the creator or an accepted member.** Every query is scoped by one predicate: `created_by = me` or an accepted row in `shopping_list_members` for me. Anyone else, a pending invitee included, gets exactly the answer an id that does not exist gets: `shopping.ErrNotFound`, HTTP 404, never 403, on reads and on every change, so a guessed id tells nobody that the list exists (`TestForeignShoppingListAnswers404`, `TestPendingInviteeCannotRead`). Admins have no extra reach. Deleting an account deletes its lists and its memberships. The tables and their delete rules are on [Data model](/architecture/data-model#shopping-lists).

**Members do everything but share and delete.** Add and remove recipes and items, tick, rename, change servings: all of it runs under the access predicate. Sharing, removing members and deleting the list check `created_by` instead (`ownList`); a member who tries gets 404, as a stranger does.

### Adding and scaling

**Adding a recipe copies it.** `AddRecipe` writes one entry (title, the recipe's servings as `base_servings`, the chosen `servings`) and one item per picked ingredient, picked by `(group, index)` into the recipe as it is at that moment. The items keep name, unscaled quantity, unit, note and group name. Editing the recipe afterwards never reaches the list (`TestRecipeEditDoesNotReachList`); deleting it nulls `recipe_id` and the list keeps the entry's title without a link (`TestDeletedRecipeKeepsItems`). Adding the same recipe twice makes two entries. An unknown recipe is `ErrRecipeNotFound` (404 "recipe not found"), an empty pick list `ErrInvalid` (422).

**Quantities scale on the server, on every read.** `get` scales an entry's items from `base_servings` to `servings` with `ScaleQuantity` and rounds with `RoundScaled` in `service/internal/shopping/scale.go`, a port of `frontend/src/lib/recipe/scale.ts` (thirds and quarters below ten, whole numbers from ten). `TestRoundScaledMatchesFrontend` carries the rows of `frontend/src/lib/recipe/scale.test.ts`: a change to rounding on one side changes both files and both tables. Changing an entry's servings is one column; its items rescale, ticked ones too (a tick stays a tick), and an item without a quantity stays without one (`TestSetServingsRescalesAndKeepsTicks`).

**Typed and imported text split the same way.** `ParseFree` in `freetext.go` is `recipeimport.SplitItem` - the recipe import's ingredient split with every language's units and number words, without its keep-whole fallback or verdict ([Recipe import](/features/recipe-import#the-service)) - plus a " - " / " – " note when that split found none. "2 l Milch", "500g Mehl", "3Zitronen" and "½ Bund Dill" get amount and unit, "Eier (6 Stück)" the note "6 Stück", "2 l Milch, 1,5 %" the note "1,5 %", while "1 l Milch 1,5 %" keeps "Milch 1,5 %" as its name (a comma between digits is a decimal comma). A text that leaves no name ("2") stays whole as the name. One parser for typed and imported items is the point: two parsers gave two rows for one thing (`TestParseFree`, `TestParseFreeKeepsListShapes`).

**The add card takes amount and unit apart.** The list page's add card (`AddItemCard`, reusing `UnitInput`) has the recipe editor's amount and unit fields; `add-shopping-item` takes optional `quantity` and `unit` that replace what `ParseFree` found in `text` (`Service.AddItem`), so typing "2 l Milch" into the name alone still works, as it does over MCP. The added item's row flashes where it landed, without scrolling, since several items are typed in a row, and a toast names its department.

**Every item remembers who added and who ticked it.** A free item keeps `added_by`, shown as "by …": `Source.addedBy` keeps the whole `ListPerson`, and `addedByName` is the first name, the full name when two people on the list share it (`holderNames` in `#lib/user/holders.ts`). An item whose adder's account is gone shows "from a former member" (`list_added_by_former`). `SetChecked` sets and clears `checked_by` with `checked_at`, returned as `checkedBy` only while ticked. On a shared list a done row ends in the ticker's mark and first name ("You" for your own), several people's marks stacked without names; an open row shows someone else's `PersonMark` before "by Jonas", and a done row names the adder in words only, so a row never holds two faces.

### Importing text

**A pasted list imports in one call.** `ParseListText` (`listtext.go`) readies the text with `recipeimport.PrepLines` (so a ticked checkbox imports open), skips empty lines, skips every `Liste:` / `List:` line (`listNameWords`, one word per app language) and names the list from the first, skips a line without a leading amount that ends in ":" as a heading, and reads every other line with `ParseFree`. A merged row as "Copy as text" writes it, `<amount>[ <unit>] + <amount>[ <unit>] … <name>` ("2 Stück + 200 g Zwiebeln"), becomes one item per amount with the last part's name and note, so the list shows the same merged row again (`TestListTextRoundTrip` holds a copy of `LIST_TEXT_CASES` from `rows.test.ts`; a change to `listText` changes both by hand, since nothing links them); if any earlier part does not read as an amount for that name, the line is one item. Prose an AI adds anyway becomes an item, which the client's undo covers. `testdata/ai/` holds real answers to the list instruction (`TestParseListTextAIAnswers`).

**An import refuses, never cuts.** Each refusal is a `422` at `body.text` whose message is a code for the client to translate: more than 200 items (`too-many-items`, `ErrTooManyItems`), none (`no-items`, `ErrNoItems`, and no list is made), and a line whose name passes 120 or note 200 runes (`line-too-long:<line>`, `*LineError`; the line counts in the pasted text, a fence's own line included). `SplitItem` reports whether `clampIngredient` cut anything, and exactly those lines are refused.

**Two endpoints, both answering with the whole list.** `POST /shopping-lists/import` (`ImportList`, `201`) names the new list by the body's `name` if given, else the name line, else `defaultName` (the client's catalog "Einkaufsliste" / "Shopping list"), cut at a word boundary to 80, and writes list and items in one transaction; `POST /shopping-lists/{id}/import` (`ImportItems`) appends after the last position in one `mutate` and keeps the list's name. Both write free items added by the caller and publish the change, so the client finds the added items by their ids for "Undo" (`RemoveItems`). An import never writes a department override.

**Text reaches a list from two places, through one dialog.** A new list's import and the list menu's "Add from text" (above "Copy as text") both open `ListImportDialog` (`mode` `new` or `add`; `BaseDialog` from `md`, `BottomSheet` below, as the recipe import). The `new` one is mounted once in the root layout and opened through `listImport` (`#lib/shopping/new-list.svelte.ts`), as `importDialog` is for recipes; the layout closes it on a navigation, as a page would have taken it along, and a closed dialog forgets its paste, name and error. It holds the recipe import's dashed field with an example list as its placeholder (`list_import_example`, with its "List:" line; `list_add_text_example` without, since adding keeps the list's name), in `new` mode "Choose a name", the `AiInstruction` card (`kind` `list`), and the primary button, which a phone's sheet keeps at its foot. "Choose a name" is a disclosure: the paste's "List:" line or the default name fits most lists, so the name is an override, and a name left in the field after folding it away again is not sent. The dialog sends `importShoppingList` with the catalog's `list_import_default_name` as `defaultName`, or `importIntoShoppingList`, and words a 422's code in the dialog (`list_import_too_many`, `list_import_no_items`, `list_import_line_too_long`, and `list_import_too_long` for the API's own 20,000-character limit, which answers without a code); any other failure goes to `failList`.

**There is no preview, and Undo takes back only my imported items.** "Import" (the recipe import's `import_submit`, so both imports name their action alike) opens the new list, and the list page is the preview. After "Add" the dialog hands the answer and the ids of the items it did not have before and the caller added (`addedIds` in `#lib/shopping/added.ts`, so a member's item that landed meanwhile stays) to the page, which takes the answer through `ListState.apply` and toasts the count with "Undo"; Undo sends `removeItems` for exactly those ids through `apply` too, so a later change's answer still wins. `e2e/shopping-lists.test.ts` imports the spec's list, refuses 201 lines without making a list, ignores a folded-away name and takes an open one, adds two items and takes them back, and on a phone checks the sheet's thumb.

### Names and departments

**Names meet through a key.** `NameKey` lowercases, trims, collapses spaces, folds French and Spanish accents (é, ç, ñ…, never ä, ö, ü, ß) and strips a German or English plural ending from the last word, with `irregularPlurals` for what the suffix rules cannot reach ("Äpfel", "Eier"). "Zwiebel", "Zwiebeln" and " zwiebeln " share a key. The key is stored with the item when it is written.

**A department comes from an override or the word list.** `shopping_department_overrides` wins; otherwise `DefaultDepartment` looks the key up in `words` in `departments.go`, one German and English word list per department, exact match first, then the longest known word the key ends with ("rinderhackfleisch" ends with "hackfleisch"), which needs four letters or a word boundary so "graham" never lands on "ham". A qualifier after a comma or in parentheses is cut off for the lookup only. Unknown names go to `other`. The order of `Departments` is the aisle order on the page. Recipes carry no language, so every name is looked up in both languages at once.

**Every demo ingredient has a department.** `TestDemoIngredientsAreSorted` puts every ingredient of both demo sample sets through the lookup and fails on any that lands in `other` without a reason in `demoOther`, which is empty. The sample recipes stand for real ones, and a common ingredient in `other` defeats the sorting.

**Moving an item teaches the whole instance.** The item sheet's department choice calls `PUT /api/v1/shopping-departments` with the row's name; `SetDepartment` upserts the override for its key, and every list of every person reads it on its next load (`TestDepartmentOverrideAppliesToAllLists`).

### The API

**The API takes a session or a token and every change answers with the whole list.** The routes are in `handler.go` under the `shopping-lists` tag; the three readers (list, get, events) need `lists:read`, every other operation `lists:write` (`auth.Protected`), and a token acts as its issuer, so it sees only the issuer's lists (`TestTokenActsAsIssuer`). The operations: list, create, get, rename and delete lists; add a recipe, change an entry's servings, remove an entry; add a free item, create a list from text or add text to one, tick or untick and remove items by id, clear the ticked ones; set a department. Each change runs in one transaction that first touches the list (which proves ownership and moves `updated_at`) and returns the list as it is afterwards, scaled and sorted, so the client replaces its copy instead of patching it.

### Live updates

**Every committed change is announced over a stream.** `GET /api/v1/shopping-lists/{id}/events` (`stream.go`, `lists:read`, 404 like every other read) answers `text/event-stream`: `retry: 3000` first, then `event: changed` / `data: {"revision":N}` per change and a `: ping` comment every 25 s (`pingEvery`, shortened in tests through `export_test.go`). A reverse proxy must not buffer the path: the reverse-proxy page of the user docs says how, and an open list holds one HTTP/1.1 connection of the browser's six per host. `stream_test.go` runs against a real server ([Streaming responses](/conventions/testing#streaming-responses)).

**`revision` counts the list's changes, never a timestamp.** The column `shopping_lists.revision` is also on every list answer. `TouchShoppingList` and `RenameShoppingList` raise it in the change's own statement, `Service.changed` raises it for member changes and `SetDepartment` for every list (`TestRevisionCountsEveryChange`). The stream reads it when it sends, so the event names the list as it is then; a page that shows that revision already - its own change, whose answer carried it - skips the refetch. A timestamp would not do ([Detecting change](/conventions/go#detecting-change)): two people ticking in the same second would share an `updated_at`.

**`Hub` (`hub.go`) signals open streams after each commit.** It holds one buffered channel of size one per open stream, so a burst of changes coalesces into one signal and a publisher never blocks. The service publishes after the transaction committed - every `mutate`, `Rename`, `Delete`, `Invite` that inserted a row, `RemoveMember`, `Accept`, `Leave` - and `SetDepartment` signals every open stream, since an override reaches every list (`TestChangesPublish`). After each signal the stream reads the revision through `GetShoppingListRevision`, which finds no row without access, so a removed member's stream ends (`TestStreamEndsWhenAccessLost`). Every `Service` makes its own hub; `main.go` registers `Service.Hub().Close` with `httpserver.Server.OnShutdown`, which ends every stream when shutdown starts instead of letting `Shutdown` wait for them.

**The client follows the stream and refetches.** `followList` (`frontend/src/lib/shopping/live.ts`) opens an `EventSource` per list id with credentials and calls `onchange` for each `changed` event, coalescing a burst within 150 ms into one call with the burst's highest revision (none once an `open` or an error joined it), and again on every `open`, the first included: a change between the page's own fetch and the subscription, or during a reconnect, sends no event. When the server ends the stream for good the reconnect gets a 404 and the source closes; `onchange` then fires once, so the refetch meets the same 404 and the page shows `list_gone`. The list page and the shopping-mode page both subscribe by list id in an `$effect`, so linking from one list to the next re-subscribes.

**`ListState` (`list-state.svelte.ts`) owns the list, its revision, `toggle` and `refresh`.** Both list pages call `connect`, which takes each later page load and follows the live stream. `follow` refetches unless the page shows the event's revision already. `refresh` lets what another device ticked linger for `LINGER_MS`: `shown` counts those items as open so their row stays in place, shown ticked and lit before it moves to Done. `refresh` passes only a 404 or a lost session to the page's `onfail` (`failList` in `fail.ts`: toast, and `/lists` on a 404); any other failure passes quietly, since the next event or reconnect retries.

**A gone list leaves the open page within seconds.** When a person is removed (or the list deleted) while the page is open, the server ends their stream, the reconnect gets 404 and the refetch meets the same 404: the page shows the toast `list_gone` and goes to `/lists` within about 3 s (`failList`). A change sent before that gets the same 404 and the same answer. An invitation withdrawn (or its list deleted) while the overview is open answers Accept or Decline with 404: the card drops out, the dot count follows, and the toast is `list_gone`.

### Sharing

**Sharing is an invitation the invitee answers.** `Invite(userID, listID, inviteeID)` is the creator's alone, refuses oneself and an unknown account (`ErrInvalid`), and inserts a `shopping_list_members` row without `accepted_at`; inviting someone who already has a row (pending or accepted) changes nothing and notifies nobody (`TestInviteIsIdempotent`). `Accept` sets `accepted_at` on the caller's own pending row, 404 without one. `Leave` deletes the caller's row, accepted or pending, so declining and leaving are one call; the creator cannot leave (`TestCreatorCannotLeave`). `RemoveMember` is the creator withdrawing an invitation or removing a member.

**The sharing endpoints and shapes.** `POST /shopping-lists/{id}/invitations {userId}`, `DELETE …/members/{userId}`, `POST …/membership` (accept) and `DELETE …/membership` (leave or decline). The overview answers `{items, invitations}`; an invitation carries only the list's name, the inviter and the open count, nothing of its content. A `List` names `owner`, `members` and, for the creator only, `pending`; a `Summary` adds `updatedBy`, the person who changed the list last (`shopping_lists.updated_by`, null once that account is gone).

**The invitation notifies at once.** `shopping.Service.SetInviteNotifier` takes the `notify` service, called only when the insert added a row, so a repeated invite sends nothing. The kind is `list_invite` ([Notifications](/features/notifications)). The demo seed sets no notifier, so seeding invitations mails nobody.

**The sharing UI.** The overview shows an invitation card per pending invitation (Accept, Decline) above two sections, "Your lists" and "Shared with you"; a card's footer reads "Only you", "Shared with …", "From … · with …" and `Changed by {name} {when}`. An invitation puts a dot on the lists link and slot, from `frontend/src/lib/shopping/invitations.svelte.ts`, which the overview and the shell keep in step with the count. On the list page the owner has "Share" (`ShareDialog`: `InvitePicker`, "Has access" with the owner, members and pending invitees, × to remove) and a menu with Delete; a member has no Share, and the menu offers "Leave list" with a confirmation. On a shared list the faces in the header open `PeopleSheet`, so phones and tablets, which have no side column, show who is on the list too.

**Inviting is pick, then send.** `InvitePicker` is a Bits UI `Combobox` (`type="multiple"`) whose picks sit in the field as chips; typing narrows by display name and username (accents folded), and every suggestion and chip shows the person's `PersonMark` and `@username`, so two people with one first name tell apart. Picking sends nothing; the footer button counts the picks and sends one `inviteToList` per person through the page's `apply`, one after another, since each answers with the whole list and parallel answers could land out of order. The status line names who was invited (`list_share_sent`) or who was not (`list_share_error`): those stay picked as chips marked "Not invited". Opening the dialog drops any pick left from last time, so closing never invites; a send still running from an earlier opening writes nothing back (`run` counts openings). Only picks still among the candidates count (`live`), so someone invited meanwhile from another device drops out of the send. Picking and unpicking are announced in a polite live region (`list_share_picked`, `list_share_unpicked`), since the chip itself is silent; a failed load of the people picker toasts `users_load_error`.

**The picker follows the Combobox rules of [Bits UI](/conventions/svelte#bits-ui)**, each commented in `InvitePicker.svelte`: `cleared` empties the field after a pick, the list opens on click, and `ContentStatic` keeps it in the flow under the field.

**The share tests pick people by `@username`** ([Picking a person](/conventions/testing#picking-a-person)). `e2e/shopping-lists.test.ts` picks two (one taken off with Backspace and picked again), invites, closes with a third picked and checks the reopened dialog holds no pick; a second test fails one invitation once through `page.route` and retries it.

### The overview and a new list

**The overview before a first list explains the lists.** With no list and no invitation, `/lists` shows a `NoteEmptyState` ([Design system](/architecture/design-system#components)) with the mascot's basket pose (`rezepte-mascot-shopping.webp`) and the three steps - start a list, add recipes with the basket, fill in and share - and "New list" at the note's end.

**New list and the import are where New recipe and its import are.** On the lists the top bar's actions become "New list" and "Import list" (the circle), and the phone's "+" opens the New sheet as "New list" with the recipe's two ways, "Start one yourself" and "Import" ([App shell](/architecture/design-system#app-shell)). The overview's title has its line to itself and no buttons, as the recipe overview's, so "Shopping lists" never wraps on a phone. `startList` raises the keyboard inside the tap and opens `/lists/new`; the draft sets no top-bar actions, and the New sheet's "Start one yourself" only closes there (`onDraft`). From the New sheet the sheet closes first, synchronously and without handing focus back to "+": Bits UI's focus trap pulls a stand-in outside the sheet back, and iOS drops the keyboard with any focus that is not a field. `e2e/shopping-lists.test.ts` starts lists and imports through the top bar on a desktop and through the "+" on a phone (`startFromLists`).

**A new list starts as its own page, and only "Create" makes it.** "New list" goes to `/lists/new`: the list page's layout with the name as a field where the title stands, and the rest shown dimmed and `inert`. Enter or "Create" makes the list and replaces the draft with its page (`replaceState`, so back goes to the overview); close, back, Escape or leaving the app makes nothing, with no question asked. Arriving with `page.state.created`, the list page fades in what the draft lacked and `AddItemCard`'s `focus` puts the caret into "What do you need?". `e2e/shopping-lists.test.ts` cancels a named draft and finds no list, then creates one with Enter and finds the caret in the add field.

**The keyboard is handed across the navigation.** Both the draft's name field and the add field are focused after a navigation, so "New list" and "Create" call `holdKeyboard()` and the next field takes it with `handKeyboardTo` ([User activation](/conventions/svelte#user-activation)); check it on an iPhone.

### Shopping mode

**Shopping mode is its own bare route.** `/lists/[id]/shop` has no app bars, like cook mode, and is reached from the list's "Shop" button (`shopHref`), whose counts are a description through `aria-describedby` so its name stays "Shop". It holds the screen awake with `ScreenWakeLock` the way the cook page does and shows the caption only while the lock is real. Its head shows under whose account the ticks go, since the page has no top bar: the viewer's `PersonMark`, `ringed` like the top bar's own avatar ("Checking off as …"), and on a shared list a `FaceStack` of everyone else. Items stay neutral; nothing in a row takes a person's color.

**Shopping mode is for ticking only.** Each recipe's amount sits on its own line (`ShoppingRow` with `shop`), the whole row ticks on a tap, done rows collapse under a "done" row with its count, the recipe view hides recipe groups whose rows are all ticked and has no entry menu (both pages draw the groups with `ListGroups`, this one with `shop`), and there is no add field (owner's call: the list is kept before, the store is for ticking).

### The list page

**The list page groups on the client.** `frontend/src/lib/shopping/rows.ts` builds the views from the list the server sent:

- **By department**, items with the same `nameKey` form one row with one circle; each source - one per recipe, or per person who typed it in, and unit - shows its amount and recipe on a line of its own. A ticked row moves to "Done"; ticking a row ticks every item behind it, and a row shows ticked only when all its items are. A row lingers (`Row.lingering`) only when all its items are ticked, so a half-ticked merged row never shows ticked.
- **A merged row's total** adds up amounts per unit (`rowsOf`, `sumByUnit`, `unitKey`): weights and volumes across units through `frontend/src/lib/recipe/measures.ts`, read in g or kg and ml or l as the sum suits, pieces rounded up since nobody buys a quarter egg, other units only when spelled alike ignoring case. An open row adds up only its open items; an item without an amount never joins one with ("Eier" and "2 Eier" stay two lines) and leaves the row without a total, so it cannot hide in a sum. Adding up is the client's: the server keeps every item as it came. `measures.ts` knows metric units only and is the table a later unit conversion of recipes builds on.
- **By recipe**, one block per entry with its servings and a menu (open recipe, change servings, remove), then "Without a recipe" for free items; each item shows its own tick.
- Counts are **rows** everywhere: the server's `openCount`/`doneCount` on a list and on every overview card count name keys, a row open while any item behind it is unticked and done once all are (`ListShoppingLists` in SQL, `get` in Go, `TestCountsAreRowsOfMergedNames`). The list header computes the same from `rows.ts`, and the e2e spec checks that card and header agree.
- The list's menu renames it, copies it as text ("Copy as text": the open rows in aisle order, one per line, amounts first, then the name and the open items' notes after a comma, e.g. `1 Bund Petersilie, glatt` - `listText`, the shape the recipe stub copies and the list import reads back; a row mixing items with and without an amount keeps the amounts it has) and deletes it.
- The side panel on a wide screen lists the recipes on the list and "Shared with" (the creator, then the members); every overview card says who shares it and when the list last changed (`relativeTime`).
- On a phone every target in a row is 44px tall without moving anything, as the comment in `ShoppingRow.svelte` describes; a row is an `@container`, so at 200% text a done row's ticker shows the face only and keeps the name in its accessible label.
- The chosen view is remembered per device (`rezepte:list-view`); the add dialog remembers the last list it added to (`rezepte:last-list`).

**The list page drops answers that arrive out of order.** Two quick taps send two requests whose answers may come back in either order. Every change takes the next revision, and only the newest change's answer replaces the list; an older answer is dropped, and so is an answer for another list, since the page stays mounted when one list links to the next. A failed change undoes its own optimistic step only while nothing newer was sent, and reloads otherwise. Ticks and department moves are optimistic.

### The add dialog

**The add dialog starts from the recipe page.** Only the signed-in recipe page offers the basket (`addable` on `RecipeView` and `IngredientList`); the public share page keeps the copy button but has no basket, since its recipe id is client-only and an anonymous reader has no lists. `AddToListDialog` opens at the reader's current servings with every ingredient picked except the ones ticked off on the recipe page; it can create a list inline, and a failed add after a created list retries on that list rather than making a second one. It says when the recipe is on the picked list already (`add_to_list_already`, from `recipeId` on each summary's recipes), since adding it again adds its amounts to the same lines. The toast after adding links to the list.

**Navigation and people.** Shopping lists are a top-bar link on a computer and a bottom-nav slot on a phone ([Design system](/architecture/design-system)). The people page shows each person's number of lists they started, from `CountsByCreator`, never their content ([Users and authentication](/features/users-and-auth)).

## Why it is this way

**Copy on add, because a recipe save rewrites its ingredient rows.** `recipe.Service.Update` deletes and re-inserts ingredients on every save, so an item pointing at an ingredient row would break on the next edit; and a list someone is shopping from must not change under them because an author fixed a typo. To pick up a correction one adds the recipe again.

**Scaling lives in Go too, because more than the browser adds at servings.** Changing servings on the list, and adding through the API, both need scaled amounts the server computes; the stored quantity stays unscaled so that a servings change is exact rather than a rescale of a rounded number.

**404 rather than 403**, because a 403 confirms the id exists and belongs to someone.

**An invitation, not an add**, because a list holds what a person shops for and a stranger's list in your overview is noise until you want it; accepting is the consent, declining costs the inviter nothing and can be retried.

**Admins stay out.** Access is the predicate above and nothing else: an admin sees a list only as its creator or member, so the privacy sentence in the user guide holds without exceptions.

**Overrides are instance-wide**, because one person teaching Rezepte where capers go helps everyone on the instance; a per-person or per-list override would have to be taught again by each.

**The word list is a short table, not a dictionary library.** A stemmer or lemma library is a dependency against "standard library first", and the ingredients that matter are a few hundred words.

## Rejected

- **Items referencing ingredient rows** - see the copy rule above.
- **A dictionary or stemming library for name keys** - see the word list above.
- **A dialog or bottom sheet for a new list's name.** A sheet's field sat under the iOS keyboard or far above it on Android, as the step editor found too ([Ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references)); a field at the top of a page is never where a keyboard comes up.

## Limits

- Deleting an account publishes nothing on the stream: a list it owned stays on a member's open page until the next change or reconnect brings the 404, and a list it was a member of shows it in "Shared with" until the next refetch.
- Irregular plurals beyond `irregularPlurals` stay separate keys, and a changed `NameKey` does not rewrite stored keys.
- An override is per name key, not per list or person, and nothing lists or resets overrides except moving the item again.
- Text reads numbers with every language's rules at once, so a stray English "1.250 kg" is 1250 kg by the German thousands rule; "Copy as text" never writes three decimals.
- Free-text parsing is naive: "Salz, Pfeffer" becomes name "Salz" plus note "Pfeffer", and a bare "pepper" is pantry rather than spice.
