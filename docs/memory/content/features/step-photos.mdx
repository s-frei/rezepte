---
title: Step photos
description: A phrase in a step's text that opens one of the recipe's own photos, linked in the editor, previewed at the phrase
---

A word or phrase of a preparation step can point at one of the recipe's photos: "vom Brett ins kochende Salzwasser schaben" opens the photo that shows the scraping. The photo stays in the gallery; the step only links to it. The editor side shares the machinery of [ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references) and [step times](/features/step-times), which own the details this page only points at.

<Callout type="warn" title="Traps">

- A link names the image id, never its position: positions are compacted on every delete and reorder.
- A save can name a photo another member just deleted; `dropDeletedPhotos` skips it, while a photo of another recipe stays a 422 ([Wire format](#wire-format-and-validation)).
- The trigger is a `span role="button"` named by `aria-labelledby`: a `<button>` cannot wrap across lines, and an `aria-label` would hide the quantities inside ([Reading](#reading)).
- A new recipe's links go out in a second write after the uploads; throwing on its failure created the recipe twice on the next Save ([Linking](#linking-in-the-editor)).
- A step without links writes no `photos` key, or an older Rezepte refuses the export ([Elsewhere](#elsewhere)).

</Callout>

## How it works

### Wire format and validation

**The wire format sits beside `references` and `times`.** `Step.Photos` is `[]StepPhoto`, on the wire `photos: [{phrase, imageId}]`, at most 20 per step. `phrase` (1 to 120 characters, the limit "Link word" already has) is the anchor; `imageId` is the image's id, never its position. A step that leaves `photos` out has none, like `references` and `times` ([HTTP API](/architecture/api)). `step_photos` (migration `0028_step_photos.sql`) is written with the steps in `writeChildren` and read in `load`, so a save rewrites it like every other child row.

**Validation.** `ValidatePhotos` in `service/internal/recipe/photos.go` is the one place the rules live; the editor's `photoBlocker` and `photoErrors` mirror the overlap rule to say it under the step in the reader's language. A phrase is trimmed, stands in the text on whole-word boundaries (`wordIndex`), and overlaps neither a time nor another photo phrase, first occurrences compared - which also refuses the same phrase twice, as twins share their first occurrence. A referenced ingredient word inside the phrase is fine. The image must be one of the recipe's: Create validates against none, so a link on create is a 422 that says a new recipe has no photos yet and to link after the upload; Update validates inside the transaction against the recipe's image ids. Errors are `RefError{List: "photos"}`, a 422 at `body.steps[i].photos[j].phrase|imageId`. The archive check calls the same function with file names.

**Deleting a photo deletes its links** through `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `image_id`; the phrase stays as text. Two household members can edit one recipe, so a save can still name a photo the other deleted: Update first skips every link whose image no longer exists at all (`dropDeletedPhotos`, `ImageExists`) and the save lands; an image that exists but belongs to another recipe stays a 422. Reordering changes nothing, because the link names the id and the reader derives "Photo 3 of 9" from the current position.

### Reading

**Reading.** `segmentStep` cuts its segments at photo phrases and marks every segment inside one with `photo`, so a referenced ingredient inside keeps its quantity; a reference the phrase cuts in two gives its quantity to its last part, so the amount never sits between its halves inside the trigger. `StepText` turns a run of segments with one photo into one trigger when given `onphoto`: semibold, a dotted muted line (not `primary`, so it never reads as a quantity) and an image icon kept with the phrase's first letter only - a whole first word kept unbroken cannot hyphenate and ran out of cook mode's column at its largest size. It is named by a hidden "Show photo:" plus its own content (`aria-labelledby`), so the quantities inside are read; an `aria-label` would replace them. Like a native button it opens on Enter's keydown and Space's keyup: on Space's keydown focus moved into the card and the keyup pressed "View large". The line is the `.photo-link` class in `app.css` (also the editor's marking), a dot background on the phrase's own box, because a `text-decoration` restarts its dot phase in every text run and two dots met at each icon/word seam; its hover color follows the [hover rule](/architecture/design-system#interaction-rules). Without `onphoto` (the shared picture card) and in print the phrase is plain text.

**The preview.** `StepPhotoPreview.svelte` is a Bits UI `Popover` at the phrase on a desktop (`role="dialog"` with a label of its own, which Bits does not set; focus returns to the phrase on close) and a `BottomSheet` on a phone (`ui/phone.svelte.ts`) or wherever the caller passes `sheet`. Both end in `StepPhotoRow` ("Photo n of m" and "View large" into the page's `Lightbox`), as does cook mode's panel. The card is the app's quiet popover surface, never an orange frame. `RecipeView` mounts the preview for the detail and public share pages; cook mode mounts its own.

**Cook mode on a large screen puts the photo beside the step** (`sideWidth` in the cook route says when it fits: a window of 64rem and up that is not a phone (`phone`), a photo large enough to see, and the step's text keeping about 28 characters of its own type size beside it; the largest type size, portrait tablets and narrow windows fall back to the sheet). The panel lives inside the step's `section`, so it splits the stage and can never cover the footer or the ingredient bar. It is a non-modal dialog (`data-step-photo-panel`); a second press on the phrase, ×, Escape or any page turn closes it, and so do the reading view and a timer's jump to another recipe, which reuse the page without a page turn. A resize or a type size change measures again and closes a panel that no longer fits, handing focus back to the phrase only when it was inside the panel. Everywhere else cook mode passes `sheet`. The sheet caps its photo to about a third of the height, or the step at a large type size (and on a phone held sideways, the whole step) vanished under it; and its column keeps a minimum width so a short window narrows the photo and not the caption ([Sheets](/architecture/design-system#sheets)).

**The step stays in sight above the sheet.** The sheet spotlights the step (cook mode's scroll box, else the step's list item) and reveals the phrase's last line above itself ([Sheets](/architecture/design-system#sheets)): it scrolls the window on the detail and share pages, or cook mode's step box, padded by what the sheet covers so the centered step re-centers above it. The detail page's phone top bar fills in only once scrolled, and the reveal's own scroll may fill it, so it is always `data-covers-top="4"`, its filled height. Cook mode's "this step is long" latch, which hides the recipe title, ignores measures taken while the sheet pads the step or the panel narrows it; otherwise the title stayed gone for that step after the photo closed. `step-photos-read.test.ts` holds the reading side's checks but the public link's, which lives in `public-shares.test.ts` beside the other tests that flip public sharing, the sheet geometry on the mobile project (`expectSpotlight` in `e2e/helpers.ts`).

### Linking in the editor

**Linking.** "Link photo" sits beside "Link word" and "Mark as time" while the recipe has a photo. It takes the selection or the word at the caret like "Link word", keeps `fitsWordLimit`, and refuses a phrase across a line break (`editor_photo_one_line`: each line is a paragraph of its own, which the marking cannot cross) or one overlapping a stored time or another visible photo link (`photoBlocker`); an ingredient word inside it is fine. The phrase it will link is framed in the step as a sheet's word is (a decoration, never a document change, so undo is untouched); on a phone the native selection is dropped, since its handles and menu sat over the sheet. Below `md` the three actions show one word each, the full label staying the button's name, since the German labels took two lines on a phone.

**The picker.** `StepPhotoPicker.svelte` is a `BottomSheet` on a phone and the editor's popup elsewhere: a large `detail` preview that pages on a swipe, a strip of thumbnails, and "Link photo n". It opens on the linked photo, else the first, and holds the choice by tile key, so a photo restored, reloaded or moved while it is open leaves the choice where it was.

- **The phone sheet keeps the phrase in sight.** It leaves the step out of its dim and scrolls the phrase clear of itself: `revealPhrase` measures the `data-photo-picking` mark and shares `revealAbove` with the word sheets ([Sheets](/architecture/design-system#sheets)). Its second run after the keyboard closes does nothing once the picker has closed, or its padding outlived the sheet.
- **The button sits in a footer that never scrolls,** and the large photo gives way in the body while the header and the strip keep their height: with the button as the scroll body's last row, a long phrase on a short screen pushed it to the bottom edge. `step-photos-edit.test.ts` measures it on a short phone on the mobile project.

**The editor keeps photo links by tile key.** `FormStep.photos` holds `{ phrase, imageKey }`, a third attribute of the step document beside `references` and `times` (`STEP_WORDS`), whose machinery and undo [Ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references) owns; a pick is one `closeHistory` transaction like a card's accept. The key is the `ImagesSection` tile's `key` - the image id for a stored photo, a client id for a queued or uploading one, unchanged when the upload lands - so a photo can be linked before it has an id, and `toInput(form, photoIds)` resolves it, leaving out a link whose key the map lacks.

**A hidden photo hides its links, and the form never changes.** The section exposes the tiles a step may link to as a bindable `visible` (stored, queued, uploading; not a removal waiting for its undo toast, not a failed upload), and everything that reads the links - the field's marking, the list under the step, the blocker - skips links whose key is not among them (`visiblePhotos` in `step-photos.ts`). Because the form itself never changes when a photo goes, undoing the removal brings the links back, with no coupling between the document's history and the toast. The marking is the `.photo-link` class, drawn on its own so a linked ingredient word inside the phrase keeps its own mark.

**The save resolves the keys after the section's `settle()`** (`RecipeForm`), which waits for the uploads and leaves pending removals to their undo toast ([Images](/features/images#the-editors-photos)). `photoIds` in `step-photos.ts` maps a stored tile's key to its id (for a new recipe, a queued tile's key to itself), so a removed photo's links go nowhere; the full `flush()` sends the removals only once the save went through, so a refused save leaves a removal undoable. `flush()` returns the keys of the photos whose delete then failed; a link to one was already left out of the save, so a toast says so (`editor_photo_link_dropped`). `toInput` leaves `photos` off a step without links, which the server reads as none (`TestStepWithoutPhotosDropsItsLinks`).

**`photoErrors` stops the save on what "Link photo" cannot prevent.** It runs once, after the wait, in `form.ts`, and refuses under the step, in the reader's language:

- a sent link overlapping a sent time or another sent link (`photoBlocker` on what will be sent) - a link or a time made while a photo was hidden and brought back by undo, or an edit that moved a time's first occurrence into a photo phrase;
- a link to a photo whose upload failed (`failedKeys()`) or that has no id yet (a retry started during the wait; sent as its key, the server would skip it as a deleted image), with `editor_photo_upload_failed`, so the author retries or removes it instead of losing the link unseen.

**A new recipe links its photos in a second write.** Its photos are only queued, so their tiles keep their key as the id: `routes/recipes/new` creates the recipe without links, uploads the queued files (`QueuedPhoto`, file plus tile key) recording key to id, and sends one update with the links resolved (`resolvePhotos`, which `toInput` uses too) when any step links an uploaded one. A failed update, or a linked photo whose upload failed, says so in a toast (`editor_photo_links_failed`) and opens the new recipe's editor (`RecipeForm`'s `savedHref`) instead of its page, so the author links again there; throwing instead kept the author on the new-recipe form, and the next Save created the recipe and its photos a second time. The e2e file is `step-photos-edit.test.ts`.

### Elsewhere

**Elsewhere.** The field follows the [checklist](/features/recipes#adding-a-field-to-a-recipe-or-a-step); what is particular here: the public share carries the links with the same ids its `images` list uses, and a zip names the photo by its archive file (`"imageId": "3.jpg"`), import setting the links after the uploads ([Import and export](/features/import-export)), and a step without links writes no `photos` key, so an export without links stays readable by a Rezepte from before step photos, whose `Step` schema refuses an unknown key. The demo links Käsespätzle step 2 (de) and Sausage Rolls step 4 (en) to their third photo.

## Why it is this way

Image ids are stable and positions are not: `position` is compacted on every delete and reorder, so a positional link would drift onto a neighbor silently. The anchor is the phrase for the same reason references and times use their words: steps are rewritten on every save.

The trigger is a `span role="button"`, not a `<button>`: a button is laid out as an inline block in Chromium and cannot wrap across lines, so a phrase of several words turned into a block that broke the sentence, at its worst in cook mode's display type.

A photo phrase may not hold a time - the editor neither proposes nor lets "Mark as time" mark one inside it ([Step times](/features/step-times)) - because in cook mode a time is a button that starts a timer, and a button inside the photo trigger is an interactive element inside another.

## Rejected

- **A thumbnail at the step's margin** and **the photo under the step**: the first detached the photo from the words it explains, the second doubled the method's length on a phone.
- **The lightbox straight from the phrase**: it hides the step the photo explains; the preview keeps the step in view, the lightbox is one tap further.
- **A carousel or a grid in the picker**: a carousel takes long swipes at 20 photos; a grid of square crops makes similar process shots hard to tell apart. The large uncropped preview with a strip does both.
- **Photos as a row in the "Link word" picker**: the list grew long and the photos landed below the fold on a phone.
- **Suggestions**: nothing in a sentence says which photo shows it.
- **An orange-bordered card**: it read dated and claimed the accent the quantities own; the plain popover surface keeps the photo the subject.
- **The card in cook mode**: anchored at the phrase, it slid in over "Next" and the ingredient bar on a short desktop window.
- **The sheet on every desktop**: a sheet rising over a wide screen covers the step to show a photo that has room beside it.

## Limits

- One photo per phrase, 20 photo links per step, phrase up to 120 characters.
- Archives exported before photo links existed import without them; an archive with links does not import into a Rezepte from before them (unexpected property).
- Cook mode on a phone held sideways (360px tall) leaves no room above the sheet: its stage starts below the header at about the sheet's top, so the sheet covers the step.
