title: Step photos
description: A phrase in a step's text that opens one of the recipe's own photos, linked in the editor, previewed at the phrase
---

A word or phrase of a preparation step can point at one of the recipe's photos: "vom Brett ins kochende Salzwasser schaben" opens the photo that shows the scraping. The photo stays in the gallery; the step only links to it. The editor side shares the machinery of [ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references) and [step times](/features/step-times), which own the details this page only points at.

## How it works

**The wire format sits beside `references` and `times`.** `Step.Photos` is `[]StepPhoto`, on the wire `photos: [{phrase, imageId}]`, at most 20 per step. `phrase` (1 to 120 characters, the limit "Link word" already has) is the anchor; `imageId` is the image's id, never its position. A step that leaves `photos` out (or sends `null`) has none: a `PUT` rewrites the step, so a REST client built before photo links that sends back a step without the field silently clears its links, as with `references` and `times`; the field's `doc` in the API reference says so. MCP requires the field for that reason. `step_photos` (migration `0028_step_photos.sql`) is written with the steps in `writeChildren` and read in `load`, so a save rewrites it like every other child row.

**Validation.** `ValidatePhotos` in `service/internal/recipe/photos.go` is the one place the rules live; the editor's `photoBlocker` and `photoErrors` mirror the overlap rule to say it under the step in the reader's language. A phrase is trimmed, stands in the text on whole-word boundaries (`wordIndex`), and overlaps neither a time nor another photo phrase, first occurrences compared - which also refuses the same phrase twice, as twins share their first occurrence. A referenced ingredient word inside the phrase is fine. The image must be one of the recipe's: Create validates against none, so a link on create is a 422 that says a new recipe has no photos yet and to link after the upload; Update validates inside the transaction against the recipe's image ids. Errors are `RefError{List: "photos"}`, a 422 at `body.steps[i].photos[j].phrase|imageId`. The archive check calls the same function with file names.

**Deleting a photo deletes its links** through `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `image_id`; the phrase stays as text. Two household members can edit one recipe, so a save can still name a photo the other deleted: Update first skips every link whose image no longer exists at all (`dropDeletedPhotos`, `ImageExists`) and the save lands; an image that exists but belongs to another recipe stays a 422. Reordering changes nothing, because the link names the id and the reader derives "Photo 3 of 9" from the current position.

**Reading.** `segmentStep` cuts its segments at photo phrases and marks every segment inside one with `photo`, so a referenced ingredient inside keeps its quantity; a reference the phrase cuts in two gives its quantity to its last part, so the amount never sits between its halves inside the trigger. `StepText` turns a run of segments with one photo into one trigger when given `onphoto`: semibold, a dotted muted line (not `primary`, so it never reads as a quantity) and an image icon kept with the phrase's first letter only - a whole first word kept unbroken cannot hyphenate and ran out of cook mode's column at its largest size. It is named by a hidden "Show photo:" plus its own content (`aria-labelledby`), so the quantities inside are read; an `aria-label` would replace them. Like a native button it opens on Enter's keydown and Space's keyup: on Space's keydown focus moved into the card and the keyup pressed "View large". The line is the `.photo-link` class in `app.css` (also the editor's marking), a dot background on the phrase's own box, because a `text-decoration` restarts its dot phase in every text run and two dots met at each icon/word seam; its hover color applies only under `@media (hover: hover)`, as a tap leaves `:hover` stuck on a touch screen. Without `onphoto` (the shared picture card) and in print the phrase is plain text.

**The preview.** `StepPhotoPreview.svelte` is a Bits UI `Popover` at the phrase on a desktop (`role="dialog"` with a label of its own, which Bits does not set; focus returns to the phrase on close) and a `BottomSheet` on a phone (`ui/phone.svelte.ts`) or wherever the caller passes `sheet`. Both end in `StepPhotoRow` ("Photo n of m" and "View large" into the page's `Lightbox`), as does cook mode's panel. The card is the app's quiet popover surface, never an orange frame. `RecipeView` mounts the preview for the detail and public share pages; cook mode mounts its own.

**Cook mode on a large screen puts the photo beside the step** (`sideWidth` in the cook route says when it fits: a window of 64rem and up that is not a phone (`phone`), a photo large enough to see, and the step's text keeping about 28 characters of its own type size beside it; the largest type size, portrait tablets and narrow windows fall back to the sheet). The panel lives inside the step's `section`, so it splits the stage and can never cover the footer or the ingredient bar. It is a non-modal dialog (`data-step-photo-panel`); a second press on the phrase, ×, Escape or any page turn closes it, and so do the reading view and a timer's jump to another recipe, which reuse the page without a page turn. A resize or a type size change measures again and closes a panel that no longer fits, handing focus back to the phrase only when it was inside the panel. Everywhere else cook mode passes `sheet`. The sheet caps its photo to about a third of the height, or the step at a large type size (and on a phone held sideways, the whole step) vanished under it; its column keeps a minimum width so a short window narrows the photo and not the caption; and its body is `min-h-0 flex-1` like every sheet's, or its bottom padding ran off a short screen.

**The step stays in sight above the sheet.** The sheet spotlights the step (cook mode's scroll box, else the step's list item; [Ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references) has the hole) and scrolls the phrase's last line clear of its resting top (`BottomSheet`'s `revealAbove`, shared with the editor): the window on the detail and share pages, padded at its end when the page is too short, or cook mode's step box, padded by what the sheet covers so the centered step re-centers above it. It never scrolls the phrase's first line under a top bar (`coveredTop`). The detail page's phone top bar fills in only once scrolled, and the reveal's own scroll may fill it, so it is always `data-covers-top="4"`: a value caps a bar's cover at that many rem, its filled height. Cook mode's "this step is long" latch, which hides the recipe title, ignores measures taken while the sheet pads the step or the panel narrows it; otherwise the title stayed gone for that step after the photo closed. `step-photos-read.test.ts` holds the reading side's checks, the sheet geometry on the mobile project (`expectSpotlight` in `e2e/helpers.ts`).

**Linking.** "Link photo" sits beside "Link word" and "Mark as time" while the recipe has a photo; `StepPhotoPicker.svelte` (large preview that pages on a swipe, thumbnail strip, "Link photo n") is a sheet that spotlights the step on a phone and the editor's popup elsewhere. On the phone the button sits in a footer that never scrolls, with the sheets' `pb-8` below it, and the body is a column the large photo gives way in (its 4:3 box `min-h-0`, the photo inside it capped by `max-h-full max-w-full`, centered and rounded itself, so a shrunk photo shows no band beside it) while the header, clamped to two lines (the sheet's title keeps the phrase whole), and the strip keep their height; with the button as the scroll body's last row, a long phrase on a short screen pushed it to the bottom edge. `step-photos-edit.test.ts` measures it at 360×560 on the mobile project. The phrase it will link is framed in the step as a sheet's word is (a decoration, never a document change, so undo is untouched); on a phone the native selection is dropped, since its handles and menu sat over the sheet. The form holds `{phrase, imageKey}`, the `ImagesSection` tile's key, so a queued or uploading photo can be linked before it has an id. [Ingredient references](/features/ingredient-references) owns the document attribute, undo, blockers, the picker and how a save resolves keys, stops on a failed upload and links a new recipe's photos after its create.

**Elsewhere.** MCP's `requireStepLists` requires `photos` on every step, so a model rewriting a step sends them back; the public share carries them with the same ids its `images` list uses; a zip names the photo by its archive file (`"image": "3.jpg"`) and import sets the links after the uploads ([Import and export](/features/import-export)); a step without links writes no `photos` key, so an export without links stays readable by a Rezepte from before step photos, whose `Step` schema refuses an unknown key; `archiveStep` is written by hand, and `TestArchiveStepCarriesEveryStepField` fails when `recipe.Step` gains a field it does not carry. The demo links Käsespätzle step 2 (de) and Sausage Rolls step 4 (en) to their third photo.

## Why it is this way

Image ids are stable and positions are not: `position` is compacted on every delete and reorder, so a positional link would drift onto a neighbor silently. The anchor is the phrase for the same reason references and times use their words: steps are rewritten on every save.

The trigger is a `span role="button"`, not a `<button>`: a button is laid out as an inline block in Chromium and cannot wrap across lines, so a phrase of several words turned into a block that broke the sentence, at its worst in cook mode's display type.

A photo phrase may not hold a time - the editor neither proposes nor lets "Mark as time" mark one inside it ([Step times](/features/step-times)) - because in cook mode a time is a button that starts a timer, and a button inside the photo trigger is an interactive element inside another.

## Rejected

- **A thumbnail at the step's margin** and **the photo under the step**: the first detached the photo from the words it explains, the second doubled the method's length on a phone.
- **The lightbox straight from the phrase**: it hides the step the photo explains; the preview keeps the step in view, the lightbox is one tap further.
- **A carousel or a grid in the picker**: a carousel takes long swipes at 20 photos; a grid of square crops makes similar process shots hard to tell apart. The large uncropped preview with a strip does both.
- **Photos as a row in the "Link word" picker**: the list grew long and the photos landed below the fold on a phone.
- **Suggestions**: nothing in a sentence says which photo shows it.
- **An orange-bordered card**: it read dated and claimed the accent the quantities own; the plain popover surface keeps the photo the subject.
- **The card in cook mode**: anchored at the phrase, it slid in over "Next" and the ingredient bar on a short desktop window.
- **The sheet on every desktop**: a sheet rising over a wide screen covers the step to show a photo that has room beside it.

## Limits

- One photo per phrase, 20 photo links per step, phrase up to 120 characters.
- Archives exported before photo links existed import without them; an archive with links does not import into a Rezepte from before them (unexpected property).
- Cook mode on a phone held sideways (360px tall) leaves no room above the sheet: its stage starts below the header at about the sheet's top, so the sheet covers the step.
