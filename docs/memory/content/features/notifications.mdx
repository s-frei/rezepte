---
title: Notifications
description: The notifications people can switch off per channel (mail, push) and group - a new comment, the daily new recipes digest, a tasty mark, an accepted invitation, mail failing for the owner - their preferences, the signed unsubscribe link and the two pages
---

Rezepte mails and pushes four things a person may not want: a new comment on a recipe they follow, a daily digest of the recipes others added, a tasty mark on their recipe and, to admins and the owner, an invitation of theirs that was accepted. A fifth goes to the owner by push alone: mail is not getting out. Each can be switched off per channel in Settings → Notifications, and for mail from the link in the mail. The sender, the templates and the SMTP setup belong to [Email](/features/email); the fan-out, the preferences and the unsubscribe live in `service/internal/notify`. Password and address notices are security notices, not notifications, and nothing here reaches them.

## How it works

**Notification preferences.** `service/internal/notify` owns the switchable notifications. A `Kind` (`comment`, `recipe` and `tasty` in `household`, `invite_accepted` and `mail_failing` in `admin`, in the order the settings show them) belongs to a `Group` and goes out on a `Channel`, `mail` or `push`; the three values are stored and mailed, so they never change. `KindsFor(role, channel)` gives the admin group only to admins and the owner, and `mail_failing` only to the owner and only on `push` (a mail cannot warn that mail fails); an unknown channel has no kinds. Every kind is on for both channels until the account opts out, at two levels: `notification_optouts` holds a row per (kind, channel) switched off, `notification_group_optouts` a row per (group, channel) whose master switch is off. A kind goes out on a channel only when neither row exists, so a group switched off also silences a kind added later (`TestGroupOffSilencesLaterKinds`), and switching the group on again brings back each kind's own choice, which the group row never touched (`TestGroupMasterKeepsKindChoices`). `Preferences` answers a `Prefs`: `Kinds[kind][channel]`, the kind's own choice whatever its group says, and `Groups[group][channel]`, for every pair the account may have (a group is present on a channel where the account has a kind of it). `SetPreferences` takes a partial `Prefs` in one transaction and answers `ErrUnknownKind` (`422`) for any pair the account may not have, and then changes nothing (`TestSetPreferencesAllOrNothing`); `Wants(userID, kind, channel)` is the check before a send and reads both rows in one query (`HasOptOut`); the senders call it through `wanted`, which logs a failed lookup and sends nothing. The session's own switches are `GET`/`PUT /api/v1/auth/me/notifications`, session only, with `{"kinds": {"comment": {"mail": true, "push": false}, …}, "groups": {"household": {"mail": true, "push": true}, …}}`; `PUT` takes the same shape, both maps optional. Migration 0021 gives every opt-out from before channels `channel = 'mail'`, and turns an account's rows for every kind a group had then (what a group switch that wrote each kind left behind: `comment` and `recipe`, or `invite_accepted`) into that group's mail row; push starts with no rows.

**Notifications are decided in `notify`, sent by `mail` and `push`.** `notify.Mailer` is `Enabled`, `SendComment`, `SendRecipeDigest`, `SendTasty` and `SendInviteAccepted`; `mail.Comment`, `mail.RecipeDigest`, `mail.Tasty` and `mail.InviteAccepted` carry the recipient, the copy's facts, the locale and the `Unsubscribe` links. Each goes only to a confirmed address of an account that did not switch the kind off for mail. The code is `service/internal/notify/fanout.go`, `digest.go` and `tasty.go`.

- **New comment.** `recipe.Service.AddComment` calls its `CommentNotifier` (`SetCommentNotifier`, nil is off) after the insert committed, so the REST handler and MCP `add_comment` both mail; the demo seed writes comments through the query and mails nothing. `notify.Service.CommentAdded` returns at once and runs `mailComment`, then `pushComment`, in one goroutine (`detach`: `context.WithoutCancel`, a 10 minute bound, `detachedTimeout`, and a `recover` that logs, the `forgot-password` pattern); mail off or a failed lookup for the mails does not stop the pushes. Each follower's send has a 15 second timeout of its own (`sendTimeout`), so a server that stalls for one follower costs that follower, not every one after them (`TestCommentMailStallCostsOneFollower`). `ListCommentRecipients(recipe, author, channel)` picks the followers with neither `comment` nor the household group switched off on that channel, and `mailComment` asks it for `mail` and keeps only confirmed addresses (`mailable`; a push needs no address, `TestCommentRecipientsPushNeedNoAddress`). The followers are, as the unread dot defines them: the recipe's `created_by` and everyone with a comment on it, never the author. One mail per follower and comment, in the follower's locale, saying whether the recipe is theirs or they commented on it ("is yours", not "you created": `created_by` may be an admin who inherited the recipes of an account they deleted); its button opens `/recipes/<slug>?tab=comments`. The comment's photos ride along inline, loaded once per comment and not per follower ([Email](/features/email)). The author's circle shows their picture when they have one: `picture` reads the file through `avatar.Service.Open` and `mail.Comment.AuthorAvatar` carries it into the mail (see [Email](/features/email) for the inline part); an unreadable file is logged and the mail falls back to the initial. Without a picture the circle inlines the light `--color-user-*` pair from `app.css` (`userColors` in `mail/notifications.go`), since a mail cannot read the stylesheet. Mail off stops the loop quietly; a refused send is logged by `deliver`, any other failure by `mail.LogUnsent` (user ID, never the address; also for the invitation mail and the security notices), and the next follower still gets theirs.
- **New recipes digest.** Once a day the household hears of the recipes others added, as one mail (see [Email](/features/email) for its layout). A recipe is queued by `recipes.announce_pending`, which only `recipe.Service.CreateAnnounced` sets, in the create's own insert: the HTTP create (the form and URL/text import) and MCP `create_recipe` call it, archive import and the demo seed call `Create` and never queue (`TestDigestIgnoresUnannounced`; see [Recipes](/features/recipes)). `notify.Service.StartRecipeDigest` (started in `main.go` beside the sweep loops) ticks once a minute (`digestTick`) and calls `RunRecipeDigest(ctx, time.Now())`, logging an error and recovering a panic per tick; the channel it returns closes once it has stopped. With mail off it runs too and only pushes (`TestDigestPushWithMailOff`), so on an instance without mail the day's recipes are claimed whether or not anyone has a device; it does nothing before the owner's hour (`instance_settings.recipe_digest_hour`, default 17, in `time.Local` from `TZ`, set on Settings → Email) or when `recipe_digest_last_day` already holds today's local date. Otherwise one transaction claims the day (`ClaimRecipeDigestDay` must change one row, so two ticks or a restart cannot run twice), takes every queued recipe created more than an hour ago (`ListDueAnnouncements`; a younger one stays queued for the next day, a deleted one is gone) and clears their flag. A day missed entirely sends nothing extra; its recipes join the next run. An hour moved earlier after the day's run waits for tomorrow (`TestDigestRunsOncePerDay`). `ListRecipeDigestRecipients(channel)` (neither `recipe` nor the household group switched off on that channel; the run asks for `mail` and keeps only confirmed addresses) is read before the claim and for `push`, so a failure leaves the day unclaimed. After the commit the run detaches from its context (`context.WithoutCancel`), so a shutdown does not cut it short (`TestDigestOutlivesItsContext`): `main.go` waits for that channel after the server stopped, at most `digestShutdownWait` (2 minutes, so a hung SMTP server cannot hold the exit; `TestDigestStopWaitsForTheRun`); the wait for push under way (`pushShutdownWait`, 30 seconds) starts at the same moment, so a stop takes the longer of the two and the shipped stop timeouts only have to cover 10 seconds of open requests plus 2 minutes, and a creator that cannot be read is logged and named a former member rather than ending the run (`TestDigestSurvivesACreatorLookupFailure`). Each recipient gets, with `sendTimeout`, the recipes others created in creation order; whoever is left with none gets no mail (`TestDigestSkipsOwnRecipes`). Mail switched off mid-run ends the mails, not the pushes, which go after them. Each recipe carries its cover read from `_thumb.jpg` through `image.MailPhoto` (unreadable: logged, a card without photo, `TestDigestMissingPhoto`) and its creator's name, color and picture; a creator whose account is gone has an empty name, which the mail shows as a former member.
- **Tasty mark.** `recipe.Service.SetTasty` calls its `TastyNotifier` (`SetTastyNotifier`, nil is off) only when the insert added a row (`SetTasty` is `:execrows`), so marking again, clearing and the author's refused mark tell nobody (`TestTastyRemarkDoesNotRenotify`); REST and MCP `add_tasty` both go through it, the demo seed uses its own `recipe.Service` without a notifier. `notify.Service.TastyMarked` returns at once and arms `time.AfterFunc(tastyDelay)` (a minute, a variable for tests), whose callback runs `sendTasty` under `detach`. A set of pending `<recipeID>:<markerID>` keys (`pendingTasty`) arms one timer per pair, so mark, clear, mark within the minute tells once. When it fires, `tastyFacts` checks that the mark is still there (`ListTastyRecipeIDs`), the recipe and both accounts still exist and `created_by` is not the marker; a mark taken back sends nothing on either channel (`TestTastyTakenBackSendsNothing`). Then one mail to the recipe's `created_by` at a confirmed address unless `tasty` or the household group is off for mail, with the marker's picture or initial and a button to `/recipes/<slug>`, and one push unless they are off for push (`TestTastyRespectsChannels`, `TestTastyNotifiesCreator`). The pending timers live in memory: a restart within the minute drops them (a `ponytail:` mark).
- **Invitation accepted.** `notify.Service.InviteAccepted` is the `auth.Notifier` (`sessions.SetNotifier` in `main.go`): one mail to the admin who issued the setup link, synchronous, naming the newcomer's display name and username, with a button to `/settings/users`, and a push detached from the request. Nothing goes when the inviter is the newcomer, is gone or is no longer admin or owner; the mail also not without a confirmed address or with `invite_accepted` or the admin group off for mail, the push not with either off for push (`Wants(inviter, invite_accepted, push)`).
- **Mail is not getting out.** `notify.Service` is the `mail.DeliveryReporter` (`mailer.SetReporter(notifications)` in `main.go`); the code is `service/internal/notify/mailalert.go`. `MailFailed(reason)` comes for a real send that failed on the mail setup (see [Email](/features/email)) and pushes the owner (`GetSuperadmin`) when `instance_settings.mail_alert_last_at` is empty or 24 hours old or more, then sets it to now. `MailReset`, after every accepted send and after the owner's save or turn-off, empties it (`TestMailAlertReset`). So the first failure since mail last worked, since the settings last changed, or ever (a fresh instance with a wrong password alerts), pushes at once, and a mail that keeps failing pushes once a day (`TestMailAlertOncePerDay`). The state lives in the database, so a restart does not re-arm it (`TestMailAlertSurvivesRestart`). The decision and the state write run inline under one mutex, so concurrent failing sends push once; the owner lookup, `Wants(owner, mail_failing, push)` and the push run detached. The push's copy is in the table below, one body per reason. The time of an alert is recorded even when the owner switched it off, so switching it on again does not alert before the day is up. The service's clock is `Service.now`, which tests move.

**Pushes go beside the mails.** `notify.Pusher` is one method, `SendToUser(ctx, userID, push.Message)`, which `*push.Service` is; `notify.NewService` takes it, so `main.go` builds `pushes` first; tests that do not care pass a no-op pusher. `SendToUser` reaches every device of the account, each with its own timeout, drops a device the push service forgot and logs its failures; notify never retries. A push needs no address and does not care whether mail is on. The copy comes from the mail catalog through `mail.Text` in the recipient's locale (`TestPushCopyGerman`), with `mail_push_*` keys in `frontend/messages/*.json` that `copymessages` carries over; the code is `service/internal/notify/push.go`.

| Kind | Title | Body | URL | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `comment` | `mail_push_comment_title` `New comment on “{title}”` | `mail_push_comment_body` `{author}: {text}` | `/recipes/<slug>?tab=comments` | `comment:<recipeID>` |
| `recipe` | `mail_push_recipe_title` | `mail_push_recipe_body_one` `{title} from {names}`, from two recipes `mail_push_recipe_body_many` `{count} new recipes from {names}` | `/` | `recipe-digest` |
| `tasty` | `mail_push_tasty_title` | `mail_push_tasty_body` `{name} finds your recipe “{title}” tasty` | `/recipes/<slug>` | `tasty:<recipeID>:<markerID>` |
| `invite_accepted` | `mail_push_invite_accepted_title` | `mail_push_invite_accepted_body` `{name} signs in as {username}` | `/settings/users` | `invite:<newcomerID>` |
| `mail_failing` | `mail_push_mail_failing_title` | `mail_push_mail_failing_<reason>`, reason `auth`, `connect`, `tls` or `sender` | `/settings/mail` | `mail-failing` |

A comment push names what happened in its title, as every other kind's does (a bare recipe title left a toast without its reason), and goes to `ListCommentRecipients(recipe, author, push)`, never the author (`TestCommentPushToFollowersNotAuthor`), also to a follower without a confirmed address (`TestCommentPushWithoutAddress`), and its `Collapse` carries `mail_push_comment_title_many` (`New comments on “{title}”`), the same body and `mail_push_comment_body_many` (`{count} new comments`, the `{count}` left for the service worker), so a phone shows one notification per recipe that counts up ([Installable app](/features/installable-app) has the worker). `{text}` is the comment through `shorten(s, 120)`: whitespace runs become one space, and a longer text is cut to 120 runes plus "…", never inside a UTF-8 sequence nor between an emoji and its skin tone, joiner or variation selector (`TestShortenRuneSafe`; the `ponytail:` mark names what it does not cover, a flag's two regional indicators). The digest push goes per `ListRecipeDigestRecipients(push)` recipient with recipes from others, after the mails; `{names}` joins up to three distinct creators with ", ", a creator whose account is gone named by `mail_recipe_digest_former_member` (`TestDigestPush`). The tasty push goes to the recipe's author in their locale, one per marker, so two members' marks are two notifications. The invitation push is in the inviter's locale (`TestInviteAcceptedPush`). The test push and its `mail_push_test_*` copy belong to `push`.

**Unsubscribing needs no session.** `Service.UnsubscribeLinks(userID, kind)` returns the `mail.Unsubscribe` a mail carries: `/unsubscribe?u&k&s` for the page, the same with `&scope=group`, and `/api/v1/unsubscribe/one-click?u&k&s` for `List-Unsubscribe`. `s` is a `sign` token over `userID:kind` under the label `rezepte unsubscribe`, for 90 days, keyed by `instance_settings.link_preview_key` (`settings.Service.LinkPreviewKey`): one instance key, and the label keeps a link preview token from verifying as an unsubscribe token or the other way round. The token is checked with `Signer.Verify` before anything is read; a forged or expired token, or one naming an account that is gone or a kind its role no longer has, answers `410` alike (`httpserver.PublicError`). A link reaches only the mail channel: `KindsFor(role, mail)` decides which kinds have one, so `mail_failing` has none. `GET /api/v1/unsubscribe` changes nothing and answers the username, the address masked by `mail.MaskAddress` (an old link may sit in the mailbox the account left, so it never learns the new address), kind, group, `kindSubscribed` (the kind arrives by mail: its own switch and its group's are on), `groupSubscribed` (the group's mail switch) and `groupKinds`, each mail kind of the group the account may have, true when it arrives. `POST /api/v1/unsubscribe {u, k, s, scope, subscribed}` switches the kind's mail row, or with `scope: group` the group's mail switch alone, and `subscribed: true` undoes exactly that row: the other kinds' own choices and every push choice stay as they were (`TestUnsubscribeGroupSwitchesMaster`). The one-click route is `POST` only, so a scanner following the address with `GET` unsubscribes nobody. It never reads the body: RFC 8058 asks for `List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` as multipart form data, many senders post it urlencoded, and huma would make a declared raw body required, so the signed address alone decides (`TestOneClickOptsOutKindOnly` posts all three, empty included); it switches off that kind's mail only and answers `200` with no body. The token reaches only `u`'s preferences and never signs anybody in. It is not bound to the address it went to: whoever reads that mailbox can switch the account's notification preferences for the link's 90 days, even after the account changed its address; security notices it cannot touch.

**The two pages.** Settings → Notifications (`frontend/src/routes/settings/notifications/`, `NotificationsCard.svelte`) is every member's, in "For you" right after Profile, since the address it writes to lives there. It has two sections, "By mail" and "Push", each a `region` named by its heading. Each opens with a `StatusBox` (round icon in a good, neutral or warn tone, title, hint, a button that goes full width under the text below `md`): by mail the address, confirmed (green `MailCheck`, "Change in profile"), unconfirmed or missing (red, "Confirm in profile" / "Add in profile"), or, with `mailEnabled` off, the "mail isn't set up" hint and, for the owner only, "Set up mail" to Settings → Email; under push "This device" from `deviceState()` in `push.svelte.ts`: enabled (green `BellRing`, "Send test", whose answer is a line under the box, since the app cannot know whether the notification was shown; a `429` asks to wait. The line is the `StatusBox`'s `role="status"` paragraph, rendered empty from the start because screen readers announce a live region's text changing, rarely one inserted with its text, and each send clears it first, so a second success is announced again), off ("Enable on this device", which calls `enableDevice()` straight from the click so the permission prompt is allowed; a decline turns the box to blocked, a failed subscribe toasts), blocked or unsupported (red, a hint, no button). Below come one block per group the channel has a kind of (`KIND_GROUP` in `frontend/src/lib/api/notifications.ts` mirrors the server's grouping; a kind shows on a channel only where `prefs.kinds[kind][channel]` exists, so `mail_failing` appears only for the owner and only under push), headed by the group's `Switch`, each kind a two-segment `SegmentedControl` (Off left and On right, like a switch's track, each with the channel's icon; clicking the chosen side changes nothing) showing the kind's own value. Each switch and toggle is named `{name} · {channel}`, the group or kind and the channel's heading (`settings_notifications_control_label`), since the same kind has one under each channel. A group switched off greys its kinds and passes `disabled` to their controls, which sets `aria-disabled` rather than native `disabled`: the checked segment stays in the tab order, every click and arrow key is dropped, and they keep their choice; a channel without effect (no confirmed address or no mail on the instance; this device not enabled) greys its groups but leaves them operable, since the preferences are the account's and another device or a later confirmation makes them count. Mail ends with the Security block, "always", mail only. A change `PUT`s only its own pair at once and snaps back with a toast when it fails. Only the latest change's answer is applied (a request counter in `change`): with two quick flips the older reply, or the older failure's snap back, would otherwise undo the newer choice. `/unsubscribe` (`frontend/src/routes/unsubscribe/`) is in `authPages`, so it opens bare and without a session. Its `load` only `GET`s, so a scanner opening the link changes nothing; the buttons post the link's kind and its group, the one the mail's link named (`scope=group` or not) first as the primary, and only what is still on is offered: the kind button while the kind arrives by mail, the group button while any kind of the group does. With nothing left the page says "Already unsubscribed" and offers nothing to click. The answer to a click shows "Unsubscribed" with an Undo that posts the same scope with `subscribed: true`; since the group scope writes only the group's row, the undo never turns on a kind the person had off. A `410` (or a `422` for a link missing its parameters) shows "This link no longer works". Every state links to Settings → Notifications; signed out, the root layout's login redirect carries it as `next`. The shell for `/unsubscribe` gets `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `noindex` like `/s/…` (`share.ShellHeaders`), since the token is in its address.

## Why it is this way

- **Opt-outs, not opt-ins.** Notifications are on by default, so only the exceptions are stored and a new kind needs no backfill.
- **A signed link, not a token table.** It needs no row and no sweep; the cost is that a link cannot be revoked before its 90 days, and it can only switch mails.
- **Decided in `notify`, sent by `mail` and `push`.** The rules of who is told stay out of the senders, which know only how to build and deliver a letter or a push.
- **Push copy built on the server from the mail catalog.** The recipient's locale is known where the push is built, and `copymessages` already brings the catalog into the service; the worker only fills `{count}`.

## Rejected

- **A row per kind and account.** It would need a backfill for every new kind.
- **A group switch that writes every kind of the group.** Switching it on again would turn on kinds the person had off, and a kind added later would start on for an account that had the group off. The group's own row avoids both.
- **Unsubscribing on `GET`.** Mail scanners follow links; only a `POST` changes anything.
- **Skipping a comment push while its recipe page is visible.** The page does not refresh its comments and `?tab=comments` stays in the URL after switching tabs, so the comment was lost silently, and the closed notification took the collapsed count with it.

## Limits

- One mail per comment: three comments in a row are three mails; only new recipes are batched, into the daily digest. The pushes of one recipe's comments replace each other on the device, by tag.
- The digest's claim commits before its mails go out: only a crash mid-run, mail switched off mid-run (logged with the number left unsent) or a run outlasting the shutdown wait (logged; also the container runtime's own stop timeout, which kills the process first when shorter; the shipped `docker-compose.yaml` sets `stop_grace_period: 2m30s` and the `docker run` line `--stop-timeout 150` for that) loses that day's remaining digests rather than sending any twice (at most once, never twice; the `ponytail:` mark on `claimRecipeDigest`). One send time for the whole instance, by the hour.
- Only a confirmed address is mailed.
- No token or identity mails are kinds: setup links, resets, confirmations and security notices cannot be switched off.
- No outbox, no retry: a failed send is logged and the next recipient still gets theirs.
