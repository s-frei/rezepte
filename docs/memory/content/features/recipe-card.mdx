A member can pass a recipe on as a picture: one long PNG with the cover, title, description, servings and times, every ingredient, every step and a credit line. It is for people without an account and for a phone's photo library - whoever gets it needs nothing to cook from it. The same sheet passes on the recipe's link, for the household or for everyone; [Public shares](/features/public-shares) owns the link that works without an account.

<Callout type="warn" title="Traps">

- Nothing may be awaited between the tap and `navigator.share`: an await spends the user activation, so every picture action waits for the drawn blob ([Drawing the picture](#drawing-the-picture)).
- `modern-screenshot` draws through an SVG `foreignObject`, where WebKit smudges a `box-shadow`: the card uses no shadows ([Limits](#limits)).
- `fitRegion` runs in a `requestAnimationFrame` after the `ResizeObserver` fires: a fit inside the callback resizes the observed region again and the browser reports "ResizeObserver loop completed with undelivered notifications".
- The scissors icon hangs below its line and counts as overflow when the line is the region's last child, so without a choice list the line sits outside the region.
- The Link tab's grid cell column is `minmax(0,1fr)`: an implicit column takes its items' min-content width and pushed the list and the copy button off a narrow sheet.

</Callout>

## How it works

### The sheet

**One entry, two tabs.** "Pass on" on the detail page opens `ShareSheet.svelte` with an **Image | Link** switch (a `SegmentedControl` with `track="surface"`, since the sheet is `bg-background`). On a phone the sheet is a `BottomSheet`; from `md` a `wide` `BaseDialog` whose left panel holds the switch above the preview and whose right column holds the hint and the selected tab's buttons. The tab is remembered per device (`localStorage` key `rezepte-share-tab`, in try/catch, defaulting to Image), unless the opener names one: the "Shared publicly" marker and the colophon line open the sheet on Link. From `md` each tab draws its own switch, so `pickTab` moves focus to the new tab's switch when the old one held it; otherwise an arrow key left focus on Close, and the next Enter closed the dialog. The UI says "image", never "card", which reads as a greeting card.

- **Image** keeps the preview, which is the drawn PNG itself, not a second rendering of the card. With an image clipboard but no share sheet (a narrow desktop Firefox or Chrome on Linux), Image shows Copy image beside Save and Link Copy link alone, so the primary's width differs between the tabs; accepted, since each row is honest. On a phone the primary says what this browser will do: **Share image** (`shareImage`) where it has a share sheet for files (`canShareFiles`), else **Copy image** where the clipboard takes an image (`canCopyImage`), else **Save image** - with a round **Save image** beside it while the primary is not Save. On a desktop **Copy image**, **Save image** and **Share…** (only with `canShareFiles`). A primary that promised to share and then downloaded, beside a Save that downloaded too, read as two buttons for one thing.
- **Link** is `ShareLinkPanel.svelte`: a choice between the household address and a public link, then on a phone **Share link** (`shareLink`) and a round copy button where the browser has a share sheet (`canShareLink`), else one **Copy link**; on a desktop **Copy link**, and **Share…** only with `canShareLink`. [Public shares](/features/public-shares) owns what the choice, the lifetime bar and the ticket do.

**The Link tab's preview gives way on small phones.** The controls must stay reachable on the smallest phone and at browser zoom, so the preview gives way before they do. On a phone both tabs share one grid cell, so the sheet keeps the taller tab's height and neither the switch nor the buttons move on a switch; `e2e/public-shares.test.ts` checks that on the `mobile` project. `ShareLinkPanel.svelte` holds the Link tab: a region with the ticket, the scissors and the choice list above its own button row. The sheet is a `tall` `BottomSheet`, since Safari's bars leave an iPhone too little height for the ticket in a normal one.

- **`fitRegion` steps `data-fit` until the region fits:** the chip goes (the stub and the choice rows already say who and how long), the photo flattens, the photo goes, and then the ticket shrinks by CSS `zoom` (`data-ticket`, which frees layout room, unlike a transform) down to a floor; only then does the region scroll. The ticket never goes: dropping it left its room empty above the scissors. A step fits with 1px of slack and no more: an allowance for the tilted ticket's overhang clipped the choice box's bottom edge under the buttons.
- **Each audience row unfolds its own detail when chosen** (`slide`, none under reduced motion): the household's says who opens the link and that it never expires, with "People" behind a perforation; the public one's is the lifetime bar with "Shared links" and Revoke. The two details are equally tall - the household's text box is the lifetime bar's height and both footers share one class - so a pick keeps the card, the sheet, the ticket's fit step and the buttons still; only a footer holding Revoke still wraps on a very narrow screen, and then the public row slides by that line. Holding the public rows' room as gaps around the ticket made the ticket jump inside the sheet, and a sheet that glided to its content's height moved its top edge on every pick.
- **Tests.** `public-shares.test.ts` checks on a narrow phone that the switch, the buttons and the ticket's size stay, and on a computer that the dialog keeps its size under a three-line title.

### Drawing the picture

**The card is drawn off-screen, once per opening.** While the sheet is open, `ShareSheet` mounts `ShareCard.svelte` in a host that is fixed, off the viewport, `inert` and `aria-hidden` - not `display: none`, which has no layout to draw. An attachment on that host calls `renderShareImage` (`#lib/recipe/share-image.ts`, the only importer of `modern-screenshot`): it waits for `document.fonts.ready` and every `<img>`'s `decode()`, gives a failed cover one `tick()` to swap in `PlaceholderTile`, then calls `domToBlob`. Closing the sheet unmounts the host, which revokes the preview's object URL and drops the blob; the host is keyed on the recipe, so a reload of the page's data while the sheet is open draws the picture again. Tests find it by `data-testid="share-card"`.

**Laid out like the phone page.** The card is 420 CSS px wide with the page's own type sizes and tokens, and it inherits the active theme from `<html>`, so the picture looks like the page in light or dark. Quantities are scaled to the servings chosen on the page: `createServings` is read afresh on each opening, the same stored choice the stepper writes. Left off: the source, tags, further photos, favorite and tasty, author, dates and anything that names the instance.

**Sharp, but never too big.** `pickScale` draws at 3x and lowers the scale for tall recipes so width × height × scale² stays under 16,000,000 px and the longest side under 32,767 px, never going below 1x. Legibility depends on the proportion of type to width, not on pixels, so a messenger scaling the picture down leaves it as readable as a phone page.

**Nothing is drawn after the tap.** Every picture action is disabled until the blob exists, because an await spends the tap's activation ([User activation](/conventions/svelte#user-activation)). `shareImage` (`#lib/recipe/share.svelte.ts`) builds the `File` and awaits `navigator.share({ files, title })` first thing, falls back to a download without file sharing or on `NotAllowedError`, and does nothing on any other failure - a canceled sheet (`AbortError`) or a second call while one is open (`InvalidStateError`) - which leaves the sheet open. **Share image** stays disabled while a share is pending. **Copy image** (`ClipboardItem`, with an error toast when the browser refuses) and **Share…** (`navigator.canShare({ files })`) show only where the browser has them.

### Without https, the credit, tests

**Without https.** Plain http on a home network is not a secure context, and browsers then leave out `navigator.share` and `navigator.clipboard` entirely. The phone's primary becomes **Save image** (there is no clipboard for images either) and the desktop dialog's Save turns primary. The sheet - and the dialog on a touch tablet - says to press and hold the preview, because the device's own long-press menu on an `<img>` shares or saves it without any API; only on a touch screen (`pointer: coarse`), as a narrow desktop window gets the phone sheet too and has nothing to hold. `copyText` - behind **Copy link** and the ingredient copy everywhere - falls back to a selected hidden field and `execCommand('copy')`, placed inside the open dialog so its focus trap keeps the selection, and where that fails too, the toast shows the text itself instead of failing silently.

**The credit follows the household setting.** `publicShareAttribution` ("Name Rezepte when sharing", owner only, on by default) switches the card's foot - the ingredient list's perforation, `Lockup` and `SOURCE_URL` - as well as the public page's. The People page shows the switch whether public shares are on or not.

**Tests.** `pickScale` in `share-image.test.ts`. `e2e/share-image.test.ts` runs in `desktop`, `mobile` and a `webkit` project (iPhone 15) that runs only this file and `step-times.test.ts`; it removes `navigator.share` so the phone's primary turns into **Copy image** / **Copy link** and **Save image** downloads deterministically, stubs it to reject with `AbortError` for the cancel case, answers the cover with a `404` for the placeholder case, and takes both APIs away to cover plain http: the copy fallback, the text shown when nothing copies, and the press-and-hold hint. The credit test lives in the serial `public-shares.test.ts`, since it flips the instance-wide setting that file's public-page tests read.

## Why it is this way

Only the browser knows the active theme and the servings chosen on the page, and drawing there gives the service no renderer to run or secure. Photos are same-origin (`/images/…`, `detail` variant), so the canvas is never tainted.

One long picture, rather than several, matches how the card is used: one file in a chat or the photo library, sent in HD where a messenger offers it. The fixed 420px layout keeps that one picture readable however long it gets, since only its height grows.

A dedicated card, rather than a picture of the page, because the page is interactive and responsive: checkboxes, stepper, gallery, buttons. The card reuses `StepList`, `PlaceholderTile` and the quantity helpers, so it cannot drift from the page's formatting.

Drawing before the tap is forced by Safari: `navigator.share` only runs while the tap's user activation lasts, and an await spent on drawing uses it up.

## Rejected

- **Several pictures, split to a fixed aspect ratio.** Readable without HD, but an album instead of one recipe, and a harder layout; the one long picture was the choice.
- **Text alongside the picture.** Messengers treat a file plus text inconsistently, often dropping one; the link is the text alternative.
- **Drawing on the server** (headless Chrome or Go). Heavy in the container, and the server knows neither the theme nor the chosen servings.
- **`satori` with `resvg-wasm`.** About 2 MB of WASM, a subset of CSS, and the fonts loaded separately.
- **`html-to-image`.** Unmaintained, with open Safari issues around fonts and images; `modern-screenshot` is maintained and sits behind one function should it need replacing.
- **Opening the preview in `Lightbox`.** It takes recipe photos only; the preview scrolls in its own frame instead.

## Limits

- `modern-screenshot` draws through an SVG `foreignObject`, and WebKit renders a `box-shadow` there as a smudge beside its box. The card uses no shadows; its pills carry a border, like `MetaPills`.
- iOS Safari hands back an empty canvas above 16,777,216 px, and Chrome and Firefox draw nothing past 32,767 px on a side; `pickScale` stays under both, but a recipe taller than 32,767 CSS px would still exceed the side limit at 1x.
- The picture is a snapshot: later edits to the recipe never reach it.
- A screen reader cannot read the picture; the link is the accessible way to pass a recipe on.
