---
title: Meal plan
description: Recipes and free texts on the days of a week or on the tray, in a personal plan or plans shared like lists, put on shopping lists that follow later changes, with per-plan staples, a live stream and a calendar feed
---

A signed-in person plans recipes and free texts ("Reste") on days, or on the tray for "some time this week", in their personal plan or in any number of plans shared with others on the instance, and puts a week's recipes on a shopping list that keeps following the plan. The service side is `service/internal/mealplan` (service, staples, the list link, huma handler, stream, calendar feed); the list side of the link is in `service/internal/shopping` ([Shopping lists](/features/shopping-lists#planned-entries)); the tables are on [Data model](/architecture/data-model#meal-plans).

<Callout type="warn" title="Traps">

- A plan entry's list blocks are visible to a viewer only for the lists they can access: every list name, id or count in a plan answer comes from `ListMealPlanEntryLists` (the plans week: `ListMealPlanWeekEntryLists`), which carry the viewer's list predicate; reading lists any other way leaks a private list ([The list link](#the-list-link)).
- Removing an entry is a soft delete, so every list read, count and delete must skip blocks of removed entries ([Entries](#entries)).
- A change that alters what a linked list shows must go through `shopping.FollowPlan` and publish on the shopping hub after commit, Move included ([The list link](#the-list-link)).
- The calendar feed's secret sits in the URL path, so `logRequests` writes it to the debug log, as it does a public share's token ([Calendar feed](#calendar-feed)).
- huma names schemas by Go type, so `mealplan`'s types carry a `Plan` prefix; a plain `Summary` or `Entry` panics at startup beside `shopping` ([HTTP API](/architecture/api)).
- svelte-dnd-action clicks a card again after a touch that never became a drag; the card's link drops the untrusted click (`event.isTrusted`), or a tap opens the recipe twice ([The week view](#the-week-view)).
- A Bits menu focuses its first item twice, the second time a frame after opening; an e2e step that focuses an item before that frame has its pick undone (`pickByKey`, [The week view](#the-week-view)).
- `alsoListed` is sent only by the one-entry dialog; the bulk dialog, MCP and any HTTP caller without it must skip an entry already on any accessible list, or a second list gets the week's staples again ([The list link](#the-list-link)).
- The long press on a dragged card lands on the copy the library lifted into `<body>`, outside the list: a guard on the list never hears it; it has to be the window's ([Touch drag](#touch-drag)).
- svelte-dnd-action stops a press at the card: a Svelte `ontouchstart` on the list (delegated) never runs; capture it with `on(…, { capture: true })` ([Touch drag](#touch-drag)).
- CDP's `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` held still fires no long-press `contextmenu`; `Input.synthesizeTapGesture` with a `duration` does. Chromium knows no `-webkit-touch-callout`, so the root's style reads empty there: test the guard by dispatching a `contextmenu`, not by reading the style ([Touch drag](#touch-drag)).
- A desktop day's drop zone runs below the row on purpose (`pb-[60vh] -mb-[60vh]`, clipped by the grid); taking the negative margin away shrinks a short day's target to its cards, taking the clip away grows the page ([The week view](#the-week-view)).

</Callout>

## How it works

### Plans and access

**Access is the creator or an accepted member, as on a list.** Every query in `queries/mealplan.sql` repeats the predicate; anyone else, a pending invitee included, gets `ErrNotFound` (HTTP 404) on reads and changes alike (`TestForeignPlanIsNotFound`, `TestPendingInviteeCannotRead`). Admins have no extra reach.

**Everyone has one personal plan, made on first use.** `Plans` inserts it lazily (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` against a partial unique index), so two first reads make one (`TestPersonalPlanIsCreatedOnce`). It has no name - the client words it "My plan" / "Mein Plan" - and cannot be shared, renamed or deleted: those answer `ErrInvalid` wrapping `ErrPersonal`, HTTP 422 (`TestPersonalPlanCannotBeRenamedDeletedOrShared`). Every other plan is named by its creator, up to 80 runes.

**Sharing works like a list's.** Only the creator invites, removes members and deletes; members do everything else (`TestMemberCannotShareOrDelete`, `TestCreatorCannotLeave`). An invitation is a pending row; accepting is the consent, leaving and declining are the same delete (`TestInviteAcceptFlow`, `TestInviteIsIdempotent`). A new invitation reaches `InviteNotifier`, which `main.go` sets to the notify service ([Notifications](/features/notifications)). Deleting an account deletes its plans and memberships; its entries in other plans keep their title and lose `createdBy` (`TestAccountDeletionDropsOwnPlans`).

### Entries

**An entry is a recipe or a free text on a day or on the tray.** Days are dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`), never instants, and weeks run Monday to Sunday; `week.go` parses strictly, so November 31 is a 422. A read takes `from` and `to`, both inclusive and at most 62 days apart, and answers with those days plus the tray (`TestRangeIncludesBothEnds`, `TestRangeLimit`). A recipe entry copies the recipe's title and starts at its servings unless given others (1-99); a free text has no servings, up to 120 runes (`TestAddRecipeCopiesTitleAndServings`, `TestAddFreeTextHasNoServings`). A deleted recipe leaves the entry with its title and no link (`TestDeletedRecipeKeepsEntry`).

**A day's live entries always sit at positions 0..n-1.** Add appends, Move and Remove renumber the days they touch (`TestMoveRenumbersBothDays`).

**Removing is a soft delete, restorable for 24 hours.** `removed_at` hides the entry, and its blocks on lists hide with it; Restore puts it back at its old position, clamped, and the blocks return with their ticks (`TestRemoveAndRestore`, `TestRestoreBringsBackTickedBlock`). Each Remove purges the plan's removals older than 24 hours, and their list blocks cascade (`TestPurgeDropsOldRemovals`).

**Every change bumps `revision` and publishes after commit.** `mutate` touches the plan with the access predicate (no row: `ErrNotFound`), runs the change, reads the plan back and publishes on the plan's own `Hub` once committed; member changes go through `changed` (`TestChangesPublish`).

### The list link

**`ToList` puts recipe entries on a list at their planned servings.** One transaction, through `shopping.AddPlanned`, onto an existing list the caller can access (else `shopping.ErrNotFound`, HTTP 404, and nothing written) or a new list of theirs. An entry already on a list the caller can access is skipped; one only on someone else's private list is added again, so two people shopping apart each get it (`TestToListSkipsOnlyAccessibleLinks`, `TestToListNeedsListAccess`). Free texts never go on a list (`TestFreeTextNeverGoesOnList`).

**`alsoListed` lets one entry go on a second list.** With the flag, `ToList` skips an entry only when it is on the target list already; the one-entry dialog from an entry's ⋯ sends it, because picking a list for one meal is deliberate. Without it the rule above holds, so the bulk "Auf die Liste", any HTTP caller that leaves it out and MCP's `put_meal_plan_on_list` (which never sets it) put nothing twice (`TestToListAddsToASecondList`, `TestToListAlsoListedOverHTTP`, `TestPutMealPlanOnListSkipsStaples`).

**Changes follow into every linked list, whoever edits the plan.** Servings, Move, Remove, Restore, the purge and deleting the plan call `shopping.FollowPlan`, which bumps every list the entries are on, sets servings when they changed, and checks no list access on purpose; `followed` publishes those ids on the shopping hub after commit. Move changes no list row but bumps and signals, since a list shows the planned day (`TestServingsFollowIntoList`, `TestMoveBumpsLinkedLists`, `TestRemoveFollowsIntoForeignList`, `TestPurgeAndDeleteFollowIntoList`).

**A viewer sees only their own lists in a plan.** `Entry.Lists`, `Plan.Lists` (most entries first) and `Plan.Unlisted` (recipe entries on no list the viewer can access) all come from `ListMealPlanEntryLists`, so Ben reads Anna's plan without learning the name, id or count of her private list, while removing the entry still removes her block (`TestPlanHidesForeignLists`, `TestToListOverHTTP`). A deleted list releases its entries (`TestDeletedListReleasesEntries`).

**`unlisted` counts what is still to shop for: a recipe entry on no accessible list, on the tray or on the reader's today or later.** A past day never counts, so a week wholly behind the reader counts only its tray, and a week ahead counts every day. One predicate, `unlisted` in `week.go`, feeds `Plan.Unlisted` and `WeekPlan.Unlisted`, so the week bar's badge and the overview card's foot agree; the entry card's "Auf keiner Liste" (`PlanEntryCard`) and the dialog's days (`ToListDialog` starts at today in this week) apply the same cut on the client, so a badge of 1 opens the dialog with that one recipe (`TestUnlistedCountsFromTheReadersToday`, `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` "the badge counts what the dialog picks"). **Today is the reader's**: every operation that answers a plan, and the plans week, takes `today` (`Range.Today`), which the handler puts on the context (`WithToday`); `span` in `#lib/api/mealplan.ts` sends the browser's own on every call. Left out (MCP, scripts), the server's date in its time zone decides, a day off for a reader east or west of it around midnight.

### Staples

**Staples are per plan, keyed by `ingredient.Key`.** "Zwiebeln" and "zwiebel" are one staple; marking a key that exists keeps its first name (`TestStapleKeyMergesPlurals`, `TestStaplesArePerPlan`). Staples are not part of `Plan` and do not bump its revision.

### The API

**The routes are in `handler.go` under the `meal-plans` tag.** The readers (list, plans week, get, events, staples, shopping preview) need `plan:read`, every other operation `plan:write`, and a token acts as its issuer (`TestPlanNeedsPlanScopes`). Every operation that answers a plan takes `from` and `to` (both left out: the current week) and `today` (the reader's, for `unlisted`, [The list link](#the-list-link)) and answers with the whole plan for that span, as the list operations answer with the whole list. Errors map as the list's do: not found 404, a recipe gone 404, validation and the personal plan 422 with the field.

**`GET /meal-plans/week` is the plans page in one request.** `PlansWeek` answers the reader's plans in `Plans`' order, each with its week as the plan page would show it: entries on a day (the tray left out, counted in `tray`), `lists` and `unlisted` as `Plan.Lists` and `Plan.Unlisted` for the same range, tray included, through the same list predicate, and `pending` as `Plan.Pending` (only on the reader's own plans); plus the invitations. It costs two queries beyond `Plans` however many plans there are (`ListMealPlanWeekEntries`, `ListMealPlanWeekEntryLists`). The order of the cards is the client's. Left out, the range is the current week in the server's time zone, so the client always passes its own (`getPlansWeek`), or late on a Sunday the strip shows the wrong week (`TestPlansWeekSummarizesEachPlansWeek`, `TestPlansWeekOverHTTP`).

**`entry=<id>` previews one recipe entry** for the one-entry dialog: `PreviewOne` finds it on its day or the tray, whatever `from`, `to` and `tray` say; a free text is 422 (`TestPreviewOne`).

**`PATCH …/entries/{entryId}` moves and changes servings in one body.** A `day` key moves the entry, `null` to the tray, and a missing key keeps its day while `position` places it ([HTTP API](/architecture/api)); `servings` changes first, after the move's own day and position checks, in `Service.Update`, which MCP's `update_meal_plan_entry` calls too, so a refused change leaves the entry where it was (`TestPlanRoundTrip`, `TestTrayMoveWithNullDay`, `TestPatchChecksServingsBeforeMoving`).

**The shopping preview is what the "Auf die Liste" dialog offers.** `GET …/shopping-preview?from&to&tray` lists the recipe entries of the range (and the tray when asked) with their `day` (null on the tray), each ingredient by `(group, index)` with its quantity scaled to the planned servings through the lists' rounding, `alreadyListed` by the caller's accessible lists and `staple` by this plan's staples (`TestShoppingPreviewMarksStaplesAndListed`).

### Live updates

**`GET …/events` is the list stream, keyed by plan.** The handler calls `shopping.Hub.Stream` (`shopping/stream.go`, [Shopping lists](/features/shopping-lists#live-updates)) with a `GetMealPlanRevision` lookup: `retry: 3000`, `event: changed` with the plan's `revision`, a ping every 25 s, and the stream ends once `GetMealPlanRevision` finds no row, so a removed member's stream ends (`TestStreamEndsWhenAccessLost`, `TestStreamForbidsStrangers`). `main.go` closes the plan hub on shutdown.

### Calendar feed

**Every signed-in person can subscribe to their plans in a calendar app.** `POST /api/v1/calendar-feed` (session only, never a token) makes a secret of 32 random bytes, base64url, stores only its SHA-256 in `calendar_feeds` (one row per person, replaced by an upsert) and answers `{url}`, `REZEPTE_PUBLIC_URL` + `/calendar/<secret>.ics`; a new one revokes the old, and `DELETE` revokes it (`TestFeedReplaceRevokesOld`). Deleting the account cascades the row. Without a public URL the answer is the bare path and events carry no recipe link. `GET /api/v1/calendar-feed` answers only `{active, createdAt}`, since the secret is not stored and cannot be shown again (`TestFeedStateTellsWithoutTheSecret`).

**"Im Kalender abonnieren" is in every plan's ⋯, "Mein Plan" too, and opens `CalendarFeedDialog`.** The feed covers every plan the reader is in, so the entry is the same everywhere. The dialog reads the state on each opening: without a feed it offers "Link erzeugen"; with one it says since when the link is active ("Link aktiv seit") and never shows the old address. A created address shows once in a readonly field with Copy (focus moves there; the toast announces the copy), a warning that it reads the plans, and a `webcal:` link that subscribes in one tap on an iPhone or a Mac. "Link neu erzeugen" and "Abo beenden" sit behind a `ConfirmDialog`; ending focuses "Link erzeugen". A bare path from the server is completed with the page's origin. Short iPhone and Google steps stay in the dialog (`e2e/meal-plan.test.ts`, "creates and replaces the calendar link": the first address answers `text/calendar`, the replaced one 404).

**`GET /calendar/{file}` needs no session, since a calendar app has none.** ServeMux cannot match `{secret}.ics`, so the handler takes the segment whole and cuts `.ics`; anything else, and an unknown or revoked secret, is the same 404. It answers `text/calendar; charset=utf-8` with every live, dated entry of every plan the person can access, from 365 days ago on - no tray, no removed entries, no plan they are only invited to (`TestFeedShowsOnlyAccessiblePlans`, `TestFeedSkipsTrayAndRemoved`). The dev server proxies `/calendar` to the service like `/api`.

**`ical.go` writes RFC 5545 by hand, with the standard library.** One all-day event per entry: `DTSTART;VALUE=DATE` the day, `DTEND` the next, `UID` `<entryID>@<host of REZEPTE_PUBLIC_URL>` so a calendar keeps an entry across moves, `SUMMARY` the title, prefixed with a shared plan's name ("Haushalt · Curry"; the personal plan's stay bare, `TestFeedShowsOnlyAccessiblePlans`), and `URL` and `DESCRIPTION` the recipe's permalink when it has one. Lines end in CRLF and fold at 75 octets, never inside a rune (continuation: CRLF plus a space); `\`, `;`, `,` and newlines are escaped in text (`TestICalFoldsAtOctetsNotRunes`, `TestICalEscapes`, `TestICalAllDayDates`, `TestICalUsesCRLF`). The fold test carries an all-umlaut summary, since a mixed one lands its cuts on ASCII and passes with a rune-splitting fold.

### The week view

**`/plans/<id>` shows one plan's week, laid out by width.** `routes/plans/[id]/+page.svelte` holds the head (back arrow below `lg`, the name as h1, people, share and ⋯), the week bar (week label and pager, every linked list as a stamp with its open count, and "Auf die Liste" with the count `plan.unlisted`) and `AddEntrySheet`; `WeekView` in `#lib/components/mealplan/` holds the days: seven `DayColumn`s from 1200px, a `DayRow` per day from `md`, the same `DayRow` as a card on a phone. **The tray leaves no blank end**: from `md` its head sits above two even columns, from `lg` its cards fill the strip (`auto-fit` columns). **A row leaves no blank half**: its label (name, date, "+" beside them) is no taller than one entry, and an odd last entry spans both entry columns (`EntryZone`). The tray (`TrayStrip`) heads all three. A week is empty, and shows `NoteEmptyState`, only without day and tray entries, so a plan with tray entries never shows it.

**The plan and its lists point at each other with stamps** (`LinkStamp`, `StampRow`, [Design system](/architecture/design-system#components)). A plan's own lists, on its overview card and in its week bar, go most used first: `rankLists` counts the plan's entries on each (`entries` on `MealPlan.lists` and `WeekPlan.lists`, `TestRankListsCountsEntriesMostFirst`), and `byPlanUse` breaks ties by name in the reader's locale, so a one-meal list never crowds the week's main one off a one-line card. An entry's own stamps, their popovers and its ⋯ go by name (`byListName`), so none moves when counts change (`plan-ref.test.ts`). The week bar shows every linked list, labeled "On lists" ("Auf Listen"). An entry card shows each of its lists as a link to the list at that meal's block (`listEntryHref`, `?entry=`, [Shopping lists](/features/shopping-lists#planned-entries)); a recipe entry on no list, today or later (the page's `today`, passed down) or on the tray, shows a dashed "Auf keiner Liste" that opens the one-entry dialog; free texts and past days get nothing. The entry's ⋯ adds "Auf eine Liste…" ("Auf eine weitere Liste…" once it is on one) and "„X“ öffnen" for three lists at most; the card's "+n" reaches the rest. Cards and menus reach the dialog through `setToList` / `getToList` (`#lib/mealplan/to-list.ts`), the page's context, not through props down `WeekView` and `EntryZone`.

**The one-entry dialog is `ToListDialog` with `only`.** It previews the entry alone (`getEntryShoppingPreview`, `?entry=`), has no days, opens its ingredients and gives the recipe no checkbox, offers only the lists the entry is not on (says which it is on: "Steht schon auf „Wocheneinkauf“"), offers a new list when it is on every one, takes the menu's words as its title, names the target on its button ("Auf „Vorrat“ setzen") and sends `alsoListed` ([The list link](#the-list-link)). `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts`, "one meal goes on a second list from its menu". **The bulk dialog starts with "Irgendwann mitnehmen" on while the tray holds recipes on no list**, so it offers what the button's count says ("the dialog takes the tray along when it holds recipes on no list"), and its "already" line names every list those recipes are on.

**`?day=&entry=` jumps to a meal**, the address of a list's plan stamp (`planJumpHref` in `#lib/shopping/plan-ref.ts`; the load's canonical-`from` redirect keeps both). After the navigation the page unfolds a folded day on a phone, scrolls the card (`[data-entry-id]`, else the day's `[data-day]`, a `tabindex="-1"` section) to the middle, focuses the card's link or the day, lights it up once (`animate-flash`) and drops the two parameters with `replaceState`, so Back or a reload does not jump again ("a planned block wears its plan stamp, which jumps to the meal", head stamp and folded day included). **`routes/plans/[id]/+layout.svelte` keys the page on the plan's id**: from one plan to another the page mounts afresh, so folds, open dialogs and the history flag do not carry over; paging weeks keeps it.

**A phone folds this week's days gone by into one row** (`PastDays`, "Mo–Do · 6 Einträge"); another week shows every day. Today's day is `aria-current="date"`, outlined and marked "Heute". Planning onto a folded day opens the fold.

**Every day and the tray is one `dndzone` of the type `plan-entry`** (`EntryZone`), with `zoneOptions` and `applyDrag` from `#lib/recipe/dnd.ts` as in the recipe editor. A finger holds 300ms (`delayTouchStart`) so a swipe still scrolls. While a card is dragged `WeekView` keeps the zones as the drag shows them (`dragged`, also passed down as `dragging`); the zone that receives the card saves the move. The cards are no Tab stops (`zoneItemTabIndex: -1`) and the library's own drag hints are off (`autoAriaDisabled`), since they would point to a keyboard drag that does not exist: the keyboard and a screen reader move an entry with its ⋯ "Auf Tag verschieben…" (`MoveSheet`), which also pages to other weeks.

### Touch drag

**A held finger gets no link menu, and the guard is the window's.** By the time Android's `contextmenu` (and iOS's callout) comes, the drag has lifted a copy of the card into `<body>`, so the long press lands on the copy's link outside the list. `EntryZone` captures `touchstart` on the list, then blocks `contextmenu` on the window and sets `-webkit-touch-callout: none` on the root, which the copy inherits, until the last finger lifts (`touches.length` 0). A card taken off the page mid-press sends its `touchend` nowhere, so the next first finger and the list's own teardown let go too. A tap still opens the recipe ("a held finger on a card gets no link menu", mobile e2e).

**The lifted copy is drawn on a layer behind it** (the `li`'s `::before`, reaching 8px past the card, `isolate`; in dark mode, where `shadow-lift` barely shows, the layer and the card take `surface-elevated` and `shadow-float-dark`, so the copy still rises): an outline on the card itself hugged the photo and the stamps and was cut where the column card's photo meets its rounded top. **The slot where it will land is a dashed box**, `visible` against the library's `visibility: hidden` on the shadow item, as `editor/DropSlot.svelte` does.

**A day row takes a drop on its name** ("a finger drops a card on an empty day by its name", mobile e2e). The library finds the zone under the finger (`useCursorForDetection`), and an empty row's zone was only its "+" box. While a card is dragged, an empty row's list covers the whole row (`absolute inset-0` in the positioned `DayRow`, the "+" left in place under it); once the slot is in, the row has its full layout and the list reaches over the day's head: up on a phone (`-mt-12.5 pt-12.5`, the head's height), left over the name's column from `md`, where the row is two columns (`-ml-42 pl-42`, the 152px column and its gap), so the finger that brought the slot in stays over the zone. Without that reach the slot flickered in and out as the row changed layout under the finger.

**A moved or removed card takes its ⋯ with it, so `WeekView` hands the focus on** (`focusAfter`, after the redraw and a frame): a move to the card's ⋯ in its new day (out of the week: the "+" of the day it left), a removal to the next card's ⋯, else the one before, else the day's "+"; Undo to the restored card's ⋯. The move sheet and the entry menu skip their own return of focus then (`onCloseAutoFocus`). `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` drives both by keyboard. A Bits menu focuses its first item twice, a tick after opening and again from its focus scope's `requestAnimationFrame`, so the e2e helper `pickByKey` waits until a `menuitem` holds the focus and then for one frame (a frame callback asked for later runs after the scope's) before it focuses one; picked in between, the pick is undone and Enter takes the first item (the old flake of "the keyboard stays on the week after a move or a removal").

**Every change is optimistic and goes through `PlanState.apply`.** `placeEntry` in `#lib/mealplan/entries.ts` renumbers both days the way the server will answer (`entries.test.ts`); a refused change puts the plan back, and a 404 for the entry refetches the week. Removing toasts "… entfernt" with Undo, which calls `restorePlanEntry`; when the entry was on lists the reader can open, the toast names the list ("Curry entfernt – auch von „Wocheneinkauf“") or counts them, since the lists follow (`e2e/meal-plan.test.ts`, "removing a listed recipe says the list lost it too"). Adding waits for the server, which names the new entry.

**`AddEntrySheet` plans a recipe or a note in one field.** Day chips (the week's seven and "Irgendwann") preset to the day whose "+" opened it, else today in this week, else the shown Monday. The field is an ARIA combobox over `listRecipes({ q, limit: 8 })` (the recently changed recipes while empty), and the typed text as a note last; Enter takes the highlighted row, and does nothing on the empty field. `addRows` lists only an answer for exactly the text in the field, so a note typed and taken before its search answers stays a note (`entries.test.ts`, `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts`). A recipe starts at its own servings. The desktop's "Eintragen", the empty week's button and a phone's "+" open it; `shell.add` gives that "+" the week's name and the sheet's open state.

**A tap on a recipe entry opens `/recipes/<slug>?servings=<n>`.** The detail page reads `servingsParam` (`#lib/recipe/servings.ts`: a whole number clamped to 1-99, anything else ignored), keeps it as the reader's choice, as the stepper would, and drops it from the address (`replaceState`): left there, a reload or the recipe loading again after a photo would undo a change made since.

**"Auf die Liste" is `ToListDialog`, one form over `getShoppingPreview`.** The days start at today when the shown week holds it, else at the shown Monday, and end on its Sunday; a `RangeCalendar` behind the days' button changes them and folds away once both ends are picked, or on Escape: Bits closes a dialog from a document `keydown` listener, so the calendar's wrapper stops Escape on its own element first. "Irgendwann mitnehmen" shows only while the tray holds a recipe on no list, and counts those. The list preselects the last one the plan went to (`rezepte:last-plan-list`), then the list the shown week went to (`plan.lists[0]`), then `rezepte:last-list`, else a new list named "Einkauf 23.–29. Nov." after the picked days (`spanLabel`; one day reads "Einkauf Mo 23. Nov."), a name that follows the days until the reader types one. Each recipe starts with every ingredient picked but its staples; one without picks stays off the list. Recipes already on a list the reader can open are left out and counted in one line, named when every list they are on is the same one. Each row names its day: the weekday in the shown week, with the date beyond it (the preview's `day`), "Irgendwann" for the tray. A changed range or tray, or a staple marked with the row's house, refetches the preview; until it answers the recipes shown are the old days', so the frame dims (`aria-busy`) and the button stays disabled. The refetch keeps the picks of recipes still in it, except that a staple's own pick follows the staple, in every recipe that holds it. `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` covers the staple, the plural merge, the second run, the held refetch and Escape.

**The calendar offers at most 62 days, the server's limit.** With one end picked, `isDateDisabled` grays out the days that would span more, so the preview's range check never refuses the range; a 422 the preview gets anyway says so in place (`plan_to_list_range_error`), without a Retry that would fail again (`e2e/meal-plan.test.ts`, the held refetch).

**"In den Plan" on a recipe page is `AddToPlanDialog`**, behind the slip's "In den Plan" key beside "Kopieren" ([Cooking](/features/cooking#copying-the-ingredients)), shown where the basket is (`addable`). Chips for the fourteen days of this and next week, today marked and picked, and "Irgendwann"; the plan opened last (`lastPlan`, else the personal one), offered only when there are several (a failed plans load says so, "Deine Pläne ließen sich nicht laden", with Retry); the servings the page shows. It shows only with the recipe's title, which the toast names ("… für Mittwoch eingeplant", with the date for next week). Saving remembers that plan, and the toast's "Woche öffnen" goes to `/plans/<id>?from=<Monday>` (`planHref`).

### The plans overview

**`/plans` is the plans overview, built like `/lists`, from one request.** `routes/plans/+page.ts` calls `getPlansWeek` for the reader's own Monday to Sunday; there is no request per plan. Invitation cards head it (under an `sr-only` h2), then "Deine Pläne" and "Mit dir geteilt". "Neuer Plan" is the top bar's action and a phone's "+", and opens `/plans/new`. A person with only "Mein Plan" gets a dashed "Plant ihr zusammen?" tile beside it.

**A plan with something this week is a card, an idle one a chip.** `isIdle` in `#lib/mealplan/overview.svelte.ts`: a named plan with no entry on a day of this week and none on its tray; "Mein Plan" is always a card. Cards sit in the lists' grid; idle plans follow as a wrapping band of name chips (with faces when shared) under "Diese Woche leer", in both sections, so a dozen idle plans cost a few lines, not a dozen cards. **The order is the client's: "Mein Plan", then meals this week (most first), then last change** (`byUse`, `overview.test.ts`), inside each section.

**A card has four parts, which line up across a row of cards** (subgrid, [Design system](/architecture/design-system#components)): name and meta, strip, today, lists. The foot says "2 noch auf keiner Liste" (`unlisted`) while recipes wait, "Nichts einzukaufen" when there are none. The first part sits at its row's foot, so a neighbor's wrapping name puts the room above a title, never between meta and strip. **The meta is short enough for the narrowest phone**: "9 Mahlzeiten · mit Mila Majoran", "1 ohne Tag", "Nichts geplant"; no faces says "only you" and the strip says "this week", so neither is written; pending invitees are named ("Eingeladen: Jonas Zimt").

**The strip is seven cells, Monday to Sunday** (`weekStrip`, `cellKind`): the day's first meal as its photo, a letter for a recipe without one (a deleted recipe too, `recipeId` null), a pencil for a note, "+N" for more. Each cell's screen-reader text is one message (`plans_day_sr_*`); today's cell is `aria-current="date"`. "Heute …" links to `/plans/<id>#today` (today's day carries `id="today"`) and is named whole ("Heute: … und 2 weitere"); an empty today is a quiet "Heute eintragen" (muted; orange stays for a today with a meal), `/plans/<id>?add#today`, which opens the add sheet on today and drops `add` from the address.

**Nothing streams the overview, so it reads again on return**: window focus, a visible tab and a page from the back-forward cache each `invalidateAll` (at most once in two seconds), which also moves it on to a new day or week. There is no per-reader stream to listen to, and one per card would be one connection per plan.

`e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` covers the order, the chip, the strip, the way into a plan and back, "Heute" and "Heute eintragen".

### Named plans in the app

**A new plan starts as its own page, as a new list does** ([Shopping lists](/features/shopping-lists#the-overview-and-a-new-list)). `/plans/new` is the name field at the top over the empty week, dimmed and `inert` (a `PlanState` of an empty plan that is never sent). Enter or "Plan anlegen" creates it and replaces the draft with `/plans/<id>` and `page.state.created`, which puts the focus on the plan's share button once, in `onMount`: `page.state` keeps `created` for the whole visit and `PlanState` replaces the plan on every change, so an effect on the two would pull the focus back after each edit, invite and live update (`e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` holds the focus on the ⋯ through a member's change). Escape, × or back make nothing.

**A named plan's head carries who is on it, Share and ⋯, as a list's head does.** The faces open `PeopleSheet`, the round share button (the creator's) opens `ShareDialog`, and `PlanMenu` holds Rename for everyone, Share and "Plan löschen" for the creator, "Plan verlassen" for a member, each destructive one behind a confirmation, and "Im Kalender abonnieren" for every plan. "Mein Plan" shows no faces or share button, and its ⋯ holds only the calendar. `ShareDialog`, `PeopleSheet` and `InvitationCard` are the lists' components, which take the people and the requests as props.

**When the shown plan is gone for the reader, the page leaves it for the overview, and Back never reopens it.** Deleting, leaving, and a 404 on any change or refetch (the owner deleted it, or removed the reader, which ends the stream) toast `plan_gone` or the action's own toast, then go back when the entry before is the overview the plan was opened from (`afterNavigate` records it; the back arrow does the same), else to `/plans` with `replaceState`; the overview puts the focus on its heading (`arrival`). Week paging replaces its entry too, so Back leaves the plan instead of walking back through weeks.

**A plan the reader cannot open never shows the recipes' 404 page, and its `load` never toasts**, since SvelteKit runs a load to preload a link on hover or touch. `routes/plans/[id]/+page.ts` turns a 404 into a redirect to `/plans?gone`, where the overview toasts `plan_gone` once, reads its plans again and drops `gone` with `replaceState` (in `afterNavigate`: the router refuses it before it is up), or, sent from the overview itself (a stale card), goes back to the entry it came from, so Back is not a step to the same page; a reader only invited goes to `/plans` without it, under the invitation card. The load also moves any `from` that is not a Monday of another week (a Sunday, this week's Monday, garbage) on to the canonical address. `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` covers delete and leave with Back (no access toast), a plan opened directly, paging and Back, a deleted plan's link, a stale card and an invited-only one.

**Plan invitations wait on the overview** as `InvitationCard`s, their buttons named whole ("Annehmen: …"); accepting opens the plan. When the shell's count moves on its own (it refreshes when the tab comes back), the overview reloads its data, so a new invitation's card appears without a reload; the lists overview does the same for list invitations. The count joins the lists' in `#lib/shopping/invitations.svelte.ts`: the place's dot shows both, the switch's Listen and Woche segments each their own. An invitation's link from the mail and push is `/plans/<id>`: a pending invitee lands on the overview under its card, someone who accepted elsewhere on the plan. The address is `/plans`, never `/week`: "week" would read as this week, and the page holds every plan. Accepting drops the shell's count before it opens the plan, since the overview, which counts the cards, unmounts. `e2e/meal-plan.test.ts` covers creating, sharing with a second browser, the live entry, the removal, renaming, deleting, leaving and the dots.

## Why it is this way

**A phone's "+" makes what its page is about** instead of opening the new-things sheet: a new plan on the overview, a meal on a plan's page; a new list is one tap away on the Listen side.

**The Week side lands on the overview, not on the plan opened last**, decided by the owner over reopening the last plan: it matches the lists, and the card's "Heute …" line makes today one tap away.

**Plans copy the lists' sharing**, because the owner asked for "wie Listen": one model of creator, invitation and accepted member for both, the same 404 for a stranger and a pending invitee.

**The plan pushes into the list rather than the list reading the plan**, because the list must keep working on its own: it keeps a copied block with its ticks, and only the link (`plan_entry_id`) lets later plan changes find it.

**The feed has its own secret instead of an API token**, because tokens are admin-only and reach the whole API, while the feed is for everyone and reads one thing; a leaked feed URL is fixed by making a new one.

**A soft delete rather than a delete with Undo re-adding**, because re-adding would make new list items and lose their ticks.

## Rejected

- **A plan pull-down on the week.** Switching plans hid in it, and nothing showed how plans and lists relate; the overview shows every plan and its lists.
- **A list that is a live date range of the plan.** It would be a second kind of list whose ticks must survive every recomputation, a rolling window would change the list on its own, a recipe edit would reach it, and its visibility would clash with the plan's.
- **Fixed meal slots (breakfast, lunch, dinner).** Three slots on seven days are 21 mostly empty fields on a phone; a day holds a list of entries instead.
- **One plan per instance.** An instance serves several households; personal data is shared by invitation, as lists are, so each person has a personal plan and any number of shared ones.
- **A week start from the locale.** English accounts, and the English docs screenshots, would get Sunday weeks, and members of one plan would page through different weeks; weeks run Monday to Sunday everywhere.
- **Showing every linked list to every plan member.** It would leak the name and contents of a private list to people its owner never shared it with.
- **An iCalendar library for the feed.** All-day events need a handful of lines, escaping and folding; a dependency would be against "standard library first".
- **Dragging a card by keyboard.** A card holds a link and a ⋯; a third Tab stop per card, and a keyboard drag across seven zones, cost more than "Auf Tag verschieben…", which also reaches other weeks.

## Limits

- Deleting an account publishes nothing to the lists its plan entries were on; those lists show the stale block until their next refetch.
- Deleting a list does not signal open plans; a plan page shows that list until its next refetch.
- Staple changes do not publish, so another member's open dialog does not update live.
- A calendar app refreshes the feed on its own schedule, often hours apart; a changed plan reaches it only then.
