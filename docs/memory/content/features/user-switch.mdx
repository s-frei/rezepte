## How it works

`/switch` ("Who's cooking?", "Wer kocht?") lists the people signed in on this device as a stack of index cards; a tap makes that person the active one without a password. The server side - the device cookie, the accounts list, the switch, removing a person, sign-out keeping someone on the device - is in [Users and authentication](/features/users-and-auth). This page is the screen.

- **A route, not an overlay.** `/switch` is in `authPages` (`frontend/src/lib/auth.svelte.ts`), so it opens with nobody active and renders bare, without top bar or bottom nav; `navPlace` lists it as `null`. Its `+page.ts` waits for the root load (`await parent()`), then loads `listDeviceAccounts()` and reads `?next=` through `safeNext` (`/` when absent or unsafe).
- **Order:** the active person's card first (from `session.user`), then the device's accounts in the API's order, then the add card (`/login?add=1`, with `next` unless it is `/`). With nobody active there is no active card and no "Cooking now"; the list header still reads "On this device".
- **A card** (`AccountCard.svelte`) is a grid `[avatar | name + info line | 2.5rem action column]`, at least 5.5rem tall, ruled like an index card, in the person's color pair (`userColorClasses`). The avatar is `PersonMark xl` on a `bg-background` ring. The name is two lines at most (`line-clamp-2`, `wrap-anywhere`, the full name in `title`), a step smaller for long names and narrow screens (`nameSize` in `switch.ts`). The info line is "Cooking now" (or "That's you" while editing) on the active card, "Sign in again" on an expired one, otherwise `seenLabel`: today, yesterday, the weekday from 2 to 6 days back, `switch_seen_last_week` from 7 to 13 days, then `switch_seen_weeks` with `{count}` whole weeks (never a date: the approved mockups said "Vor 5 Wochen hier"), in calendar days of the device's clock. The active card carries a `primary` ring; an expired one is dashed on `surface`, its avatar greyed.
- **A tap:** the active card goes back (`history.back()`, or `/` without history), like the ×. With nobody active there is no ×: back would only bounce onto `/switch` again. An expired card goes to `/login?add=1&username=<name>&next=<next>` (`addLogin`): every link from here to the login carries `next`, because when the active session itself ran out the only card is the person's own expired one, and a deep link or push tap must still land after signing in again. Any other card starts the switch: the card grows into `HelloSplash` ("Hello, Jo!", the first word of the name, `firstName`), `switchAccount` runs meanwhile, and after a short greeting the app reloads onto `next` with `location.assign`, so nothing of the person before stays in memory. While the splash shows, `<main>` is `inert`, so the page behind it takes no keys. A `409` (the session ran out since the list was loaded) goes to the login like an expired card, a `404` (removed elsewhere) reloads the list, anything else is a toast; every failure clears the splash and `pending`, so the cards take taps again, and a skipped or failed transition (`ready` and `finished` reject) still shows the splash.
- **Editing:** "Edit" is a toggle (`aria-pressed`), shown only while there is someone to remove. Every card but the active one gets a × ("Remove Jo"), a button of its own beside the card, since a button cannot hold another; it covers the card's empty action column. A tap on the card or its × opens `RemoveDialog` - a `BottomSheet` below md, a `BaseDialog` from md - with the person as a small card; confirming calls `removeDeviceAccount` and drops the card. A remove under way takes no second one (`busy`): a double tap on Remove landed on the closing dialog and then on the card behind it, which opened the dialog again for the person being removed. The dialog hands no focus back then (its trigger, the ×, goes with the card): the page moves it to the next card's control, else the one before, else the heading (`tabindex="-1"`); a failed remove puts it back on the ×. You remove yourself through "Sign out", not here.

### Ways in and out

- **The entry row** is the first row of the desktop account menu (`UserMenu.svelte`) and of the phone's You sheet. Both render `SwitchEntry.svelte` inside their own row (`markSize` `xs` in the menu, `sm` in the sheet); it fetches the device's accounts with `knownAccounts()` (`frontend/src/lib/device-accounts.ts`, which swallows errors into `[]`) each time the menu or sheet opens. With others on the device the row reads "Who's cooking?" and shows up to three overlapping `PersonMark`s of them (the server never lists the active person); with nobody else it reads "Sign in another person" with a user-plus icon. The sheet closes on the navigation (`afterNavigate`), not on the tap. Both rows go to `/switch`, whose last card is "Add a person"; the switch never clears cook timers.
- **`/login?add=1`** is the login in add mode: the title is "Sign in another person", a `username` query prefills the name and moves autofocus to the password (the `autofocus` attribute, not `onMount`: SvelteKit refocuses the body after a navigation), a back link leads to `/switch` (with `next`) - a `history.back()` when the login was opened from `/switch`, so the picker's × still returns to the app instead of to the login - and a success does `window.location.assign(safeNext(next))` (a full load, like a switch), in both modes; the identity-provider form carries the same `next`.
- **Every signed-out moment** goes through `signedOutTarget` (`device-accounts.ts`): `/switch` while `knownAccounts()` is non-empty, else `/login`. A failing list counts as empty, so it never breaks a path. The callers: sign-out (`sign-out.ts`; after clearing timers and disabling push, one navigation, without `next`; the signed-out notice shows only on `/login`; the `/switch` card of the person who left reads "Sign in again"), the root load's `401` (`+layout.ts`, `authPages` exempt, with `next`), and a `401` mid-use from the API client or an upload (`redirectSignedOut`, with `next`; not on `/login` or `/switch`, which it checks itself, not for login, `GET /auth/me` or logout). Several calls failing together leave once: `redirectSignedOut` keeps its first run in `leaving` and hands it to the rest.
- **Other tabs follow.** A switch, a sign-in (both login modes, `/welcome`) and a sign-out change the cookie under every other open tab, which would go on acting as the person before. `announceSession` (`frontend/src/lib/session-broadcast.ts`) posts the new person's id, or `null`, on the `BroadcastChannel` `rezepte-session` (without one, a `localStorage` write that reaches the others as a `storage` event); the root layout's `followSession` reloads a tab whose `session.user` differs. One channel object sends and listens, since a message never reaches the object that posted it.
- **The back-forward cache** would restore the app as it was left, maybe as the person before a switch: the root layout reloads on a `pageshow` with `persisted`.

### Motion

All of it inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; under reduce everything is simply there, the × leaves at once and the pick has no transition (the splash still shows).

- The cards fan out of a pile into the list on a spring (`linear()` easing), each starting a little short of one card pitch higher, so the pile shows its edges; the mascot pops in (app.css's `pop`, which the ×'s and the splash avatar's entries share).
- Hover stays inside the card's own space: the card lifts 2px with `shadow-lift`, its slot rises above the pile's order (`z-index: 101` while hovered or holding focus-visible), and with a mouse the avatar drifts a few pixels towards the pointer, the name block half that (`--px`/`--py` set in `pointermove`), while a soft light follows it; a press scales the card down a touch and leaving eases everything back. An expired card's key jiggles. No 3D tilt: a card leaning towards the pointer reaches over its neighbor and cuts the active card's ring, so the motion stays inside the frame.
- Leaving without a pick (the × or the active card) plays the fan-in backwards: last card first (`--n` on the list counts the slots), each slides back to the first card's place and fades, the mascot's `pop` runs reversed; only then does the page go back. `leaving` swallows a second tap. The cards keep keyframes of their own (`gather`): `fan` reversed holds each card opaque until it reaches the pile, where `gather` fades it on the way.
- Editing wobbles each card once and pops the × in.
- The pick is a view transition named `chosen-card`: the card carries the name until the splash takes it. The transition and its CSS live in `HelloSplash.svelte` (`grow` in its module script), shared with the login, whose open sheet or card grows into the same splash after a sign-in ([Users and auth](/features/users-and-auth)). Its corners start from the growing element's (`grow(show, corners)` sets `--grow-from`: the login passes the sheet's or the card's computed `border-radius`, square once the plate has collapsed), the switch's card from `--radius-index-card`. The traps, each of which showed in the spike: the root's old and new image get `animation: none`, so the page does not cross-fade under it, and the old one `opacity: 0`: the update callback also sets `picking`, and the other cards fall 24px and fade in 200ms in the live new image - with the static old image still shown underneath they reappeared as they faded; the card's old and new image fill the group (`height: 100%; object-fit: cover`), the new one opaque from the start and the old one fading out on the browser's own fade-out, with a duration and easing of its own; the image pair is clipped and its radius goes from `radius-index-card` to 0 with the group. The splash takes the name only while the transition runs (`named`), so nothing keeps one afterwards. The splash's keyframes for the pseudo-elements are declared `@keyframes -global-switch-corners` and referenced as `switch-corners`: written `-global-` in the `animation` property, the name matches no keyframes and the corners silently did nothing. The root rules hang off `html[data-switching]`, which `grow` sets only for this transition: a component's styles stay loaded after it is left, and unscoped they would have switched off the cross-fade of every page transition afterwards.
- **The arrival** carries the splash over the reload (`frontend/src/lib/arrival.ts`), after a switch and after a sign-in with the form alike. Once the greeting has had its time it fades with the avatar (`greeted` on `HelloSplash`), then `markArrival` stores the person's token, `--color-user-<color>` as the page resolved it, in `sessionStorage` (`rezepte-arrival`; never under reduced motion, and only when `next` is a client route by `match` from `$app/paths`: a page outside the app, the API's docs, never reads the key, which then painted the tab's next load) and `location.assign` runs. The inline script in `app.html` reads and deletes the key before the first paint, sets `--arrival-paint` and `html[data-arrival=""]`, and an inline `<style>` there paints `html[data-arrival]::after` as a full-screen panel in it - and the canvas too. Once the root layout mounts (`onMount(arrive)`) the panel closes as a `clip-path` circle into the visible `[data-arrival-target]` - the account menu's avatar from md, the You tab below - which pops once (`data-arrival="closing"`, timed in app.css), and is gone soon after. The overview's cards rise on the arrival's timing meanwhile (`'arrival' in dataset` in `routes/+page.svelte`). No visible target (cook mode, a 401 landing on `/switch` or the login) fades the panel instead. The script removes it after 2s if the app never took it over, so a failed load cannot leave it stuck; one-shot, a later reload shows nothing. The traps: the fallback page links no stylesheet - app.css and its tokens come with the bundle - so the panel's rule lives in `app.html` and carries the resolved color, not the token's name; a fixed panel stops short of the scrollbar gutter `:root` keeps (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), which showed the page's ground as a strip down the right edge, so the canvas wears the paint and turns back with the circle (`arrival-ground`); and the circle closed onto an empty ring while the avatar's photo still loaded, so `arrive` waits for it to decode, capped at 300ms.
- The list is `isolate`: the pile's `z-index` (the first card on top) otherwise stacked the cards over the remove dialog.

### Testing

`frontend/e2e/user-switch-screen.test.ts` runs against one typed `DeviceAccount[]` fixture and one `mockDevice` helper (`page.route` on `/api/v1/auth/accounts` and `/api/v1/auth/switch`; a later `mockDevice` overrides an earlier one): the order and the active card, the expired card's and the add card's way to the login carrying `next`, edit mode and removing (a double tap on Remove removes once), a pick landing on `/`, a `409` landing on the login with `next` and a `404` reloading the list, focus after a remove, a pick with motion leaving no `data-switching` and no inline `view-transition-name` behind and `<main>` inert, a pick onto `/api/...` leaving no arrival key, the × with motion going back exactly once after a double tap (counted `popstate`s, polled until half a second after the first), the × after the add mode's back link returning to the app, the long name at 320px, and the ways there: the entry row, the add mode, the root load's 401 and a 401 mid-use (desktop only) landing on `/switch?next=%2Flists` without a × and a mocked switch reloading onto `/lists`. The pure helpers are in `switch.test.ts`. `user-switch.test.ts` is the real thing, no mocks: it adds a second person through the menu row and the add card, switches both ways, signs out as the second person (landing on `/switch`), removes them, checks a cook timer after a switch, a deep link after the own session ran out landing on its recipe after signing in again, and a second tab of the same context reloading as the new person after a switch and onto `/switch` after a sign-out; with motion, a pick ends with the arrival gone and the panel's `::after` cleared, and a reload shows none.

Traps writing the real one: the device's accounts live in the browser context's cookie jar, so each test starts with an empty device and a second person is added by signing in through the UI (the `login` helper signs in by API and would not hand over). `signOut` in `helpers.ts` waits for `/login`, which is where a lone person lands; with others on the device the spec clicks "Sign out" itself and waits for `/switch`. A test that must end on `/login` after a sign-out creates its user from a second browser context so the first holds one person only. A sign-out does not make an expired card of a lone person - it lands on `/login`; a session that ran out does, and a password change from a second context (`PATCH /auth/me`, which ends the person's other sessions) is how the spec makes one. Asserting `data-arrival` right after `waitForURL` raced the arrival's own removal; the spec checks the end state only. A test waiting for another tab's reload awaits that page's `load` event set up before the click, or its menu opened on the old page. Cook mode has no menu or nav, so the picker is reached with `page.goto('/switch')` there, and the timer dock exists in cook mode only: look for it on the recipe's cook page after the switch, not on `/`. The timers are the device's, not the person's. `svelte-check` fails on `resolve('/switch')` until the route exists.

## Why it is this way

- **Index cards in one column, one left edge.** The owner picked the "Karteikasten" look (deck2) over aprons on a rail, a menu card and a carousel: names of any length fit a card, and a phone and a tablet show the same thing.
- **A reload after the switch** rather than swapping `session.user`: every cache in memory belongs to the person before.
- **No PIN and no password to switch** (owner decision): only people who signed in on this device appear, each once.

## Rejected

- **An overlay over the app** (the spike): the page's scrollbar showed beside it, and it needed a session to render in.

## Limits

- The "last here" line reads the device's clock, so a tablet set a day wrong says "yesterday" for today.
- The splash holds for a minimum time even when the switch answered at once, so the greeting can be read; a slow server makes it longer, never shorter.
- The screen tests run against mocks; the device cookie and the switch itself are the service's to test ([Users and authentication](/features/users-and-auth)).
