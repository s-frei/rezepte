`/switch` ("Who's cooking?", "Wer kocht?") lists the people signed in on this device as a stack of index cards; a tap makes that person the active one without a password. The server side - the device cookie, the accounts list, the switch, removing a person, sign-out keeping someone on the device - is in [Users and authentication](/features/users-and-auth#several-people-on-one-device). This page is the screen, the ways to it, and the switch's motion.

<Callout type="warn" title="Traps">

- **A button cannot hold another, and an `<a>` cannot hold a button**: the × is a sibling of the card, covering its empty action column.
- **A double tap on Remove landed on the closing dialog and then on the card behind it**, reopening the dialog; a remove under way takes no second one (`busy`).
- **The list is `isolate`**: the pile's `z-index` otherwise stacks the cards over the remove dialog.
- **The pick's view transition needs the root's old image hidden** (`opacity: 0`, `animation: none`), or the other cards reappear as they fade ([Motion](#motion)).
- **The arrival panel's rule lives in `app.html` with the resolved color**: the fallback page links no stylesheet, so a token name resolves to nothing.
- **`markArrival` only for a client route**: a page outside the app never reads the key, which then painted the tab's next load.
- **Prefill focus with the `autofocus` attribute, not `onMount`**: SvelteKit refocuses the body after a navigation.
- **E2E: the `login` helper signs in by API and never hands over**; a second person on the device is added through the UI ([Testing](#testing)).

</Callout>

## How it works

### The screen

- **A route, not an overlay.** `/switch` is in `authPages` (`frontend/src/lib/auth.svelte.ts`), so it opens with nobody active and renders bare, without top bar or bottom nav; `navPlace` lists it as `null`. Its `+page.ts` waits for the root load (`await parent()`), then loads `listDeviceAccounts()` and reads `?next=` through `safeNext` (`/` when absent or unsafe).
- **Order:** the active person's card first (from `session.user`), then the device's accounts in the API's order, then the add card (`/login?add=1`, with `next` unless it is `/`). With nobody active there is no active card and no "Cooking now".
- **A card** (`AccountCard.svelte`, its look in [Design system](/architecture/design-system)) carries the person's mark, name and an info line in their color pair. The name never truncates to one line (`nameSize` in `switch.ts` steps it down for long names). The info line is "Cooking now" on the active card, "Sign in again" on an expired one, otherwise `seenLabel`: relative days and weeks in calendar days of the device's clock, never a date.

**A tap decides by the card's state.**

- The active card goes back (`history.back()`, or `/` without history), like the ×. With nobody active there is no ×: back would only bounce onto `/switch` again.
- An expired card goes to `/login?add=1&username=<name>&next=<next>` (`addLogin`). Every link from here to the login carries `next`, because when the active session itself ran out the only card is the person's own expired one, and a deep link or push tap must still land after signing in again.
- Any other card starts the switch: the card grows into `HelloSplash` ("Hello, Jo!", `firstName`), `switchAccount` runs meanwhile, and after the greeting the app reloads onto `next` with `location.assign`, so nothing of the person before stays in memory. While the splash shows, `<main>` is `inert`.
- A `409` (the session ran out since the list was loaded) goes to the login like an expired card, a `404` (removed elsewhere) reloads the list, anything else is a toast. Every failure clears the splash and `pending`, so the cards take taps again, and a skipped or failed transition (`ready` and `finished` reject) still shows the splash.

**Editing removes people from the device.** "Edit" is a toggle (`aria-pressed`), shown only while there is someone to remove. Every card but the active one gets a × ("Remove Jo"); the card or its × opens `RemoveDialog` - a `BottomSheet` below md, a `BaseDialog` from md - and confirming calls `removeDeviceAccount` and drops the card. The dialog hands no focus back (its trigger goes with the card): the page moves it to the next card's control, else the one before, else the heading; a failed remove puts it back on the ×. You remove yourself through "Sign out", not here.

### Ways in and out

- **The entry row** is the first row of the desktop account menu (`UserMenu.svelte`) and of the phone's You sheet, both rendering `SwitchEntry.svelte`. It fetches the device's accounts with `knownAccounts()` (`frontend/src/lib/device-accounts.ts`, which swallows errors into `[]`) each time the menu or sheet opens: with others on the device it reads "Who's cooking?" with their marks, with nobody else "Sign in another person". The sheet closes on the navigation (`afterNavigate`), not on the tap. The switch never clears cook timers.
- **`/login?add=1`** is the login in add mode: "Sign in another person" as the title, a `username` query prefills the name and moves autofocus to the password, a back link leads to `/switch` with `next` (a `history.back()` when the login was opened from `/switch`, so the picker's × still returns to the app), and a success does `window.location.assign(safeNext(next))`; the identity-provider form carries the same `next`.
- **Every signed-out moment** goes through `signedOutTarget` (`device-accounts.ts`): `/switch` while `knownAccounts()` is non-empty, else `/login`. A failing list counts as empty, so it never breaks a path. The callers:
  - sign-out (`sign-out.ts`; after clearing timers and disabling push, one navigation, without `next`; the card of the person who left reads "Sign in again");
  - the root load's `401` (`+layout.ts`, `authPages` exempt, with `next`);
  - a `401` mid-use from the API client or an upload (`redirectSignedOut`, with `next`; not on `/login` or `/switch`, and not for login, `GET /auth/me` or logout). Several calls failing together leave once: `redirectSignedOut` keeps its first run in `leaving` and hands it to the rest.
- **Other tabs follow.** A switch, a sign-in (both login modes, `/welcome`) and a sign-out change the cookie under every other open tab. `announceSession` (`frontend/src/lib/session-broadcast.ts`) posts the new person's id, or `null`, on the `BroadcastChannel` `rezepte-session` (without one, a `localStorage` write that reaches the others as a `storage` event); the root layout's `followSession` reloads a tab whose `session.user` differs. One channel object sends and listens, since a message never reaches the object that posted it.
- **The back-forward cache** would restore the app as it was left, maybe as the person before a switch: the root layout reloads on a `pageshow` with `persisted`.

### Motion

All of it sits inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; under reduce everything is simply there and the pick has no transition (the splash still shows). The timings and keyframes are in the components.

- **Entering and leaving.** The cards fan out of a pile into the list and the mascot pops in (app.css's `pop`). Leaving without a pick plays that backwards, last card first (`--n` on the list counts the slots), and only then goes back; `leaving` swallows a second tap. The cards keep keyframes of their own (`gather`) for the way in, since `fan` reversed holds each card opaque until it reaches the pile.
- **Hover stays inside the card's own space.** The card lifts, its slot rises above the pile's order, and with a mouse the avatar and name drift towards the pointer (`--px`/`--py` from `pointermove`). No 3D tilt: a card leaning towards the pointer reaches over its neighbor and cuts the active card's ring.
- **The pick is a view transition named `chosen-card`.** The transition and its CSS live in `HelloSplash.svelte` (`grow` in its module script), shared with the login ([Users and auth](/features/users-and-auth#the-login-form)). `grow(show, corners)` sets `--grow-from` from the growing element's radius; the keyframes are `switch-corners` and the root rules hang off `html[data-switching]`, which `grow` sets only for this transition. The general rules are under [View transitions](/architecture/design-system#view-transitions). Specific to this one: the root's old and new image get `animation: none` so the page does not cross-fade, and the old one `opacity: 0`, because the update callback also sets `picking` and the other cards fade out in the live new image - with the static old image underneath they reappeared as they faded. The card's image pair fills the group (`object-fit: cover`) and is clipped. The splash takes the name only while the transition runs (`named`), so nothing keeps one afterwards.
- **The arrival carries the splash over the reload** (`frontend/src/lib/arrival.ts`), after a switch and after a form sign-in alike. Once the greeting has had its time, `markArrival` stores the person's resolved `--color-user-<color>` in `sessionStorage` (`rezepte-arrival`; never under reduced motion, and only when `next` is a client route by `match` from `$app/paths`) and `location.assign` runs. The inline script in `app.html` reads and deletes the key before the first paint and sets `html[data-arrival]`, whose `::after` an inline `<style>` there paints as a full-screen panel. Once the root layout mounts, `arrive` closes the panel as a `clip-path` circle into the visible `[data-arrival-target]` (the account menu's avatar from md, the You tab below); with no visible target the panel fades instead. The script removes the panel itself if the app never takes it over, so a failed load cannot leave it stuck; one-shot, a later reload shows nothing.
- **Arrival traps.** A fixed panel stops short of the scrollbar gutter `:root` keeps (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), which showed the page's ground as a strip down the right edge, so the canvas wears the paint too and turns back with the circle (`arrival-ground`). The circle closed onto an empty ring while the avatar's photo still loaded, so `arrive` waits for it to decode, capped.

### Testing

**`frontend/e2e/user-switch-screen.test.ts` runs against mocks**: one typed `DeviceAccount[]` fixture and one `mockDevice` helper (`page.route` on `/api/v1/auth/accounts` and `/api/v1/auth/switch`; a later `mockDevice` overrides an earlier one). It covers order, the ways to the login carrying `next`, editing and removing (a double tap removes once), the `409` and `404` answers, focus after a remove, a pick leaving no `data-switching` or inline `view-transition-name` behind, a pick onto `/api/...` leaving no arrival key, the × going back exactly once after a double tap (counted `popstate`s), the long name at phone width, and the entry points. The pure helpers are in `switch.test.ts`.

**`user-switch.test.ts` is the real thing, no mocks**: adding a second person, switching both ways, signing out to `/switch`, removing, a cook timer surviving a switch, a deep link after the own session ran out, a second tab following, and the arrival ending cleanly.

**Traps writing the real one.**

- The device's accounts live in the browser context's cookie jar, so each test starts with an empty device and a second person is added by signing in through the UI (the `login` helper signs in by API and would not hand over).
- `signOut` in `helpers.ts` waits for `/login`, which is where a lone person lands; with others on the device the spec clicks "Sign out" itself and waits for `/switch`. A test that must end on `/login` creates its user from a second browser context so the first holds one person only.
- A sign-out does not make an expired card of a lone person; a session that ran out does, and a password change from a second context (`PATCH /auth/me`, which ends the person's other sessions) is how the spec makes one.
- Asserting `data-arrival` right after `waitForURL` races the arrival's own removal; check the end state only.
- A test waiting for another tab's reload awaits that page's `load` event set up before the click, or its menu opens on the old page.
- Cook mode has no menu or nav, so the picker is reached with `page.goto('/switch')` there, and the timer dock exists in cook mode only: look for it on the recipe's cook page after the switch. The timers are the device's, not the person's.

## Why it is this way

- **Index cards in one column, one left edge.** The owner picked the index-card box over aprons on a rail, a menu card and a carousel: names of any length fit a card, and a phone and a tablet show the same thing.
- **A reload after the switch** rather than swapping `session.user`: every cache in memory belongs to the person before.
- **No PIN and no password to switch** (owner decision): only people who signed in on this device appear, each once.

## Rejected

- **An overlay over the app.** The page's scrollbar showed beside it, and it needed a session to render in.
- **A 3D tilt on hover.** A tilted card reaches over its neighbor and cuts the active card's ring.

## Limits

- The "last here" line reads the device's clock, so a tablet set a day wrong says "yesterday" for today.
- The splash holds for a minimum time even when the switch answered at once, so the greeting can be read; a slow server makes it longer, never shorter.
- The screen tests run against mocks; the device cookie and the switch itself are the service's to test ([Users and authentication](/features/users-and-auth)).
