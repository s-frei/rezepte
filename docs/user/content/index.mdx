---
title: Rezepte
description: A self-hosted recipe manager for your household
---

Rezepte keeps your household's recipes in one place: a single binary with an embedded web app, an SQLite database and your photos in one data directory. Members log in with a username and password, write recipes with ingredient groups and steps, tag and search them, and cook from a phone at the stove.
