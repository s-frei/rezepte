<Hero name="guests" alt="Rezepte: the waving cookbook mascot on a stack of cookbooks at a kitchen table, with a dill, a marjoram and a cinnamon sprite" />

Rezepte keeps the recipes of your household and your friends in one place: a single binary with an embedded web app, an SQLite database and your photos in one data directory. You invite people with a setup link, they sign in with a password or your sign-in provider, write recipes with ingredient groups and steps, tag and search them, and cook from a phone at the stove.
