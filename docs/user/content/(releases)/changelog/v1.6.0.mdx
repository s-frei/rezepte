Upgrade as usual (see [Updates](/operations/updates)). A few things are worth knowing as the one who runs it:

- **Rezepte looks for new versions.** Once a day it asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release, sending nothing about your instance. Set `REZEPTE_UPDATE_CHECK=false` to stop it (see [Updates](/operations/updates#how-you-learn-about-a-new-version)).
- **Mail goes out through a queue.** A dialog no longer waits for the mail server, and a failed attempt is tried again. Rezepte also stops within a few seconds now, so a systemd unit no longer needs `TimeoutStopSec=150` (see [Running the binary](/getting-started/binary)).
- **Plans stream like lists.** An open meal plan keeps one connection open, as a shopping list does. A setup that streams lists streams plans too; the nginx block in [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy#live-lists) now covers both.
- **Calendar links need the public address.** Without `REZEPTE_PUBLIC_URL`, a calendar link takes the address you open Rezepte at, and its meals carry no link to their recipe.
- **Search is rebuilt once.** On the first start, Rezepte fills the new search indexes from the recipes there are.

## Plan the week, shop from the plan

**Shopping & week** → **Week** opens your meal plans. Put a recipe or a note such as "leftovers" on a day, or on **Someday**, and drag it to another day when plans change. **Put on the list** puts the days you pick on a shopping list, without the staples you always have. The list keeps following the plan: a moved day, new servings or a removed meal reach it.

<Screenshot name="plan-week" caption="A shared plan's week, the days in one strip" />

Everyone has **My plan**. Create more plans and share them by invitation, as you share a list; changes show for everyone within a second. Plans and lists carry stamps that lead to each other, and every plan can show up in your calendar app. See [Meal plan](/guide/meal-plan).

## What can I cook?

Pick the ingredients you have in the overview's search, and the recipes that use most of them come first. Each card says how many ingredients are still missing, or **All there**. Tick what else you have under **Already at home**, and every card counts it at once. See [What can I cook?](/guide/tags-and-search#what-can-i-cook).

<Screenshot name="ingredient-search" caption="Potato and onion picked, the soup's missing ingredients in the row" />

Search itself finds more: parts of words ("bread" finds "cornbread"), plurals, and umlauts and ß typed either way. The best matches come first, and each card says where it was found. See [Tags and search](/guide/tags-and-search).

## The owner sees every mail and every new version

Under **Settings → Email**, the **Outbox** lists every mail of the last 30 days, with whether it arrived and why not. A mail the server could not take right away is tried again for a few minutes. See [The outbox](/guide/mail#the-outbox).

When a newer version is out, the owner sees a note on the overview with a link to the release, and the account menu ends with the running version. Members never see it. See [Updates](/operations/updates#how-you-learn-about-a-new-version).

## API

- Meal plans: `GET` and `POST /api/v1/meal-plans` and `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}` list, create, read, rename and delete plans. `POST /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}/entries` plans a recipe or a note, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/entries/{entryId}` move, change and remove one, and `POST …/entries/{entryId}/restore` brings it back. `GET /api/v1/meal-plans/week` answers every plan's week in one request.
- Plans and lists: `GET /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}/shopping-preview` shows what days would put on a list, and `POST /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}/shopping` puts them there. `GET` and `PUT …/staples` read and mark a plan's staples. List entries carry the plan they came from (`planId`, `planName`, `planDay`, `planEntryId`, `planPersonal`), shown only to members of that plan (`planMember`), and who put them there (`planAddedBy`).
- Sharing plans: `POST /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}/invitations` invites, `POST` and `DELETE …/membership` accept, decline or leave, and `DELETE …/members/{userId}` removes a member. Notifications have a new kind, `plan_invite`.
- Scopes: reading plans takes the new `plan:read`, everything else `plan:write`. Putting a plan on a list takes `lists:write` as well.
- Live: `GET /api/v1/meal-plans/{id}/events` sends each change's `revision` as Server-Sent Events. This stream and the shopping list's now send the current `revision` as soon as they connect.
- Calendar: `GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /api/v1/calendar-feed` read, create and end your calendar link, with a session only. `GET /calendar/{file}` serves the plans as iCalendar and needs no sign-in.
- Search: `GET /api/v1/ingredients` suggests ingredient names. `GET /api/v1/recipes` takes `ingredients` and `have`, one parameter per name, and its cards carry `pantry`, what each recipe uses and misses, and `match`, where a search found it.
- People: `GET /api/v1/people` entries carry `planCount`, the plans a person made besides their own.
- Mail: `GET /api/v1/settings/mail/history` reads the outbox, owner only, with a session. Mailing a setup link answers once the mail is queued; a later failure shows in the outbox, not as `mailError`.
- Updates: `GET /api/v1/settings/update` tells the owner whether a newer version is out.
- MCP: six tools for meal plans, `list_meal_plans` and `get_meal_plan` with **Meal plans: Read**, and `add_to_meal_plan`, `update_meal_plan_entry`, `remove_meal_plan_entry` and `put_meal_plan_on_list` with **Write**. `put_meal_plan_on_list` also takes **Shopping lists: Write**, and `search_recipes` takes ingredients you have. See [Connect an AI assistant](/api/mcp).

The [API reference](/api/reference) lists every endpoint.

## Also in this release

- **New:** People cards count the meal plans each person made. See [People](/guide/users#read-the-list).
- **New:** A recipe names its author under the title, in their color, and each comment sits on a card in its writer's color.
- **New:** The browser tab shows the mascot's face.
- **Improved:** Rezepte speaks to everyone on an instance, not to one household. Settings say **For everyone**, notifications **Everyone else**, and the **Link** tab of **Pass on** offers **Just for us**.
- **Improved:** **Shopping & week** in the top and bottom bars holds your lists and your plans.
- **Improved:** On a recipe, **Add to shopping list** and the copy button sit together at the end of the ingredients.
- **Improved:** With public sharing off, the **Link** tab of **Pass on** says so in one card, and the owner gets a link to switch it on.
- **Improved:** Your own color stays on you and your things, no longer on the browser bar or the focused comment box.
- **Improved:** Toasts sit above the bottom bar on a phone, and **Undo** is easier to hit.
- **Security:** Resetting someone's password closes their open setup link, so it can no longer set a password over the new one.
- **Fixed:** The photo carousel on a phone moves one photo per swipe.
- **Fixed:** After signing in again, you land on the page you were going to.
- **Fixed:** A new invitation after one you answered counts in the badge again.
- **Fixed:** A row that scrolls sideways keeps the focused item clear of its arrows.
