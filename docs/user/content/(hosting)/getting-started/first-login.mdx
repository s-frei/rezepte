Sign in as the owner, secure the account and invite the household.

<Screenshot name="login" caption="The login page" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Log in as the owner

    Use `admin` (or the value of `REZEPTE_ADMIN_USER`) and the password from `REZEPTE_ADMIN_PASSWORD` in [Quick start](/getting-started).

    Locked out? After five wrong passwords in a row, the username is locked for 30 seconds, doubling with each further try up to 15 minutes, and the login page says so. Devices already signed in are not affected, and a correct password after the wait clears the count.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Change the owner password

    Open "Settings" and choose **Change** in the **Password** row (8–128 characters). This signs every other device out of the account.

    Nobody else can change this password for you, not even another admin. Put it in a password manager before you go on.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add the household

    Open **People** and press **Add account** for each person. Pick a role:

    - **Admin** manages accounts.
    - **Member** cooks and edits recipes.

    Leave **Send a setup link** selected. You get a one-time link to pass on, or Rezepte mails it once [mail](/guide/mail) is set up. They pick their own password, or continue with [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on) if you offer it. [People](/guide/users) covers **Set a password now** and later changes.

    <Screenshot name="welcome" caption="What the link opens to for the person you invited" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot name="first-login" caption="The overview right after the first login, before anyone has added a recipe" />

## The owner account

The first account owns the instance:

- It cannot be deleted or turned into an ordinary member.
- It is the only account that can make someone else an admin.
- `REZEPTE_ADMIN_USER` and `REZEPTE_ADMIN_PASSWORD` are read only on the first start. Changing them later changes nothing on its own.

<Callout type="warn" title="There is no handover">
  The owner stays the owner for the life of the instance; you cannot move ownership to someone else. If its password is lost, only whoever runs the server can get back in, with [`--reset-superadmin-password`](/getting-started/configuration#--reset-superadmin-password).
</Callout>

## Forgot your password?

- Once mail is set up, the login page shows **Forgot password?** below **Sign in**.
- Enter your username or email address. Rezepte mails a link to your confirmed address ([Settings → Profile](/guide/settings#profile)). It is valid for one hour and works once.
- Setting the new password signs you out everywhere else.
- Still have an open setup link from an admin? Use that instead; no mail goes out.
- Signing in with an identity provider and have no password? The mail says so.
- The owner cannot reset by mail; see [`--reset-superadmin-password`](/getting-started/configuration#--reset-superadmin-password).
- The page answers the same whether or not the account exists.
