<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start a recipe

    Click "New recipe" (top bar, bottom navigation, or the command palette).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Fill in the basics

    Under "Basics": a title (required), an optional description, servings (1–99), "Prep time" and "Cook time" in minutes, and "Source" as an `http://` or `https://` link.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add ingredients

    Under "Ingredients", use "Add group" to split the list into sections, e.g. "Filling" and "Mash topping". Each row takes a quantity, a unit, a name and an optional note.

    <Screenshot name="editor-ingredients" caption="Ingredient groups in the editor" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add steps and tags

    Write the steps under "Steps", one per line, and add search tags under "Tags".

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Save

    Click "Save".

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  Pressing `Enter` in an ingredient's "Ingredient" or "Note" field adds a new row below it. Every ingredient, group and step has a drag handle for reordering — or select it with `Space`/`Enter` and move it with the arrow keys (see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts)). Leaving the editor with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?" first.
</Callout>

## The detail page

<Screenshot name="recipe-detail" caption="A recipe's detail page" />

Servings and times show as pills at the top. Ingredients list with tick boxes for checking items off while shopping or cooking; steps follow below. "Edit" opens the editor, and "Delete" — reached through the "More actions" menu — deletes the recipe after confirming.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Limits">
    - Title: up to 200 characters
    - Description: up to 2000 characters
    - Servings: 1–99
    - Preparation and cooking time: up to 1440 minutes (24 h) each
    - Source: an `http://` or `https://` link, up to 500 characters
    - Tags: up to 20, each up to 40 characters
    - Ingredient groups: 1–20, each with up to 100 ingredients
    - Steps: up to 50, each up to 2000 characters
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is a slug?">
    Every recipe gets a URL-friendly slug derived from its title — German umlauts and ß are transliterated (ä→ae, ö→oe, ü→ue, ß→ss) and remaining diacritics and punctuation are stripped. If another recipe already has the same slug, a number is appended (`-2`, `-3`, …).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deleting a recipe">
    Deleting a recipe also removes its uploaded photos. This cannot be undone.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
