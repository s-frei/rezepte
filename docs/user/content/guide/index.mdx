## Person cards

<Screenshot name="person-card" caption="A person card opened from the author circle of a recipe" />

<Screenshot name="person-card" mobile caption="The same card on a phone" />

Every person circle opens a small person card: their photo, display name, login name and role, and "Recipes by …" with the number of recipes they wrote, which takes you to the overview narrowed to that person. You find the circles on recipe cards (who added and who last edited), on the faces of the people who marked a recipe tasty, and in the list of people under Settings. The card is for a household to know who is who; a person without a photo shows their initial in their own color. Setting your photo is in [Settings](/guide/settings#profile).

## Getting around

On desktop, the top bar carries "New recipe" and the "Account menu" (your initial, top right) with links to "Settings" and "Sign out". Admins also see an "Import" button next to it, which opens [Import and export](/guide/import-export). On a recipe, in the editor and in Settings, the top bar names the page next to the app's name, and the two buttons make way for what that page needs: a recipe's own "Edit", "Cook mode" and "More actions", or nothing at all in the editor and in Settings, which keep their actions on the page.

On a phone, the same actions move into a small bar at the bottom right: a book for "Recipes" (the overview), a magnifying glass for "Search", which opens the command palette, and a person, "You", a sheet showing who is signed in with "Profile", "API", "Shared links", "People" (and, for admins, "Import & export") and "Sign out". The round "+" at the bottom left opens the editor for a new recipe. While you scroll down to read, the bar shrinks to just the current icon; scroll back up or tap it to bring it back. The editor and Settings have a one-line head at the top instead of a sidebar: it names the section you are in, and tapping it opens "Contents", a sheet listing every section to jump to.

## Settings

"Settings" has four pages, and a fifth for admins, listed in its sidebar (or its "Contents" sheet on a phone):

- **Profile** — your display name, color and language, the theme, your password and a link to this guide. See [Settings](/guide/settings).
- **API** — the address of this instance's API and its documentation, and the address an [AI assistant](/api/mcp) connects to; admins also issue [API tokens](/api/tokens) here.
- **Shared links** — the [public links](/guide/sharing#public-links) you have created, to copy or revoke; admins see everyone's.
- **People** — everyone who has an account and their role; admins also manage the accounts here, and the owner sets the household's [editing rights](/guide/editing-rights), public links and [link previews](/guide/sharing#the-household-setting). See [People](/guide/users).
- **Import & export**<Audience role="admin" /> — pass recipes with their photos to another Rezepte. See [Import and export](/guide/import-export).

"Sign out" sits at the foot of the same list, set apart from the pages.

## Command palette

<Screenshot name="command-palette" caption="The command palette, opened with ⌘K / Ctrl+K" />

Press `⌘K` (macOS) or `Ctrl+K` (everywhere else) anywhere except the login page and [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode), or on a wide screen click the palette button at the end of the search bar, to open the command palette; on a phone, tap "Search" in the bottom bar. It jumps straight to a recipe by name, or runs "New recipe" and "Settings" without leaving the keyboard. Once you have typed something, "All results for …" opens the overview with that search, where the filters and every match are.
