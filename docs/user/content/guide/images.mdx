Photos follow the recipe's editing rights: whoever may edit a recipe may add, reorder and remove its photos and choose its cover. See [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights).

<Screenshot name="editor-images" caption="The photo section of the editor" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Add photos

    In the editor's "Photos" section, drop image files onto the drop zone or click "browse" to pick them from disk.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Choose the cover

    The first photo uploaded becomes the recipe's cover automatically. Pick a different one any time with "Make cover" on that photo.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Reorder or remove

    Drag a photo by its handle to reorder it, or use "Remove photo" to delete it.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  On a recipe that already exists, photos upload immediately as soon as they are added. On a new, unsaved recipe they are queued and upload only once you click "Save".
</Callout>

## On the detail page

On a wide screen, the cover photo shows large, with the other photos as thumbnails below it. Clicking a thumbnail puts that photo in the large spot; clicking the large photo opens the lightbox. On a phone, the photos form a carousel at the top of the page that starts on the cover: swipe through them, follow the dots, and tap one to open it in the lightbox.

<Screenshot name="lightbox" caption="The lightbox, with the original download link" />

"Download the original" downloads the largest copy Rezepte keeps — the uploaded photo scaled to at most 2400 px on its longest side, not the file as it came off the camera.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Formats and limits">
    JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted, up to 10 MB per file and up to 20 photos per recipe. Each side of a photo must be between 64 and 12000 px, and the whole photo at most 60 megapixels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Orientation and stored sizes">
    A photo's orientation is fixed from its EXIF data on upload, so it always displays upright regardless of how the camera recorded it. Three JPEG sizes are stored for every photo: the original (resized to at most 2400 px), a detail size (1600 px) and a thumbnail (960 px).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deleting the cover">
    Removing the current cover photo promotes the next remaining photo to cover automatically.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
