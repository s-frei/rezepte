## Profile

The card at the top of the page is how Rezepte shows you to the household. It opens with your circle - your photo if you added one, otherwise your initial in your color - above your display name and your role.

"Add photo" (or "Change photo" once you have one) opens a file picker for a JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 10 MB. The photo then appears in a round frame to place: drag it to move, use the slider, a pinch or the mouse wheel to zoom in to four times, or use the arrow keys to move and plus and minus to zoom. Confirm, and the round section you framed becomes your photo, here and wherever Rezepte shows you - on the recipes you added, in the list of people and in your person card (see [Tour of the app](/guide#person-cards)). The photo is cut from the file you chose, so to frame it differently, choose the file again. A photo, or a framed section, smaller than about 64 pixels is refused with a message. "Remove photo" takes it off and puts your initial back.

<Screenshot name="avatar-crop" caption="Framing a photo: the round window shows what the circles will show" />

If a photo is missing or cannot be loaded, Rezepte shows your initial in your color instead. Your color and initial still identify you, so nobody has to recognize a face to know whose recipe it is.

"Color" is your color: eight to choose from, and it marks you wherever the app names you. Tapping a swatch saves it right away, no button. A small dot marks a color somebody else already has — you can take it anyway, the dot is only there so a household can spread out across the palette. Your initial stays in the circle either way, so nobody has to tell two colors apart to know whose recipe it is.

"Language" is "English" or "German", and picking one reloads the page into it. It is saved to your account rather than to this browser, so it follows you to any device you sign in from — the next login, anywhere, opens in that language. It changes the interface only; it never translates a recipe's own title, ingredients or steps, and it does not touch the language the recipes were written in. Missing your language? See [Add a language](/contributing/add-a-language).

"Login name" is what you type to sign in. It is shown, not editable, and it stays the same however often you rename yourself.

"Display name" is the name on your recipes, in the list of people and on the circles that sign a recipe card. Change it and press "Save profile"; up to 64 characters, and leaving it empty puts your login name back. It does not have to be unique, so two people can both be "Mia".

"Email" is optional and never used to sign in - it is only there for the future. Leave it empty, fill it in, or clear it again with "Save profile". If you signed in through an identity provider, this may already show the address it gave Rezepte, marked "Confirmed by your sign-in provider"; typing your own address here replaces that and starts fresh unconfirmed.

## Sign-in

<Screenshot name="settings-signin" caption="The Sign-in card, before the account is connected" />

This card is there only when your instance offers sign-in through an identity provider such as Google (see [Single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on)). "Continue with …" takes you to the provider and back; from then on the same button on the login page signs you in without a password. Once connected, the card says since when, and "Disconnect" undoes it and signs you out on your other devices - but only while your account also has a password, because without one you could no longer sign in at all. Set a password below first.

## Change password

Enter the current password once, then the new password (8–128 characters) twice. Changing your password signs out every other device that was logged in as you; this device stays signed in.

While you type the new password, a bar below it rates it from "very weak" to "strong". It knows the most common leaked passwords, keyboard runs like `qwertz` and your own names, so `anna1990` counts as weak for Anna. The rating is advice: any password of the right length can be saved. A password manager's generated password is the easy way to "strong".

## Appearance

Choose "System", "Light" or "Dark". The same choice sits in the account menu behind your initial at the top right, and on a phone in the "You" sheet, so you do not need to open the settings for it. Unlike your language, the theme is stored in this browser only — it does not follow you to another device or browser.
## Help

The "Help & guide" card opens this guide in a new tab. It is the way here from inside the app whenever something is unclear.

## About Rezepte

The last card on the page says which version of Rezepte your instance runs, and links to its source code and to the [Changelog](/changelog) - handy when you ask for help or wonder what an update brought.

## API

<Screenshot name="api" caption="The API tab, here as an admin sees it" />

Every signed-in member has this tab. It carries the base URL of this instance's API, ready to copy, the number of endpoints and areas the instance serves, and the version it is running. "Interactive docs" opens the try-it-out documentation, "openapi.json" the raw document — both work straight from the browser with your session, no token needed. See [API](/api) for what you can do with it.

Issuing an API token needs an admin; the tab says so if you are not one. Admins find the token list on the same page — [API tokens](/api/tokens) covers it.

The "AI assistants" card on the same tab carries the address an MCP client such as Claude Code connects to, with a button to copy it, and "Setup guide" opens [Connect an AI assistant](/api/mcp).

## Shared links

Every signed-in member has a "Shared links" tab: the public links you have created, across every recipe, with their status and Copy and Revoke for each - see [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links). You can narrow the list by status. Admins can also switch to everyone's links there, narrow them to one person, and revoke them all.

## Email<Audience role="owner" />

The owner has an "Email" tab, between "People" and "Import & export", for the mail server Rezepte sends setup links through — see [Mail](/guide/mail).

## Import and export<Audience role="admin" />

Admins have one more tab, "Import & export", for passing recipes with their photos to another Rezepte — see [Import and export](/guide/import-export).

## Signing out

"Sign out" ends the current session on this device. You find it in the "Account menu" (your initial, top right on desktop) and at the foot of the Settings sidebar; on a phone, in the "You" sheet of the bottom bar and at the foot of the Settings "Contents" sheet.

The instance owner can change another person's display name, color and photo from the list under "People"; nobody else can. See [People](/guide/users) for that and the rest of the account management admins do there. The owner also finds the household's **Recipe editing** setting there (see [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights)), **Link previews** and **Public sharing** (see [Sharing recipes](/guide/sharing)).
