---
title: Servings
description: Scale ingredient quantities to any number of servings
---

<Screenshot name="servings" caption="The servings stepper on the recipe detail page" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Change the number of servings

    On a recipe's detail page, use the `−`/`+` stepper next to "Portionen" (1–99). Every ingredient quantity updates immediately, scaled from the recipe's stored servings.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Reset

    Once you have scaled a recipe, a "Zurücksetzen" link appears next to the ingredients; it returns to the recipe's original, stored servings.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  Scaled quantities round the way a cookbook would — to quarters, thirds and halves near common kitchen fractions, whole numbers from ten upward — rather than showing long decimals. Your choice of servings is remembered per recipe in this browser and carries over into [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode); it never changes the recipe itself, so it is not shared with other people or devices. Editing the recipe's own "Portionen" field is what changes the baseline everyone else scales from.
</Callout>

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Where the scaled number is stored">
    Nowhere on the server — scaling happens entirely in the browser. The recipe's stored servings and every ingredient quantity are untouched; only units are never converted (an amount in "EL" stays in "EL", just multiplied).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
