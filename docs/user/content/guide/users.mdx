---
title: People
description: See who has an account; admins add, reset and delete accounts, and the owner assigns roles and edits profiles
---

Everyone with an account finds "People" in Settings: a list of every account on this Rezepte, with its display name, its login name, its color and its role, so it is always clear who takes part and who looks after the accounts. A "Member" account reads the list and nothing more; the actions on this page are for admins.

Admins are not all equal here. Every admin looks after the members; admins themselves belong to the instance owner — the account created on the very first start (see [First login](/getting-started/first-login)). Actions you are not allowed to take are simply not shown in the row, so the list already reflects this table:

| | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Cook, add recipes, edit and delete your own | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit someone else's recipe and its photos | if open | ✓ | ✓ |
| Delete someone else's recipe, or change who may edit it | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Lock recipes to their authors for the whole household | – | – | ✓ |
| Change your own password | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change your own display name and color | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| See everyone who has an account, and their role | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add a member, reset a member's password, delete a member | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add an **admin**, reset an admin's password, delete an admin | – | – | ✓ |
| Hand out the "Admin" role or take it back | – | – | ✓ |
| Change **someone else's** display name or color | – | – | ✓ |
| Issue [API tokens](/api/tokens) | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Withdraw or restore a member's right to share publicly | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Withdraw or restore an **admin's** right to share publicly | – | – | ✓ |
| Turn public links on or off for the household, and set their lifetime | – | – | ✓ |
| Delete or demote the **Owner**, or reset their password | – | – | – |
| Delete your own account | – | – | – |

There is exactly one Owner and it never changes: the role cannot be handed to anyone, which is why "Owner" is not offered when you create an account or change a role.

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Open the list

    Go to "Settings" → "People". Everyone is listed under their role — "Owner" first, then "Admins", then "Members", with the number of people beside the last two — and by name within each group. Each person shows their display name and, under it, the login name.

    On a wide screen the actions you may take sit at the end of each row. On a phone, tap a person to open a sheet with their actions; rows with a small arrow are the ones that open something, and your own row takes you to your profile.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add an account

    Click "Add account" and fill in a username, a password (8–128 characters) twice and a role. The password is rated as you type, the same way as in [Change password](/guide/settings#change-password), against the username and display name you entered. "Reset password" rates the new one too.

    <Screenshot name="user-create" caption="Creating a new account" />

    "Admin" manages accounts and can do everything a member can; "Member" cooks and edits recipes. The "Admin" option is offered to the owner only — any other admin creates members.

    "Language" starts on the language you are reading right now, on the assumption that a household sets up accounts in the language it speaks. It is only a starting point: the language is the one profile field nobody else can change once the account exists, so the new member switches it themselves in their own settings if you guessed wrong.

    "Display name (optional)" is the name Rezepte shows on recipes and in this list; leave it empty and the login name is used. The color picker starts on the color the fewest accounts hold, and a small dot marks each color somebody already has — you can still pick it. Both can be changed later, by that person or by the owner.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Change a role

    Use the "Role" select in a member's row — on a phone, the "Admin" / "Member" switch in their sheet — to change their role; they move to the matching group straight away. Only the owner has this control; every other admin reads the role from the group the person is listed under.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Change someone's name or color

    The pencil in a member's row — "Edit profile" in their sheet on a phone — opens their display name and their color. This is the owner's alone — no other admin sees it.

    It is not offered on your own row either. Your own name and color live in "Settings" → "Profile", so there is only ever one place to change them (see [Settings](/guide/settings)).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Allow or withdraw public sharing

    On a wide screen the "More actions" menu in a person's row (next to the pencil, where there is one) carries "May share publicly" as a checkbox; on a phone it is a switch in the sheet that opens when you tap the person. It follows the same rule as resetting a password: any admin can switch it for a member, only the owner for an admin, and nobody for the owner. Turning it off pauses that person's existing public links until it is turned back on, and hides "Create public link…" on their recipe pages meanwhile. It has nothing to do with whether public links are allowed for the household at all - that is the owner's own **Public sharing** switch, below the list of people. See [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Reset a password

    "Reset password" sets a new password for that member and signs them out on every device. Type the new password twice; the reset only goes through when both match. Resetting another admin's password is the owner's alone, and nobody resets their own password here — that is "Change password" in your own settings, which asks for the current one.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Delete an account

    "Delete" in their row — "Delete account" in their sheet on a phone — asks "Delete account?" and, once confirmed, deletes the account. Their recipes are kept — see the note below.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recipe editing

Below the list, admins find the **Recipe editing** card, which holds the household setting that locks recipes to their authors and admins. The owner switches it; every other admin sees what applies right now. [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights) explains how it combines with the setting on each recipe.

## Public sharing

The **Public sharing** card is where the owner turns public links on for the household - after confirming, since from then on anyone a link reaches sees the recipe - and picks the default and the longest lifetime a new link may have; every other admin sees a sentence stating what currently applies. This is the household-wide switch - whether any member may create a public link at all - separate from "May share publicly" per person above. See [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links).

<Screenshot name="public-sharing-card" caption="The Public sharing card, switched on" />

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  The owner is listed under "Owner" and their row carries no actions at all: that account cannot be deleted, cannot lose its admin rights, and its password cannot be reset from here by anyone, the owner included. So the household always keeps at least one admin. You also cannot delete your own account from this page.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="A display name is not a login">
  Renaming somebody changes only what Rezepte shows. They still sign in with their username, which is why the list prints it under the display name. Display names do not have to be unique either, so two people can both be "Mia" — and their recipes stay theirs whatever they are called.
</Callout>

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="What happens to a deleted member's recipes">
    They are not deleted — they are transferred to the admin who performed the deletion, who becomes their author. Where the deleted member was the last to edit somebody else's recipe, that admin takes their place as "Last edited by" too; the recipe's author stays who it was. Each recipe's editing setting stays as it was.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What a Member sees here">
    The same list, with a line saying that admins manage the accounts, and no buttons: no "Add account", no role select, no "Reset password" or "Delete", and no Recipe editing card. On a phone only your own row opens anything — your profile. The same rule is enforced by the API, so the missing buttons are a courtesy, not the only protection. The list shows login names, so everyone in the household can see what the others sign in with — never their passwords.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
