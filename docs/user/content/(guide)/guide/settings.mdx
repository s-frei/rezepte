In Settings you change how Rezepte shows you, your email address and password, and how the app looks. On a shared kitchen tablet, you also switch between people here. People, roles and the household's switches are under [People](/guide/users).

<Screenshot name="settings" caption="The settings page" />

## Profile<NewIn release="v1.4.0" />

### Change your photo, name and color

- **Photo**: click the camera on your photo and choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 10 MiB ([photo limits](/guide/images)). Drag and zoom to frame it, then confirm. To frame it differently later, choose the file again. "Remove photo" puts your initial back.
- **Name**: click the pencil beside your name. The display name is the name on your recipes, up to 64 characters. Save it empty to use your login name again.
- **Color**: tap one of the eight colors. It saves at once. Several people can share a color.

Your login name is shown under your name and cannot be changed here. Renaming yourself does not change how you sign in.

<Screenshot name="avatar-crop" caption="Framing a photo: the round window shows what the circles will show" />

### Confirm your email address

Your email address is optional and never used to sign in. Rezepte sends a link to it when you forget your password, but only once the address is confirmed.

1. Choose **Change**, type the address and save. With mail set up, the button reads **Save and send mail**.
2. Open the link in the mail within 7 days. The badge beside **Email** turns from **unconfirmed** to **confirmed**.

No mail? The box under the address says what happened:

| The box says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Mail sent** | Check your spam folder, or choose **Send again** (once a minute). |
| **Mail not opened** (badge **expired**) | The link ran out. Choose **Send new**. |
| **No mail sent** | Choose **Send**. |
| **Setup link sent** | Open the setup link. Using it confirms the address. |

An address your identity provider vouched for is already confirmed. To remove your address, clear the field and save.

<Screenshot name="profile-email-unconfirmed" caption="An address waiting for its confirmation" />

### Change your password

Choose **Change** in the **Password** row, enter your current password, then the new one (8–128 characters) twice. Your other devices are signed out; this one stays signed in. An account without a password shows **Set** instead.

A bar rates the new password as you type. It is advice: any password of the right length saves.

### Connect a sign-in provider

The **Sign-in** row shows only when your instance offers [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on). **Connect** takes you to the provider and back. From then on, its button on the login page signs you in. **Disconnect** undoes it and signs out your other devices.

<Callout type="warn" title="Set a password before you disconnect">
  Rezepte lets you disconnect only while your account also has a password. Without one, you could no longer sign in.
</Callout>

<Screenshot name="settings-signin" caption="The sign-in row, before the account is connected" />

### Choose language and theme

- **Language**: "English" or "German". It is saved to your account and follows you to every device. It changes the interface, never a recipe's text.
- **Appearance**: "System", "Light" or "Dark". It is saved in this browser only. The same choice sits in the account menu (on a phone, the "You" sheet).

## API

The "Access for programs" card has two parts: "For AI assistants" gives the MCP address and points to an API token, "For your own scripts" the API's base URL and its interactive docs. Each address has a "Copy" button. Admins create tokens in the "API tokens" card below. See [API](/api), [Connect an AI assistant](/api/mcp) and [API tokens](/api/tokens).

## Signing out

"Sign out" ends your session on this device; on a shared device, your card stays ([Share a device](#share-a-device)). It is in the account menu, behind your initial at the top right, and at the foot of the Settings sidebar. On a phone, it is in the "You" sheet.

## Share a device<NewIn release="v1.5.0" />

A kitchen tablet can hold everyone's account, so each person is one tap away from their own recipes and lists. Open "Who's cooking?" at the top of the account menu, or of the "You" sheet on a phone. When you are alone on the device, the row reads "Sign in another person".

<Screenshot name="switch" caption="Who's cooking? lists everyone signed in on this device, the person cooking now on top" />

### Add a person

Tap "Add a person" and sign in as usual, with a password or [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on). That person now has a card.

### Switch person

Tap a card. Rezepte opens that person's view, without a password. Tap the × at the top to go back. Cook timers keep running, and [notifications](/guide/notifications) follow whoever is cooking.

### Remove a person from this device

Tap "Edit", then the × on someone else's card, and confirm. Tap "Done" to finish. Their account and recipes are untouched; they can sign in again with their password.

<Screenshot name="switch-edit" caption="Edit mode: a × on the cards that can be removed" />

### When a card says "Sign in again"

"Sign out" keeps you on the device: your card says "Sign in again", and you type your password once. The same happens after 30 days without use.
