Write down what you learned cooking a recipe, such as "Ours needed 10 more minutes in the oven." The next person who cooks it reads it right there.

<Screenshot name="comments" caption="The comments, opened from their tab beside the method" />

Open **Comments**, the tab beside **Method**. It shows how many comments there are, and a dot when some are new for you.

Everyone in the household can read and write comments on every recipe, locked ones included. A comment is not a change to the recipe (see [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights)).

## Writing a comment

Type into **What did you notice?** and press **Send**. `⌘`+`Enter` or `Ctrl`+`Enter` also sends; see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts) for the rest. A comment is plain text of up to 2000 characters.

## Adding photos

1. Click **Add photos** beside the field, or paste a picture. You can add up to 4.
2. Write a line of text. A comment always needs some.
3. Drag the photos into the order you want. On a phone, hold one first.
4. Send. The photos upload first, then the comment.

If a photo fails, it stays red. Tap it to try again.

Everyone in the household sees a comment's photos.

<Callout type="warn" title="Recipe photos can leave the household">
  If you may edit the recipe, **Add to recipe photos** in the large view turns a comment photo into a recipe photo. Unlike comment photos, recipe photos show on [public links](/guide/sharing) and in [zip exports](/guide/import-export), so think before you add one.
</Callout>

## Editing and deleting

Open the `…` menu on your own comment.

- **Edit** lets you change the text and photos. Choose **Save** or **Cancel**. The comment then shows "edited".
- **Delete** removes the comment for everyone, after you confirm.

Admins can delete anyone's comment, photos included. Only the person who wrote a comment can edit it.

## Seeing what is new

A recipe you added or commented on gets a dot when someone else comments. The dot shows on the recipe's card and its **Comments** tab, and beside each new comment. Opening the **Comments** tab clears it.

## When someone leaves

If an admin removes a member, their comments stay, shown as **Former member**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Comments stay in the household">
  Nobody outside the household sees comments or their photos. They are not on a [public link](/guide/sharing), the recipe image you pass on, or a [zip export](/guide/import-export). Moving your recipes to another instance leaves the comments behind.
</Callout>
