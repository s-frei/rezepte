Every recipe has comments: the place for what the household learned cooking it. "Ours needed 10 more minutes in the oven." "Capers only at the very end." The next person cooking reads them right where they need them, instead of in a chat that scrolled away long ago.

The comments sit behind a tab beside the "Method" heading, on phones as on computers. A recipe always opens on its method; tap or click "Comments" to read them, and "Method" to go back — a half-written comment waits for you. The tab shows how many comments there are, and a dot when some are new for you.

Everyone in the household can read and write comments on every recipe, including locked ones — a comment is not a change to the recipe (see [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights)).

## Writing a comment

Type into "What did you notice?" below the comments. On a computer, press `Enter` or click "Send"; `Shift`+`Enter` starts a new line. On a phone or tablet, `Enter` starts a new line and you tap the round send button. `⌘`+`Enter` or `Ctrl`+`Enter` sends on every device, also from a tablet's keyboard. A comment is plain text of up to 2000 characters; line breaks are kept. Your own comments sit on the right in your color, everyone else's on the left with their picture or initial and name. Comments are grouped by day under "Today", "Yesterday" or the date.

## Adding photos

Click the photo button beside the field ("Add photos") and pick up to 4 pictures, or paste them into the field; a comment still needs some text, and the field asks "Something about your photo?" while photos are waiting. The picked photos show the way the comment will: one wide photo, two side by side, three with one large on the left, or four in a square. Drag a photo to another place to change the order (on a phone, hold it for a moment first); with three, the first one is the large one. Click or tap a photo to see it large, or its × to leave it out.

Nothing is uploaded until you send: the photos go up one after another, each showing its progress, and the comment follows. If a photo fails, it stays marked in red and a line under the field says so; tap or click the photo to try again, and the comment is sent once every photo is up. The photos that already arrived are not uploaded twice. If you give up on a send that failed, the photos that already went up are removed after a day.

A comment's photos show under its text; click one to see it large. Everybody in the household sees them, locked recipes included.

Whoever may edit the recipe can click "Add to recipe photos" in the large view. The photo then becomes one of the recipe's own photos, and the view says "In the recipe photos". Recipe photos, unlike comments, also show on [public links](/guide/sharing) and in [zip exports](/guide/import-export), so think before you add one.

## Editing and deleting

Your own comments have a `…` menu. "Edit" turns the comment into a field with your text; change it, add, remove or reorder photos if you like, and click or tap "Save" (on a computer, `Enter` saves too, and `⌘`+`Enter` or `Ctrl`+`Enter` on every device), or "Cancel" (or `Esc`) to leave it as it was. An edited comment says "edited" beside its time. "Delete" removes it after you confirm, for everyone.

Admins can delete anyone's comment, photos and all, but only the person who wrote it can edit it or change its photos.

## Seeing what is new

When somebody else comments on a recipe you added, or on one you commented on yourself, the recipe's card in the overview gets a small dot on the author's circle, and so does the recipe's "Comments" tab. Among the comments, the new ones carry the same dot beside their time. Opening the tab clears the dot on the card and on the tab; the dots beside the comments stay until you leave the page, so you can still see which ones were new. Opening the recipe without looking at the comments leaves the dots in place.

Your own comments never count as new, and editing a comment does not make it new again. Cook mode and the editor do not clear the dot — only the "Comments" tab does.

## When someone leaves

If an admin removes a member, their recipes move to the admin, but their comments stay as they wrote them, shown as "Former member" with an empty circle.

<Callout type="info" title="Comments stay in the household">
  Nobody outside the household ever sees the comments or their photos. They are not on a [public link](/guide/sharing), not on the recipe image you pass on, and not in a [zip export](/guide/import-export) — which also means that moving your recipes to another instance leaves the comments behind.
</Callout>
