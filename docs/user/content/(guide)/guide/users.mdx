"Settings" → "People" lists every account on this Rezepte, with its display name, login name, color and role. Everyone can read the list. Admins open a person's page to manage their account. Actions you may not take are not shown.

| | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| See everyone and their role | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change your own profile | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit someone else's recipe | if open | ✓ | ✓ |
| Delete someone else's recipe, or change who may edit it | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Issue [API tokens](/api/tokens) | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Manage a member's account | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Manage an admin's account | – | – | ✓ |
| Give or take back "Admin" | – | – | ✓ |
| Change someone else's name, color or photo | – | – | ✓ |
| Set the household's switches | – | – | ✓ |
| Manage the owner's account | – | – | – |
| Delete your own account | – | – | – |

- **Your own profile** is your password, display name, color and photo.
- **Edit someone else's recipe**: "if open" means the recipe is set to **Everyone**, or left on **Default** while the household does not lock recipes ([Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights)).
- **Managing an account** covers adding it, resetting the password, setting the email address, sending a setup link, disconnecting single sign-on, withdrawing or restoring public sharing, and deleting it.
- **The owner's account** cannot be deleted or demoted by anyone. Nobody else can reset its password or withdraw its public sharing.
- **The household's switches** are recipe editing, link previews and public sharing.

<Callout type="info" title="The owner account">
  There is exactly one Owner: the account created on the very first start. It cannot be deleted, demoted or handed to anyone, so the household always keeps an admin. See [First login](/getting-started/first-login).
</Callout>

## Read the list

People are grouped under "Owner", "Admins" and "Members". Each card shows the person's latest recipes and how many shopping lists they have. Only the people a list is shared with can see what is on it.

Admins see two more things on every card:

- **An envelope** after the login name: a check when the address is confirmed, a question mark while it is not, nothing when there is no address.
- **When the person was last active.** After 30 days without activity it turns amber, so unused accounts stand out.

Your own card opens your [profile](/guide/settings#profile). As an admin, the card of anyone you may act on opens their page.

<Callout type="info" title="Login names are visible">
  Everyone in the household can see the login names in this list, never the passwords. A display name is not a login: renaming someone changes only what Rezepte shows.
</Callout>

## A person's page

Everything you may do with one account is on its page: the "Account" group (email, password, setup link, single sign-on) and the "Rights" group (role, public sharing). "Delete account" sits at the end.

<Screenshot name="person" caption="The owner on a member's page" />

## Add an account

1. In "Settings" → "People", choose "Add account".
2. Type a username and pick a role. Only the owner can pick "Admin"; other admins create members.
3. Choose how the person gets in, then create the account.

<Screenshot name="user-create" caption="Creating a new account" />

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Send a setup link" (default) | The account has no password. You get a one-time link, valid for 7 days, and the person picks their own password. With an email address and [mail](/guide/mail) set up, Rezepte mails the link ("Create and send link"). |
| "Set a password now" | You type a password (8–128 characters) twice. Use it when you set up the account together. With an email address and mail set up, a confirmation mail goes out ("Create and send mail"). |

If mail is not set up, or sending fails, the link shows on screen and you pass it on yourself. The account exists either way.

<Screenshot name="setup-link-dialog" caption="After creating an account with an email address: where the link went, and the link as a fallback" />

The optional fields:

- **Email**: saved unconfirmed until the person opens a mailed link.
- **Language**: starts on yours. Only the person can change it later, in their own settings.
- **Display name**: the name on their recipes. Left empty, the login name is used.
- **Color**: any of the eight. Several people can share one.

## Change a role

Use the "Role" select on a person's page. It applies at once, and the message offers "Undo". Only the owner has this control.

## Change someone's name, color, photo or email address

Only the owner changes name, color and photo; the email address follows the account rule in the table above. On the person's page:

- **Photo**: click the camera, then "Add photo", "Change photo" or "Remove photo".
- **Name**: click the pencil beside the name.
- **Color**: pick one of the eight. It saves at once, and the message offers "Undo".
- **Email**: choose "Change" in the "Email" row, type the address and save. Clear the field to remove it.

When mail is set up, saving a new address sends a confirmation mail that names you. The box under the address shows what was sent and lets you send it again ([what each state means](/guide/settings#profile)).

## Allow or withdraw public sharing

Switch "May share publicly" in the "Rights" group. Turning it off pauses the person's public links until you turn it back on. Their links stay in their **Pass on** sheet, marked paused, and they cannot create new ones. The household-wide switch is [below](#public-sharing).

## Reset a password

Choose **Reset** in the **Password** row and type the new password twice. To change your own password, choose **Change** in the **Password** row of your [profile](/guide/settings#change-your-password) instead.

To let the person pick their own password, use the "Setup link" row: "Create link" works like the link from "Add account". With mail set up, choose "Send by mail" or "Pass it on myself". An address you change there becomes the account's new address. A new link replaces the open one.

<Callout type="warn" title="Signs the person out everywhere">
  A reset signs the person out on every device. So does using a setup link.
</Callout>

<Screenshot name="setup-link-send" caption="Sending a setup link: by mail to the address on the account, or passed on yourself" />

## Revoke a setup link

While a link is unused, the card and the "Setup link" row say until when it is open. Choose "Manage", then "Revoke" to end it at once, without a confirmation, or "Create a new link" to replace it.

## Disconnect single sign-on

When a person signs in through [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on), their page has a "Disconnect" row. Disconnecting asks first and signs them out on every device. Without a password, they need a setup link to get back in.

## Delete an account

Choose "Delete account" at the end of the person's page and confirm. You cannot delete your own account or the owner's.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="What happens to a deleted member's recipes">
    They are kept and pass to the admin who deleted the account, who becomes their author. Where the member was the last to edit someone else's recipe, that admin becomes "Last edited by". Each recipe keeps its editing setting. Their [comments](/guide/comments) stay, under "Former member".
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Recipe editing<Audience role="owner" />

The **Recipe editing** card below the list locks recipes to their authors and admins for the whole household. Only the owner switches it; other admins see what applies. See [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights).

## Public sharing<Audience role="owner" />

Below the list, the owner sets how recipes reach people outside the household. Other admins see a sentence saying what applies.

| Switch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show recipes in link previews** | off | A shared link shows the recipe's title, description and cover photo in chat apps, for 15 min, 1 hour or 1 day. Opening the recipe still takes an account. See [Sharing recipes](/guide/sharing#the-household-setting). |
| **Members may share recipes publicly** | off | Allows public links, with a default and a maximum lifetime. Turning it off pauses existing links. |
| **Name Rezepte when sharing** | on | A line at the foot of public pages and recipe images names Rezepte and links to the project. |
| **Name who shared it** | off | Public pages say "Shared by …" with the sharer's display name and photo. |

Switching off a naming switch applies at once, including to links already sent. See [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links).

<Callout type="warn" title="Public means anyone">
  Turning on public sharing asks first: anyone a public link reaches sees the recipe and its photos without signing in, including people outside the household.
</Callout>
