import { Audience } from '@/components/audience';

Everyone with an account finds "People" in Settings: a list of every account on this Rezepte, with its display name, its login name, its color and its role, so it is always clear who takes part and who looks after the accounts. A "Member" account reads the list and nothing more; the actions on this page are for admins.

Admins are not all equal here. Every admin looks after the members; admins themselves belong to the instance owner — the account created on the very first start (see [First login](/getting-started/first-login)). Actions you are not allowed to take are simply not shown, so the list and each person's page already reflect this table:

| | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Cook, add recipes, edit and delete your own | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit someone else's recipe and its photos | if open | ✓ | ✓ |
| Delete someone else's recipe, or change who may edit it | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Lock recipes to their authors for the whole household | – | – | ✓ |
| Change your own password | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change your own display name, color and photo | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| See everyone who has an account, and their role | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add a member, reset a member's password, delete a member | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Set a member's email address, send a member a setup link by mail | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Disconnect a member's single sign-on | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add an **admin**, reset an admin's password, delete an admin | – | – | ✓ |
| Set an **admin's** email address, send an admin a setup link by mail | – | – | ✓ |
| Disconnect an **admin's** single sign-on | – | – | ✓ |
| Hand out the "Admin" role or take it back | – | – | ✓ |
| Change **someone else's** display name, color or photo | – | – | ✓ |
| Issue [API tokens](/api/tokens) | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Withdraw or restore a member's right to share publicly | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Withdraw or restore an **admin's** right to share publicly | – | – | ✓ |
| Turn public links on or off for the household, and set their lifetime | – | – | ✓ |
| Delete or demote the **Owner**, or reset their password | – | – | – |
| Delete your own account | – | – | – |

There is exactly one Owner and it never changes: the role cannot be handed to anyone, which is why "Owner" is not offered when you create an account or change a role.

<Screenshot name="users" caption="The list of people in Settings, as an admin sees it" />

Admins see a small envelope after a login name that shows the state of that person's address: a green check when it is confirmed, so a forgotten-password mail can reach it (except the owner's, and someone who signs in only through a provider gets a hint instead of a link); a question mark while it is not. No envelope means no address. An admin finds the address itself, and its state in words, on the person's page. How to change an address is under [Change someone's name, color, photo or email address](#change-someones-name-color-photo-or-email-address).

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Open the list

    Go to "Settings" → "People". Everyone is listed under their role — "Owner" first, then "Admins", then "Members", with the number of people beside the last two — and by name within each group. Each person has a card with their display name and, under it, the login name; someone signed in through [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on) also shows which provider, and someone with an open setup link shows when it expires. Under a dashed line come the covers of the recipes the person added last, up to four with the newest the largest, the newest one's title and how many recipes they have in all ("No recipes yet" for someone who has none), then how many shopping lists they have. How many lists someone has is all anybody sees here: what is on a list only the people it is shared with can see.

    Everyone's circle shows their photo, or their initial in their color if they have none. The role sits at the end of the card as a label; the owner's carries an "Owner" badge.

    Admins also see on every card how long ago that person was last active, after a small dot: "Active today", "Active 3 days ago", "Active 6 weeks ago", or "No activity recorded yet". The dot is green within a week and an empty ring when nothing is recorded. Signing in, coming back to an open session and using an API token all count, and the value can lag by up to an hour. Anyone idle for 30 days or more stands out in amber, so accounts nobody uses any more are easy to spot.

    A card that leads somewhere ends in an arrow and opens with one click or tap anywhere on it: as an admin, the page of anyone you may act on; for everyone, your own card opens your profile. Every other card only shows who the person is: click or tap their circle for their person card, with name, login name, role and a link to the recipes they wrote.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### A person's page

    Everything you may do with one account is on its page, opened from its card. A trail above it, "People / " and their name, leads back to the list, as does the browser's back button.

    <Screenshot name="person" caption="The owner on a member's page" />

    At the top sit the person's photo or initial on a band in their color, their name, their role and login name. Below come three groups: "Overview" (how many recipes they have with the title of the one they added last, how many shopping lists, the month they joined, and how long ago they were last active, in amber after 30 days), "Account" (email address, password, setup link, and single sign-on when they use it) and "Rights" (role, and whether they may share publicly). "Delete account" sits apart at the very end. The owner also sees the camera on the photo, a pencil beside the name and the eight colors; every other admin sees the person's color without being able to change it.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add an account

    Click "Add account", type a username and pick a role — the fields you cannot leave empty carry a `*` — then choose how the person gets in.

    <Screenshot name="user-create" caption="Creating a new account" />

    "Send a setup link" is the default and the way most accounts should start: it creates the account with no password at all and hands you a one-time link, which you send the person yourself however you normally would — chat, email, in person — or which Rezepte mails to them (see below). You never see or choose their password.

    <Screenshot name="setup-link-dialog" caption="After creating an account with an email address: where the link went, and the link as a fallback" />

    "Email" is optional. Give it and, when your Rezepte can send mail (see [Mail](/guide/mail)), the window says above its buttons that the setup link goes to that address, and the button reads "Create and send link": the account is created and the link goes to that address in an invitation. With "Set a password now" instead, the window says a confirmation mail goes to the address, and the button reads "Create and send mail". The result window then says "Sent to …" with the date the link stops working, and keeps the link below it in case the mail does not arrive. Without mail set up, or if sending fails, you get the link on screen and pass it on yourself — the account exists either way. The address is saved on the account, unconfirmed until the person opens the mailed link, which counts as proof that it is theirs.

    "Set a password now" is for when you are setting the account up together and can hand it over on the spot: type a password twice (8–128 characters), rated as you type against the username and display name you entered.

    "Admin" manages accounts and can do everything a member can; "Member" cooks and edits recipes. The "Admin" option is offered to the owner only — any other admin creates members.

    "Language" starts on the language you are reading right now, on the assumption that a household sets up accounts in the language it speaks. It is only a starting point: the language is the one profile field nobody else can change once the account exists, so the new member switches it themselves in their own settings if you guessed wrong.

    "Display name" is optional: it is the name Rezepte shows on recipes and in this list, and left empty, the login name is used. The color picker starts on the color the fewest accounts hold, and a small dot marks each color somebody already has — you can still pick it. Both can be changed later, by that person or by the owner.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Change a role

    Use the "Role" select on a person's page to change their role. It takes effect at once, and the message that confirms it offers "Undo". Back on the list they sit in the matching group. Only the owner has this control; every other admin reads the role on the page.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Change someone's name, color, photo or email address

    On a person's page the owner changes their photo with the camera on the photo — "Add photo" or "Change photo" works as on your own profile (see [Settings](/guide/settings#profile)), and "Remove photo" puts their initial back — their display name with the pencil beside it, and their color by picking one of the eight; a color applies at once, with "Undo" in the message that confirms it. Name, color and photo are the owner's alone. The address follows the rule for resetting a password: any admin can change it for a member, only the owner for an admin. "Change" in the "Email" row opens a small window with the address.

    A badge beside "Email" says whether the address is confirmed. Type a new one and, when your Rezepte can send mail, the window says before you save that a confirmation mail naming you goes to it, and the button reads "Save and send mail"; without mail the new address stays unconfirmed. While an address is unconfirmed, a box under it says what went out: "Mail sent" with a bar from the day it was sent to the day its link stops working and "Send again" (once a minute per person, shared with their own "Send again"), "Mail not opened" with "Send new" once the link ran out (the badge then says "expired"), "No mail sent" with "Send" when none went, or "Setup link sent" when the address got a setup link, which confirms it once it is used. Clear the field to remove the address. Sending a setup link by mail to a new address (below) is the other way to change it.

    Your own card leads to your profile instead: your own name, color, photo and address live in "Settings" → "Profile", so there is only ever one place to change them (see [Settings](/guide/settings)).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Allow or withdraw public sharing

    "May share publicly" is a switch in the "Rights" group of a person's page. It follows the same rule as resetting a password: any admin can switch it for a member, only the owner for an admin, and nobody for the owner. Turning it off pauses that person's existing public links until it is turned back on, and hides "Share publicly…" on their recipe pages meanwhile. It has nothing to do with whether public links are allowed for the household at all — that is the owner's own **Public sharing** switch, below the list of people. See [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Reset a password

    "Reset" in the "Password" row of a member's page sets a new password for that member and signs them out on every device. Type the new password twice; the reset only goes through when both match. Resetting another admin's password is the owner's alone, and nobody resets their own password here — that is "Change password" in your own settings, which asks for the current one.

    The "Setup link" row below it is the alternative that keeps you from ever knowing their password: its "Create link" works exactly like the link offered when creating an account, and signs them out everywhere too once they use it. When your Rezepte can send mail, it first asks how the link gets there, the same kind of choice as "Add account" offers. "Send by mail" is picked, with the address on the account filled in; one you change becomes the account's new address, and "Send link" mails it. "Pass it on myself" drops the address, and "Show link" gives you the link to pass on yourself. Either way a new link replaces the open one.

    <Screenshot name="setup-link-send" caption="Sending a setup link: by mail to the address on the account, or passed on yourself" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Revoking a link

    While a setup link is unused, the person's card and the "Setup link" row read "Setup link open until" the date it stops working — a week after it was issued. The row's button is then "Manage": "Revoke" ends the link at once, no confirmation needed, and "Create a new link" replaces it. Before anyone has been sent a link at all, the card reads "Not set up yet" instead — unless the person already signs in through [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Disconnect single sign-on

    When your Rezepte uses [single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on) and a person has connected their account to it, their card reads "Signs in with" the provider's name, and their page has a "Disconnect" row with the provider's name. It follows the same rule as resetting a password and signs the person out on every device, so Rezepte asks you to confirm first. It works even when they have no password: their card then loses that line and reads "Not set up yet" instead, and a setup link is how they get back in, with a password or by connecting the provider again.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Delete an account

    "Delete account", at the end of a person's page, asks "Delete account?" and, once confirmed, deletes the account and takes you back to the list. Their recipes are kept — see the note below.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recipe editing<Audience role="owner" />

Below the list, admins find the **Recipe editing** card, which holds the household setting that locks recipes to their authors and admins. The owner switches it; every other admin sees what applies right now. [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights) explains how it combines with the setting on each recipe.

## Public sharing<Audience role="owner" />

The **Public sharing** card is where the owner turns public links on for the household — after confirming, since from then on anyone a link reaches sees the recipe — and picks the default and the longest lifetime a new link may have. This is the household-wide switch — whether any member may create a public link at all — separate from "May share publicly" per person above; every other admin sees a sentence stating what currently applies. The owner also finds **Name Rezepte when sharing** there, on by default: it puts a line at the foot of every public page and every recipe passed on as an image naming Rezepte, with a link to the project. Switching it off takes that line away from public pages at once, for links already sent too, and from every image made afterwards. Below it, **Name who shared it**, off by default, adds "Shared by …" to the foot of a public page: the display name of the member whose link it is, with their photo, or their initial in their color if they have none. Switching it off takes name and photo away at once, for links already sent too. See [Public links](/guide/sharing#public-links).

<Screenshot name="public-sharing-card" caption="The Public sharing card, switched on" />

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  The owner is listed under "Owner" and their card leads nowhere for anyone else and only carries the "Owner" badge: that account cannot be deleted, cannot lose its admin rights, and its password cannot be reset from here by anyone, the owner included. So the household always keeps at least one admin. You also cannot delete your own account from this page.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="A display name is not a login">
  Renaming somebody changes only what Rezepte shows. They still sign in with their username, which is why the list prints it under the display name. Display names do not have to be unique either, so two people can both be "Mia" — and their recipes stay theirs whatever they are called.
</Callout>

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="What happens to a deleted member's recipes">
    They are not deleted — they are transferred to the admin who performed the deletion, who becomes their author. Where the deleted member was the last to edit somebody else's recipe, that admin takes their place as "Last edited by" too; the recipe's author stays who it was. Each recipe's editing setting stays as it was. Their [comments](/guide/comments) stay too, under "Former member" rather than the admin's name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What a Member sees here">
    The same list, with a line saying that admins manage the accounts, and nothing to act on: no "Add account", no person pages, and no Recipe editing card. Every card still shows how many shopping lists and recipes the person has, but no envelope: addresses are the admins' business. Your own card takes you to your profile. The same rule is enforced by the API, so the missing buttons are a courtesy, not the only protection. The list shows login names, so everyone in the household can see what the others sign in with — never their passwords.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
