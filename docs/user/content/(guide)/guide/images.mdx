Add, order and remove a recipe's photos in its editor. Whoever may edit a recipe may change its photos; see [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights).

<Screenshot name="editor-images" caption="The photo section of the editor" />

## Add photos

In the editor's "Photos" section, click the add tile to pick files, or drop image files on the section. Outside a text field, you can also paste a photo with `⌘V` / `Ctrl+V`.

- On a recipe that already exists, photos upload right away.
- On a new recipe, photos upload when you click "Save".
- If an upload fails, the photo gets a red outline. Click "Retry", or remove it from its ⋯ menu.

## Choose the cover

The first photo is the cover. To pick another, open the ⋯ menu on that photo and choose "Make cover".

## Reorder or remove

- Drag a photo to a new place. On a touch screen, hold it for a moment first.
- Or open the photo's ⋯ menu and choose "Move earlier", "Move later" or "Remove".
- With the keyboard, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts).

<Callout type="warn">
  After "Remove", choose "Undo" within a few seconds. Once the message closes or you leave the editor, the photo is deleted for good.
</Callout>

## On the detail page

- On a wide screen, click a thumbnail to show it large, and click the large photo to open the lightbox.
- On a phone, swipe through the photos at the top, and tap one to open the lightbox.

<Screenshot name="lightbox" caption="The lightbox, with the original download link" />

"Download the original" gives you the largest copy Rezepte keeps: your photo scaled to at most 2400 px on its longest side, not the camera's file.

## Formats and limits

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Formats | JPEG, PNG, WebP |
| File size | 10 MiB per photo |
| Photos per recipe | 20 |
| Dimensions | 64 to 12000 px per side, 60 megapixels at most |
