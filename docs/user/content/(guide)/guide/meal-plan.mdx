Plan what you cook on the days of a week, put the ingredients on a shopping list in one go, and share a plan with the people you cook with.

## Find your plans

**Shopping & week** → **Week** opens **Meal plans**, every plan you are in with this week at a glance. Your own plan, **My plan**, is there from the start; plans others share with you follow under **Shared with you**. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

<Screenshot name="plans-overview" caption="Meal plans: each plan with this week at a glance, an invitation on top" />

- Tap a plan to open its week. **Meal plans** in the top bar, or the back arrow on a phone, leads back.
- **Today: …** on a plan opens it at today; **Plan today** adds a meal there.
- A plan with nothing this week but meals later names the next one; tap it to open that day.
- In a plan on a phone, the arrows turn the week. On a computer, the days run on in one strip with today in the middle: scroll it sideways, or use the arrows for one day, with Shift for a week. **Today** brings today back to the middle, and the line above the days jumps to any week.
- To start a plan, choose **New plan** in the top bar, or **+** on a phone. Type a name and choose **Create plan** (**Create** on a phone).

<Screenshot name="plan-week" caption="A shared plan's week on a computer: the days in one strip, Someday above them" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Plan a week

    1. In a plan, choose **Plan a meal** in the top bar on a computer. On a phone, tap **+**. Or tap the **+** of a day.
    2. Pick the day, or **Someday** for a meal without a day yet.
    3. Search a recipe, or type a note such as "leftovers" and choose it as a note.

    A recipe starts at its own servings. On a recipe's page, **Add to plan** on the shopping list stub plans it for this or next week.

    <Screenshot name="plan-week" mobile caption="The same week on a phone: one row per day, the days gone by folded" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Move, change and remove

    Drag a card to another day. On a phone, hold it for a moment first. Or open its "…" and choose **Move to a day…**, which also reaches other weeks. The "…" changes the servings too, and **Remove** has an **Undo** in the toast.

    A tap on a recipe opens it at the planned servings.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Put it on a shopping list

    1. Choose **Put on the list**.
    2. Pick the days, or include **Someday**.
    3. Pick a [list](/guide/shopping-lists), or let Rezepte make a new one.
    4. Deselect what you have at home, and choose the button at the bottom, such as **Add 3 recipes**.

    <Screenshot name="plan-to-list" caption="Putting the rest of the week on a list" />

    Recipes already on one of your lists are left out. The list keeps following the plan: a changed serving count, a moved day or a removed meal reaches it.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Mark staples

    Beside each ingredient, the house marks **We always have it**, such as salt or oil. Staples start deselected whenever this plan's recipes go on a list. The name matches "onion" and "onions" alike.

    Staples belong to one plan and never reach your other plans. To set them up front, open "…", choose **We always have it**, then **Add ingredient** and search. On a list, an item from this plan's recipes has a **We always have it** switch for this plan.

    <Screenshot name="plan-staples" mobile caption="A plan's staples on a phone" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put one meal on a list

1. Open the meal's "…" and choose **Put on a list…**, or **Put on another list…** once it is on one.
2. Pick the list, or let Rezepte make a new one.
3. Deselect what you have at home, and choose **Put on “Weekly shop”**.

The dialog offers only the lists the meal is not on yet. A recipe planned for today or later that is on no list shows **On no list** on its card, which opens the same dialog.

## Plans and lists point at each other

A plan and its lists carry stamps for each other: a calendar for a plan, a basket for a list. Tap a stamp to go there.

| Where | Stamp | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A plan's week, under **On lists** | Each list with its open items | The list |
| A meal's card | Each list the meal is on | The list, at that recipe |
| A meal's "…" | **Open “Weekly shop”** | The list, at that recipe |
| A list, beside a recipe | Its plan and day, such as "Household · Wed" | The plan, at that meal |
| A list's head, under **From plans** | Every plan that feeds the list | The plan |

On a list, you see the plan only if you are in it. Otherwise the stamp reads **Planned by Mila**, or **Planned by you**, and leads nowhere; **Planned** when that person is gone. A recipe put on the list by hand reads **Added on its own**.

## Share a plan

**My plan** stays yours alone; [create a plan](#find-your-plans) to share one.

1. In the plan's header, tap **Share**.
2. Under **Invite people**, pick everyone you want and choose **Invite 2 people**. Each gets a [notification](/guide/notifications).
3. They find the invitation on top of their **Meal plans** and choose **Accept**.

<Screenshot name="plan-share" mobile caption="Sharing a plan: who has access, and who has not answered yet" />

Members plan, move and rename. Only the owner shares further or deletes the plan, and a member leaves with "…" → **Leave plan**. Changes show for everyone within about a second. Deleting a plan also takes its recipes off the lists they were on.

## Show the plan in your calendar

Every plan you are in can appear in a calendar app, one all-day event per meal. A shared plan's meals start with its name, such as "Home · Curry".

1. Open "…" on any plan and choose **Subscribe in calendar**.
2. Choose **Create link** and copy it. On an iPhone or a Mac, **Or open it right in the calendar** subscribes at once.
3. Paste it into your calendar app as a subscribed calendar. The dialog shows the steps for iPhone and Google Calendar.

<Screenshot name="plan-calendar" caption="Subscribing in a calendar app, before the link is made" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Anyone with the link sees your plans">
  The link is shown only when you make it. **Make a new link** or **End subscription** stops the old one at once.
</Callout>

Your calendar app refreshes by itself, often only every few hours, so a change can take that long to show.
