Cook a recipe for more or fewer people, and take its ingredients shopping.

<Screenshot name="servings" caption="The servings stepper on the recipe detail page" />

## Change the number of servings

On a recipe's detail page, use the `−`/`+` stepper under the title (1–99). Every quantity updates at once. "Reset" returns to the recipe's own servings.

- Quantities round the way a cookbook does: quarters, thirds and halves, whole numbers from ten up.
- Units are never converted: "tbsp" stays "tbsp", just multiplied.
- Your choice is remembered per recipe in this browser and carries over into [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode).

<Callout type="info">
  Scaling never changes the recipe for anyone else. To change the servings everyone starts from, edit the recipe's "Servings" field.
</Callout>

## Copy a shopping list

<Screenshot name="shopping-list" caption="The shopping list stub at the end of the ingredients" />

Tick off what you already have at home. Then use the "Shopping list" stub at the bottom of the ingredients:

- The copy button copies the unticked ingredients at your servings, one per line. Pasted into Reminders or Todoist, each line becomes its own item.
- The basket button puts them on one of your [shopping lists](/guide/shopping-lists).

To copy every ingredient with its group and note, for a message, choose "copy" next to the "Ingredients" heading.
