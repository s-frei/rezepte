Cook mode shows one step at a time in large type, with timers and the ingredients a tap away. The screen stays on while it is open.

<Screenshot name="cook-mode" caption="A step in cook mode" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start cooking

    On a recipe's page, click **Cook mode** in the top bar. On a phone, tap **Start cook mode** under the servings.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Move through the steps

    Swipe, tap **Next** and **Back**, or press `←` and `→`. **Done** on the last step takes you back to the recipe.

    An ingredient linked in a step shows its quantity in parentheses, "onion **(1)**", scaled to your servings.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Run timers<NewIn release="v1.3.0" />

    Tap a time in a step, such as "20 minutes", to start its timer. For a range like "20 to 25 minutes", the timer runs for the shorter time. Timers of other steps and recipes run side by side.

    - **See your timers.** They sit above **Next**. On a phone they fold into one capsule; tap it to see them all.
    - **Pause, resume or stop.** Tap a timer's ring to pause or resume it, ✕ to stop it, or its name to go to its step.
    - **When one ends.** A **Done** banner appears and a chime sounds for up to two minutes. The banner stays until you tap **Off**.
    - **Leave cook mode.** Timers keep running, even after a reload. The next one to end shows at the bottom of the screen. A timer that ended during a reload plays its sound only after your first tap.

    Allow notifications when the browser asks. A timer that ends while your phone is locked then sends a notification that opens its step.

    <Screenshot name="cook-mode-timers" caption="Two timers running in cook mode" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### See a step's photo

    A phrase with a small image icon has a [photo linked to it](/guide/recipes#link-a-photo-in-a-step). Tap the phrase to see the photo beside the step, or below it on a phone. Tap it again, or press `Esc`, to close it.

    <Screenshot name="cook-mode-step-photo" caption="A step's photo beside the step in cook mode" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Resize the text

    Tap **Aa** in the header and pick one of five type sizes. The size holds until you leave cook mode.

    A step taller than the screen scrolls. **Whole step** at its end shows only the text and your timers.

    <Screenshot name="cook-mode-type-size" mobile caption="Choosing a type size in cook mode" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Check ingredients

    Tap the **Ingredients** bar at the bottom to open the list. It uses the servings you chose on the recipe, and its `−`/`+` changes them. Items you check off here are checked on the recipe too.

    <Screenshot name="cook-mode-sheet" mobile caption="The ingredient list open in cook mode" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

Press `Esc`, or tap **Back to the recipe** in the header, to leave cook mode.

<Callout type="info" title="Timers stay on this device">
  Another phone or computer does not see your timers. Signing out stops every timer, so the next person on a shared device starts clean.
</Callout>

## When something does not work

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The screen turns off | Keeping it on needs HTTPS. See [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy). |
| No notification arrives | Notifications need HTTPS too. On an iPhone or iPad, they work only in the [app on the home screen](/guide/install-app). |
| You declined notifications | Rezepte does not ask again. Allow them for the site in your browser's settings. |
| You are not sure notifications reach this device | Use **Send test** under [Settings → Notifications](/guide/notifications). |
