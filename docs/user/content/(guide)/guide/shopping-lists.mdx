---
title: Shopping lists
description: Collect recipes and anything else you need on lists, sorted by store department or by recipe
---

<Screenshot name="lists-overview" caption="Your shopping lists, each with its recipes and how much is done" />

"Shopping lists" sits in the top bar on a computer and in the bottom bar on a phone. Every list shows how many items are open and done, and which recipes are on it at how many servings. Your lists are yours alone: nobody else sees what is on them, the [people page](/guide/users) only shows how many you have.

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start a list

    "New list" asks for a name and opens the empty list. You can also start one straight from a recipe, below.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add a recipe

    <Screenshot name="add-to-list" caption="Adding a recipe: the list, the servings and the ingredients to buy" />

    On a recipe's page, tap the basket on the "Shopping list" stub at the end of the ingredients. The window starts at the servings you chose on the page and with every ingredient selected, except those you checked off on the recipe because you already have them. Pick the list (or "+ New list"), change the servings or the selection, and tap "Add … ingredients". The message that follows has "Open list".

    The list takes a copy of the ingredients as they are at that moment. Later changes to the recipe do not reach the list; if the recipe is deleted, its items stay, under its title. Adding the same recipe twice puts it on the list twice, for when you cook it twice that week.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add anything else

    Type into "Add something" at the top of the list and press Enter or `+`. "2 l milk" or "500g flour" become an amount and a name; "coffee" is just a name. Whatever follows a comma becomes a note.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Shop by department

    <Screenshot name="list" caption="A list by department: one row per ingredient, each recipe's amount beside it" />

    The list sorts everything into departments in the order of a typical store walk: Produce, Bakery, Dairy & chilled, Meat & fish, Pantry, Frozen, Drinks, Household, Other. The same ingredient from several recipes is one row, with each recipe's amount listed — "onions" from two recipes is one row with two amounts. Amounts are not added up, since one recipe's "1" and another's "200 g" do not add.

    Tap the circle to check a row off; it moves down to "Done", and tapping it again brings it back. "Remove done" clears everything checked off.

    The counts under the list's name, and on its card in the overview, count these rows. On a computer, the panel beside the list shows its recipes and "Shared with", which for now lists only you as the one who created the list; the cards in the overview say "Only you" for the same reason.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### See it by recipe

    <Screenshot name="list-by-recipe" caption="The same list by recipe, with the typed-in items last" />

    "Recipe" next to the add field groups the list by recipe instead, with what you typed in last under "Without a recipe". A recipe's `…` opens the recipe, changes its servings — every amount of that recipe recalculates, checked items stay checked — or takes it off the list with its ingredients. Your choice of view is remembered on this device.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Move an item to another department

    Tap an item's name. The window shows its amounts and lets you pick another department, or remove the item from the list. Rezepte remembers the choice for that name on every list of everyone on your instance from then on, so teaching it once is enough.

  </Step>
</Steps>

The list's `…` renames it, copies the open items as text — one per line, ready to paste into a reminders app — or deletes it.

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  Lists are personal for now: sharing a list with others in the household, seeing their changes live and a shopping mode for the store are on the [roadmap](/roadmap#shared-shopping-lists).
</Callout>
