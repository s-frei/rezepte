title: Shopping lists
description: Collect recipes and anything else you need on lists, sorted by store department or by recipe
---

<Screenshot name="lists-overview" caption="Your shopping lists, each with its recipes and how much is done" />

"Shopping lists" sits in the top bar on a computer and in the bottom bar on a phone. Every list shows how many items are open and done, and which recipes are on it at how many servings. A list is yours until you [share it](#share-a-list); the [people page](/guide/users) only shows how many you started.

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start a list

    "New list" asks for a name and opens the empty list. You can also start one straight from a recipe, below.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add a recipe

    <Screenshot name="add-to-list" caption="Adding a recipe: the list, the servings and the ingredients to buy" />

    On a recipe's page, tap the basket on the "Shopping list" stub at the end of the ingredients. The window starts at the servings you chose on the page and with every ingredient selected, except those you checked off on the recipe because you already have them. Pick the list (or "+ New list"), change the servings or the selection, and tap "Add … ingredients". The message that follows has "Open list".

    The list takes a copy of the ingredients as they are at that moment. Later changes to the recipe do not reach the list; if the recipe is deleted, its items stay, under its title. Adding the same recipe twice puts it on the list twice, for when you cook it twice that week.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add anything else

    Type into "Add something" at the top of the list and press Enter or `+`. "2 l milk" or "500g flour" become an amount and a name; "coffee" is just a name. Whatever follows a comma becomes a note.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Shop by department

    <Screenshot name="list" caption="A list by department: one row per ingredient, each recipe's amount beside it" />

    The list sorts everything into departments in the order of a typical store walk: Produce, Bakery, Dairy & chilled, Meat & fish, Pantry, Frozen, Drinks, Household, Other. The same ingredient from several recipes is one row, with each recipe's amount listed — "onions" from two recipes is one row with two amounts. Amounts are not added up, since one recipe's "1" and another's "200 g" do not add.

    Tap the circle to check a row off; it moves down to "Done", and tapping it again brings it back. "Remove done" clears everything checked off.

    The counts under the list's name, and on its card in the overview, count these rows. On a computer, the panel beside the list shows its recipes and "Shared with": you as the one who created the list, and everyone it is shared with.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### See it by recipe

    <Screenshot name="list-by-recipe" caption="The same list by recipe, with the typed-in items last" />

    "Recipe" next to the add field groups the list by recipe instead, with what you typed in last under "Without a recipe". A recipe's `…` opens the recipe, changes its servings — every amount of that recipe recalculates, checked items stay checked — or takes it off the list with its ingredients. Your choice of view is remembered on this device.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Move an item to another department

    Tap an item's name. The window shows its amounts and lets you pick another department, or remove the item from the list. Rezepte remembers the choice for that name on every list of everyone on your instance from then on, so teaching it once is enough.

  </Step>
</Steps>

The list's `…` renames it, copies the open items as text — one per line, ready to paste into a reminders app — or deletes it.

## Share a list

<Screenshot name="list-share" caption="Sharing a list: pick a person; those with access are listed below" />

Share a list with anyone who has an account on your instance. On the list, tap **Share** (on a phone, the share icon next to the title), choose a person under "Invite someone" and close the window with **Done**. The person gets a mail and a push notification (see [Notifications](/guide/notifications)); until they answer, the window shows them as "Waiting for an answer". The **×** beside a name withdraws an invitation or removes a member.

<Screenshot name="lists-invitation" caption="An invitation on the overview, with the lists shared with you below" />

An invitation appears at the top of the person's shopping lists, with a dot on "Shopping lists" in the top or bottom bar. **Accept** opens the list; **Decline** turns it down, and you can be invited again. Accepted lists show under "Shared with you", and each card says who owns it, who shares it and who changed it last, for example "Changed by Mila 3 hours ago".

Everyone with access can add recipes and items, tick them off, change servings, move items between departments and rename the list. Only the owner can share it further or delete it. A member leaves with `…` → **Leave list**; the list then disappears from their lists, and they get back on it only if the owner invites them again. Items a member typed in stay on the list, marked "from a former member" if their account is gone.

Only you and the people you invited see a list. An invitation shows its list's name and how many items are open, nothing more, and administrators have no way into a list they were not invited to. If someone is removed while the list is open on their screen, their next change says the list is no longer there and takes them back to their lists.

## Live

Changes show up for everyone who has the list open, within about a second: an item someone ticks in the store is ticked on your screen, too, without reloading. If the connection drops, the list catches up when it is back. Behind a reverse proxy this needs one setting, see [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy#live-lists).

## Shopping mode

<Screenshot name="list-shop" mobile caption="Shopping mode on a phone: big rows, one tap to tick, the add field below" />

<Screenshot name="list-shop-entry" caption="The Shop button beside the list's title opens shopping mode" />

**Shop** on a list opens shopping mode: the list without the bars of the app, with large rows and the add field at the bottom. Tap anywhere on a row to tick it; every recipe's amount sits on its own line. Ticked rows collapse into "Done" at the end. By recipe, a recipe whose items are all ticked disappears from view. The cross at the top leaves the mode.

The screen stays on while you shop, as in [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode) - which needs HTTPS, see [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy).
