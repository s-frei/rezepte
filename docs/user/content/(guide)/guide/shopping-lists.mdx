Put recipes and anything else you need on a list, share it with the household and tick it off in the store.

<Screenshot name="lists-overview" caption="Your shopping lists, each with its recipes and how much is done" />

You find **Shopping lists** in the top bar on a computer and in the bottom bar on a phone. A list is yours alone until you [share it](#share-a-list).

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start a list

    Tap **New list**, type a name and tap **Create**. You can also start one while adding a recipe.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add a recipe

    <Screenshot name="add-to-list" caption="Adding a recipe: the list, the servings and the ingredients to buy" />

    1. On a recipe's page, tap the basket on the **Shopping list** stub at the end of the ingredients.
    2. Pick a list, or **+ New list**.
    3. Change the servings, and deselect what you already have. Ingredients you checked off on the recipe are deselected already.
    4. Tap **Add … ingredients**.

    <Callout type="info" title="A list keeps a copy">
      The list copies the ingredients as they are now. Later changes to the recipe don't reach the list, and deleting the recipe keeps its items. Adding a recipe again adds its amounts once more, for when you cook it twice that week.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add anything else

    Type into **What do you need?** at the top of the list and press `Enter`. Only the name is required.

    - Type "2 l milk" or "500g flour", and Rezepte splits it into amount and name.
    - Text after a comma becomes a note.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Shop by department

    <Screenshot name="list" caption="A list by department: one row per ingredient, each recipe's amount on a line below it" />

    The list sorts items into departments in the order of a typical store walk, from Produce to Other. The same ingredient from several recipes is one row with a total, and each recipe's amount sits below it.

    - Weights and volumes add up across units: "100 ml" and "2 l" make "2.1 l".
    - Different kinds of units stay apart: "1 pc + 200 g".
    - An item without an amount gets its own line, so it is never hidden in a total.

    Tap the circle to check a row off; it moves to **Done**. **Remove done** clears everything checked off.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### See it by recipe

    <Screenshot name="list-by-recipe" caption="The same list by recipe, with the typed-in items last" />

    Choose **Recipe** at the top of the list to group it by recipe. Items you typed in come last, under **Without a recipe**. A recipe's "…" opens it, changes its servings or removes it with its ingredients.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Move an item to another department

    Tap an item's name and pick another department.

    <Callout type="info" title="This changes it for everyone">
      Rezepte remembers the department for that name on every list of everyone on your instance.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

The list's "…" lets you rename it, **Copy as text** for a reminders app, or delete it.

## Share a list

<Screenshot name="list-share" caption="Sharing a list: pick a person; those with access are listed below" />

1. On the list, tap **Share** (on a phone, the share icon next to the title).
2. Under **Invite people**, pick everyone you want.
3. Tap **Invite 2 people**. Each person gets a [notification](/guide/notifications).

The **×** beside a name withdraws an invitation or removes a member.

<Screenshot name="lists-invitation" caption="An invitation on the overview, with the lists shared with you below" />

The invitation shows up at the top of the person's shopping lists. **Accept** adds the list under **Shared with you**. **Decline** turns it down.

| | Members | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add and tick items, change servings, move departments, rename | yes | yes |
| Share further, delete the list | no | yes |
| Leave with "…" → **Leave list** | yes | no |

After you leave, only a new invitation from the owner gets you back on the list. Deleting the list, or the owner's account, removes it for everyone on it.

<Callout type="info" title="Who sees a list">
  Only you and the people you invited see a list. Admins can't open a list they were not invited to. An invitation shows only the list's name and how many items are open. Everyone can see how many lists you have, not what is on them.
</Callout>

## Live

Changes show up within about a second for everyone who has the list open. An item someone ticks in the store is ticked on your screen too, and a shared list shows who ticked each item. If the connection drops, the list catches up once it is back. Behind a reverse proxy, see [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy#live-lists).

## Shopping mode

<Screenshot name="list-shop" mobile caption="Shopping mode on a phone: big rows, one tap to tick" />

Tap **Shop** to get large rows and nothing to type.

- Tap anywhere on a row to tick it.
- Ticked rows collapse into one row at the end, such as "3 done".
- The cross at the top leaves the mode.

The screen stays on while you shop. This needs HTTPS; see [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy).

## From an AI assistant

An assistant connected over [MCP](/api/mcp) can read your lists, add recipes and items, and tick them off. For example: "Put Königsberger Klopse for 6 on Wocheneinkauf". It needs an [API token](/api/tokens) with **Shopping lists** access.
