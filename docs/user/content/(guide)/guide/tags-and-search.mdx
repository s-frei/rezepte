Type into the search box on the overview to find a recipe. Pick tags and filters to narrow the list further.

<Screenshot name="search" caption="Searching the overview" />

Search looks in the title, description, ingredients and tags, and finds parts of words too: "bread" finds "cornbread". With several words, a recipe needs all of them. Type umlauts and ß either way: "kaese" finds "Käse", "suesskartoffel" finds "Süßkartoffel". Plurals find the singular: "tomaten" finds "Tomate". A word of one or two letters only matches the start of a word.

The best matches come first. In the **Best match** order they are grouped by where the search was found: in the title, the tags, the ingredients or the description. Every card outside the title group shows what it was found by, such as **Ingredient: bread rolls**. **Load more** shows the next results.

## Tags

Click into the search box to see your tags, most-used first. Type to narrow them, then pick one.

- Pick a second tag to see recipes with **both** tags, not either.
- Only tags that still match your other choices are offered, with their counts.
- Pick a checked tag again to remove it.

## What can I cook?<NewIn release="v1.6.0" />

Pick the ingredients you have, and the overview shows the recipes that use most of them first, with what each one still needs.

1. Type an ingredient into the search box, such as "potato", and pick it under **Add ingredient**. Add more the same way.
2. Under **Already at home**, tick what else you have. Every card counts it at once.
3. To finish one recipe, tap its **missing** stamp. The row then lists only what that recipe needs, and the card stays where it is while you tick.
4. Tap **Done** when you are through, or press Escape on a keyboard.

<Screenshot name="ingredient-search" caption="Potato and onion, with the soup's missing ingredients in the row" />

The stamp on each card says how close it is:

| Stamp | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **6 missing** | Six ingredients are neither picked nor ticked. |
| **All there** | You have everything. Water never counts as missing. |

An ingredient also finds its compounds: "onion" finds "spring onions", "cheese" finds "cream cheese". It never finds a word that only starts with it, so "egg" does not find "eggplant". Your ingredients and ticks are part of the page address; Rezepte stores nothing. You can pick up to 20 ingredients.

## Filtering

Open **Filters** (on a phone, the funnel beside the search) to narrow the list beyond tags. Each filter you set shows as a chip under the search box. Tap a chip to change it, or its × to remove it.

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Total time, preparation and cooking together. The right end of the slider means **Any**. |
| **Favorites only** | Only the recipes you [starred](/guide/recipes#favorites). Stars are yours alone. |
| **Added by** | Only one person's recipes. Shows once two or more people have added recipes. |
| **Sort by** | **Recently changed** (while you search or pick ingredients, **Best match**), **Newest first**, **A–Z** or **Tastiest** (most [tasty](/guide/recipes#tasty) marks first). Next to the search box on a computer, in Filters on a phone. Remembered on this device. |

**Reset all filters** clears the search, the tags and the filters. It keeps the sort order and your ingredients.

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  Filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a narrowed list or send it to someone. Tags are saved in lowercase, and a recipe can carry up to 20.
</Callout>

## When nothing matches

The overview offers **Reset filters** to clear everything, or **Clear search** when only your search text emptied the list.

<Screenshot name="no-results" caption="No results for a search" />

**Add as new recipe** opens the editor with your search as the title. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**. Without a search term, the button reads **New recipe**.
