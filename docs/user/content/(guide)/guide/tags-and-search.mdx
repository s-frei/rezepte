Type into the search box on the overview to find a recipe. Pick tags and filters to narrow the list further.

<Screenshot name="search" caption="Searching the overview" />

Search matches the start of words in the title, description, ingredients and tags, so "pota" finds "potatoes". Type umlauts either way: "kaese" finds "Käse". **Load more** shows the next results.

## Tags

Click into the search box to see your tags, most-used first. Type to narrow them, then pick one.

- Pick a second tag to see recipes with **both** tags, not either.
- Only tags that still match your other choices are offered, with their counts.
- Pick a checked tag again to remove it.

<Screenshot name="tag-suggestions" caption="Tags suggested while typing into the search box" />

## Filtering

Open **Filters** to narrow the list beyond tags. Each filter you set shows as a chip under the search box. Tap a chip to change it, or its × to remove it.

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Total time, preparation and cooking together. The right end of the slider means **Any**. |
| **Favorites only** | Only the recipes you [starred](/guide/recipes#favorites). Stars are yours alone. |
| **Added by** | Only one person's recipes. Shows once two or more people have added recipes. |
| **Sort by** | **Recently changed**, **Newest first**, **A–Z** or **Tastiest** (most [tasty](/guide/recipes#tasty) marks first). Next to the search box on a computer, in Filters on a phone. Remembered on this device. |

**Reset all filters** clears the search, the tags and the filters. It keeps the sort order.

<Screenshot name="active-filters" caption="A tag and a time limit as chips under the search box" />

<Screenshot name="filters" caption="The filter panel with a time limit set" />

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  Filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a narrowed list or send it to someone. Tags are saved in lowercase, and a recipe can carry up to 20.
</Callout>

## When nothing matches

The overview offers **Reset filters** to clear everything.

<Screenshot name="no-results" caption="No results for a search" />

**Add as new recipe** opens the editor with your search as the title. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**. Without a search term, the button reads **New recipe**.
