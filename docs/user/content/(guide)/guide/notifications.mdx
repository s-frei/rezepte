Choose what Rezepte tells you about the household, by mail, as a push notification, or both. Everything is on until you switch it off under **Settings → Notifications**.

## What arrives

| Notification | Who gets it | By mail | Push |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **New comment** on a recipe that is yours or that you commented on | everyone | yes | yes |
| **New recipes** the others added, once a day | everyone | yes | yes |
| **Tasty**: someone found a recipe of yours tasty | everyone | yes | yes |
| **Shopping lists**: someone invites you to a shopping list | everyone | yes | yes |
| **Invitation accepted**: someone set up their account with your setup link | admins and the owner | yes | yes |
| **Mail delivery failing**: Rezepte can no longer send mail | the owner | no | yes |

You never get a notification for something you did yourself. Tap a push to open the page it is about. While Rezepte is open, the notification also shows inside the app. What each mail looks like is in [Mail](/guide/mail#notification-mails).

**Mail delivery failing**<Audience role="owner" /> names what went wrong. It comes at the first failed mail, then at most once a day until mail goes out again.

Notices that your password, address or sign-in methods changed always come by mail, whatever you set here (see [Security notices always come](/guide/mail#security-notices-always-come)).

## Choose what comes

**Settings → Notifications** has two sections, **By mail** and **Push**. Each change saves at once, and applies to your account on every device.

<Screenshot name="notifications" caption="By mail: your address, a switch for each group and Off | On for each notification" />

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Group switch (**Household**, **Administration**) | Turns all notifications of the group on or off for that channel. Your choices inside the group are kept. |
| **Off** \| **On** | Turns one notification on or off for that channel. For example, comments by push only. |
| Your address | Mail goes only to a confirmed address. Until yours is, the box links to your profile. |
| **Daily new recipes at**<Audience role="owner" /> | The hour new recipes go out, for everyone, 17:00 by default. It uses the server's time zone (see [Configuration](/getting-started/configuration)). |

## Push on this device<NewIn release="v1.3.1" />

Push reaches a device only after you enable it there, once per device and browser. The box **This device** at the top of **Push** shows the state.

<Screenshot name="notifications-push" caption="Push on a device that receives notifications, after a test" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Enable it

    Click **Enable on this device** and allow notifications when the browser asks. The box then says **Receives notifications**. If you already allowed notifications for a [cook timer](/guide/cook-mode) on this device, push is on already.

    In the [installed app](/guide/install-app), a note on the overview offers the same: tap **Turn on notifications**.

    <Screenshot name="push-banner" caption="The notifications note at the top of the overview in the installed app" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Send a test

    Click **Send test**. A notification "Rezepte · Test" arrives on this device within a few seconds.

  </Step>
</Steps>

When the switches under **Push** are grayed out, this device gets nothing yet. They still set what your other devices get.

Push on a device ends when you sign out there, change your password on another device, or don't open Rezepte there for 30 days. Sign in again to get it back.

### When nothing arrives

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The box says notifications are blocked | Allow notifications for Rezepte in the browser's site settings (the icon left of the address) or in the system settings, then come back to the page. |
| The box says push does not work in this browser | The browser has no push, or Rezepte is not on HTTPS. On an iPhone or iPad, see below. |
| The test was sent but never showed | Turn on notifications for your browser or the Rezepte app in the device's settings, and check Focus or Do Not Disturb. |
| The test arrives, the household's notifications don't | Check the group switch and **Off** \| **On** under **Push**. |
| Notifications stopped after a while | Rezepte was not opened there for 30 days. Sign in again. |

  A device gets the notifications of whoever is cooking on it.
</Callout>

## iPhone and iPad

On an iPhone or iPad (iOS 16.4 or later), push works only in the Rezepte app on the home screen, not in Safari.

1. Add Rezepte to the home screen (see [Install the app](/guide/install-app#iphone-and-ipad)).
2. Open it from there and sign in.
3. Enable push in **Settings → Notifications** inside the app.

## Push needs HTTPS<Audience role="owner" />

Browsers send push only to a secure address. On plain HTTP (other than `localhost`), every member's **This device** box says push does not work. Put Rezepte behind a [reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy) with TLS. Mail notifications work either way.
