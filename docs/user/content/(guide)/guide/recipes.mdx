Write a recipe yourself, link its steps to ingredients, times and photos, and mark the ones you love.

<Screenshot name="editor" caption="The recipe editor" />

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Start a recipe

    Click **New recipe** in the top bar. On a phone, tap "+" and then **Write it yourself**. To bring in a recipe from a website or copied text, see [Importing a recipe](/guide/importing).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Fill in the basics

    Under **Basics**, enter:

    - a title, an optional description and the servings
    - **Prep time** and **Cook time** in minutes
    - optionally where it comes from: **Source** for a book, site or person, **Link** for a web address

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add ingredients

    Under **Ingredients**, fill in **Amount**, **Unit**, **Ingredient** and an optional **Note** for each row. **Unit** suggests common units, but you can type any unit. Use **Add group** to split the list into sections, such as "Filling" and "Topping".

    <Screenshot name="editor-ingredients" caption="Ingredient groups in the editor" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add steps and tags

    Under **Steps**, write one step per field; **Add step** adds the next one.

    Under **Tags**, type a tag and press `Enter` or a comma, or pick a suggestion. Text you leave in the field without choosing is not added.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Link ingredients in a step

    A linked word shows its ingredient's quantity on the recipe page, scaled to the servings: "potatoes **(1 kg)**".

    **Accept suggestions.** As you type, Rezepte underlines words it recognizes as ingredients with a dotted line. Put the cursor in one (on a phone, tap it) and click **Accept**. To accept all of them, click the check mark in the "3 suggestions open" row above the steps. Rezepte saves only the suggestions you accept.

    **Link a word yourself.**

    - Type `@` and pick an ingredient to insert its name.
    - Or put the cursor in a word and click **Link word** to link it to any ingredient, for example "meat" to "ground beef".

    **When salt is in two groups**, the card lists both and puts first the one the sentence points to. Pick one and click **Accept**. If Rezepte can't tell which one, the word stays unlinked until you choose.

    **Remove a link** in the list under the step, or delete the word. Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z brings it back. Only the first mention of a word in a step shows a quantity.

    <Callout type="info" title="Names a link can tell apart">
      Each link points at exactly one ingredient. Give every ingredient group its own name: Rezepte won't save two groups with the same name, or two without a name. Two identical rows in one group can't be linked, so rename one, for example "sugar (for the crumble)".
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Mark times in a step

    Rezepte underlines durations such as "25 to 30 minutes" or "1 hour 30 minutes" when they are written in English or German. Put the cursor in one and accept it. A marked time shows a small clock on the recipe page and starts a timer in [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode#run-timers). Text without a number, like "overnight", is never a time.

    <Screenshot name="editor-times" caption="A duration suggested in a step" />

    To mark a time Rezepte missed:

    1. Select the duration.
    2. Click **Mark as time**.
    3. Choose seconds, minutes or hours and click **Set**.

    Like links, times are saved only once you accept them. A recipe saved before times existed shows its suggestions the next time you open it in the editor.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    ### Link a photo in a step<NewIn release="v1.5.0" />

    A phrase in a step can open one of the recipe's photos, for example to show how far to brush the dough.

    1. Select the words, or put the cursor in a single word.
    2. Click **Link photo** (**Photo** on a phone). The button shows up once the recipe has a photo.
    3. Pick the photo and confirm.

    <Screenshot name="editor-step-photo" mobile caption="Picking the photo for a phrase" />

    Each phrase links to one photo. Reordering photos keeps the links. Deleting a photo removes its links, and the words stay as plain text. Remove a link in the list under the step.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Decide who may edit it

    Under **Editing**, choose who besides you may change the recipe, or leave it on **Default** to follow the household setting. See [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights).

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Save

    Click **Save**. A recipe needs a title, servings and at least one named ingredient, marked with `*`. If something is missing, Rezepte marks the field in red and does not save.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
  - Drag the handle of an ingredient or step to reorder it, even into another group. Keyboard moves are in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts).
  - Leaving the editor with unsaved changes asks **Discard changes?** first.
</Callout>

## The detail page

<Screenshot name="recipe-detail" caption="A recipe's detail page" />

The detail page shows servings and times at the top, the ingredients and the **Method**.

- Check off ingredients while you shop or cook. The **Shopping list** stub at the end copies what is left, or its basket puts it on one of your [shopping lists](/guide/shopping-lists). See also [Copy a shopping list](/guide/servings#copy-a-shopping-list).
- A linked word shows its quantity in parentheses, scaled to the [servings](/guide/servings) you choose, here and in [cook mode](/guide/cook-mode).
- A phrase with an image icon opens its photo right at the step.
- The tab next to **Method** holds the household's [comments](/guide/comments). Anyone can add one.

<Screenshot name="recipe-step-photo" caption="A step's photo opened on the detail page" />

**Edit** opens the editor. The "…" menu holds **Copy link** and **Delete**. **Pass on** sends the recipe as an image or a link (see [Sharing recipes](/guide/sharing)). It is also in the "…" menu, and on a phone it is the share button at the top of the page. Who may edit or delete a recipe is in [Editing rights](/guide/editing-rights).

<Callout type="warn" title="Deleting cannot be undone">
  Deleting a recipe also deletes its photos and its public links. You cannot get it back.
</Callout>

## The overview

The overview shows your recipes as cards. To change the card size, use the squares beside **Filters** on a wide screen. On a phone, use the squares at the top right to switch between two cards per row and one. Each device remembers its own choice.

Each card shows who added the recipe and, if someone else changed it last, who did. Tap those circles to open a [person card](/guide#person-cards).

## Favorites

Tap the star on a recipe's page or card to make it a favorite, and tap it again to remove it. Your favorites are private: nobody else sees them. **Favorites only** in the overview's filters shows just them (see [Tags and search](/guide/tags-and-search#filtering)).

## Tasty

Tap the heart to tell the author you liked a recipe. Unlike a favorite, everyone sees a tasty mark: the heart counts them, and the recipe page lists who gave them under **Tasty for**. Tap it again to take your mark back.

You find the heart next to the star on the recipe page and on the overview's cards. You can't mark your own recipes. **Tastiest** in **Sort by** puts the most-liked recipes first.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Limits">
    - Title: up to 200 characters
    - Description: up to 2000 characters
    - Servings: 1–99
    - Preparation and cooking time: up to 1440 minutes (24 h) each
    - Source: up to 200 characters
    - Link: an `http://` or `https://` address, up to 500 characters
    - Tags: up to 20, each up to 40 characters
    - Ingredient groups: 1–20, each with up to 100 ingredients
    - Steps: up to 50, each up to 2000 characters, each with up to 30 linked ingredients, up to 20 times and up to 20 photo links
    - A photo link: one photo per phrase, up to 120 characters of text
    - A marked time: up to 60 characters of text, and at most one week
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
