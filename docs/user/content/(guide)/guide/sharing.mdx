Send a recipe to someone in your household, or to anyone, as an image or a link.

On a recipe's page, **Copy link** in the "…" menu copies the recipe's address. **Pass on** opens a sheet with two tabs, **Image** and **Link**. On a phone, **Pass on** is the share button at the top of the page; on a computer, it is a button in the top bar.

The household link is the recipe's normal address. Whoever opens it needs an account on your instance, so the link does not let anyone in. For people without an account, use a [public link](#public-links).

## The link preview

When you send a link in WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage, Slack or a similar app, the app shows a small card for it. That card shows either the Rezepte logo or the recipe's title, the start of its description and its cover photo.

Once the owner switches previews on, a link from **Copy link** or **Pass on** shows the recipe in the card for a limited time. After that, the link still works, but a new card shows the logo. A preview never shows ingredients, steps or your other recipes. A recipe address copied from the browser's address bar always shows the logo.

<Callout type="warn" title="Anyone with the link sees the card">
  While previews are on, anyone the link reaches in that time, forwarded or not, sees the title, description and cover photo without an account.
</Callout>

## The household setting<Audience role="owner" />

Previews are off until the owner switches on **Show recipes in link previews** under **Settings → People → Link previews**. Then the owner picks how long a shared link shows the recipe: **15 min** (the default), **1 hour** or **1 day**.

- Switching previews off applies at once: no new card shows the recipe, including for links already sent.
- A card a chat already shows stays as it is.
- A new time applies to links shared from then on.

Some chat apps only show a picture from an `https` address; [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy) covers it.

## Pass on as an image

**Pass on** turns the recipe into one picture: cover photo, title, servings, times, every ingredient and every step. Whoever gets it can cook from it without an account or a connection to your instance.

<Screenshot name="recipe-card" caption="Passing a recipe on as an image" />

- On a phone, **Share image** opens the share sheet. In WhatsApp, send a long recipe in HD so it stays sharp.
- On a computer, use **Copy image**, **Save image** or **Share…**.
- Quantities follow the servings you set on the page.
- The picture leaves out the source, tags, other photos and anything that points to your instance.

<Callout type="info">
  Over plain `http`, browsers allow no share sheet or clipboard. You get **Save image**, or you press and hold the picture to share it. See [Reverse proxy](/operations/reverse-proxy).
</Callout>

## Public links

A public link opens the recipe for anyone who has it, with no account, until you revoke it or it expires. The owner decides whether public links are allowed at all (see [People](/guide/users#public-sharing)). Until the owner allows them, the **Link** tab offers only the household link.

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Create a link

    1. On a recipe, choose **Pass on** and switch to **Link**.
    2. Pick **For everyone with the link**.
    3. Pick a lifetime, from **1 day** to **Permanent**, as far as your instance allows.
    4. Press **Create public link**. Nothing is public until you press it.

    <Screenshot name="public-link-sheet" caption="A public link, created in the Link tab of Pass on" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Share it

    Press **Share link** or **Copy link**, and send it however you like. The link opens a read-only page with the recipe's photos, ingredients and steps. Visitors can change the servings and check off ingredients. The page doesn't show your other recipes, and it names you only if the owner turned that on.

    <Screenshot name="public-page" caption="What someone opening the link sees, without an account" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Manage or revoke it

    Once you have a link, a **Shared publicly** marker sits above the recipe's title, and **Manage link** sits at the end of the page. Only you see them: each member has their own link to a recipe. Both open **Pass on** on the **Link** tab.

    - **Change the lifetime:** pick another time. It counts from now, and the address stays the same. Pick the current time again to renew a link that is running out.
    - **Stop sharing:** press **Revoke** and confirm. Choosing **For the household** again does not revoke the link.
    - **See all your links:** **Shared links**, next to **Revoke** or under **Settings → Shared links**, lists every public link you created. Copy, open or revoke them there. Admins see everyone's links.

    <Callout type="warn" title="Revoking cannot be undone">
      A revoked link stops working at once for everyone who has it.
    </Callout>

    <Screenshot name="shared-links" caption="Shared links, as an admin sees everyone's" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="A public link shows the recipe as it is now">
  Anyone with the link sees your later edits too. Revoke the link if the recipe should no longer be public.
</Callout>

- You have one public link per recipe. Sharing the recipe again shows the link you already have.
- An expired link stops working and leaves Shared links on its own.
- Deleting the recipe, or your account, removes its public links.
- A paused link works again when public sharing or your right to share returns. To end a link for good, revoke it.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Why a link shows Paused or Limited">
    A link stops working but stays in Shared links when the owner switches off public sharing, lowers the longest lifetime, or an admin withdraws your right to share. It is marked **Paused** or **Limited** with the reason, and it works again once the setting is reversed. To restart a **Limited** link yourself, pick a lifetime for it. While your right to share is withdrawn, you can't create links or change their time.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
