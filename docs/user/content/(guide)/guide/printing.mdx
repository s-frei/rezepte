---
title: Print or save a recipe as PDF
description: Print a recipe as a cookbook page or a card, or save it as a PDF file
---

Print a recipe on paper, or save and share it as a PDF. Printing prints that PDF, so paper and file look the same, with the quantities for the servings set on the recipe's page.

## Print a recipe

Choose **Print** in the recipe's "…" menu, or press Ctrl+P (⌘P on a Mac). Rezepte makes the PDF with the options you last chose and hands it to your browser:

| Browser | What happens |
|---|---|
| Chrome or Edge on a computer | The print dialog opens with the PDF. |
| Firefox, Safari, a phone or tablet | The PDF opens in a new tab; print or share it from there. |
| A browser that cannot show PDFs | The PDF downloads; open it to print. |

**File → Print** in the browser's menu prints the screen page as it is, not the designed recipe.

## Save or share a PDF

1. Open the recipe and choose **Pass on**.
2. Open the **PDF** tab.
3. Choose **Format**, **Photo** and **Footer**; the preview shows the first page.
4. Choose **Download PDF**, or **Print**.

On a phone with file sharing, **Share PDF** sends the file to another app. If your browser refuses it, the button turns into **Share now**; tap it again.

<Screenshot name="recipe-pdf" caption="The PDF tab of Pass on" />

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Format** | **Cookbook · A4** or **Card · A5** |
| **Photo** | Show the cover photo or leave it out |
| **Footer** | A QR code to the recipe and who added it; off, only the source |

The options are remembered in this browser. The footer adds "Rezepte" only while the owner's **Name Rezepte when sharing** switch is on ([Public sharing](/guide/users#public-sharing)).

<Callout type="info" title="No PDF tab or Print?">
  Your host may have PDFs off on this server. Ctrl+P (⌘P on a Mac) then prints the screen page as it is.
</Callout>

A recipe longer than a page carries its title with "(cont.)" on the next page, and a page that goes on says so at its foot.

Admins can also export many recipes at once, as one cookbook or one PDF each; see [Import and export](/guide/import-export#as-pdf).
