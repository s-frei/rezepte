By default, everyone in the household may edit every recipe. Two settings narrow that: one for the whole household, set by the owner, and one on each recipe, set by its author.

<Screenshot name="editor-editing" caption="The Editing section at the end of the editor" />

## The household setting<Audience role="owner" />

In **Settings → People**, the owner switches on **Only authors and admins edit recipes** under **Recipe editing**. Every recipe left on **Default** can then be edited only by its author and admins. The switch applies at once. Admins see the card, but only the owner can change it.

<Screenshot name="settings-recipe-editing" caption="The Recipe editing card on the People page" />

## The setting on a recipe

The last section of the editor, **Editing**, has three choices. A new recipe starts on **Default**. Only the author and admins can change it.

| Household setting | Recipe | Who may edit |
|---|---|---|
| off | **Default** | everyone |
| on | **Default** | author and admins |
| off or on | **Everyone** | everyone |
| off or on | **Locked** | author and admins |

A recipe set to **Everyone** or **Locked** keeps that setting when the owner flips the household switch. Only recipes left on **Default** follow it.

## Who may do what

| | Edit and photos | Delete | Change the recipe's setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| The author | always | always | always |
| Admins and the owner | always | always | always |
| Other members, recipe open | yes | no | no |
| Other members, recipe locked | no | no | no |

Deleting is never opened up: only the author and admins can delete a recipe.

When a recipe is locked for you, **Edit** and **Delete** are hidden. A line at the bottom of a locked recipe says who can edit it.

Editing rights cover only the recipe itself. Everyone can [comment](/guide/comments) on every recipe, locked ones included, and star it. Everyone except the author can mark it tasty.

When an admin deletes a member, the member's recipes pass to that admin, with their settings unchanged.

## Through the API

<Callout type="warn" title="Tokens act as their admin">
  An [API token](/api/tokens) acts as the admin who issued it. With the scopes for it, a token can edit and delete locked recipes too. Only the owner can change the household setting, in the browser.
</Callout>
