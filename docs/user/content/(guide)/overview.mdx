<Hero name="guests" alt="Rezepte: the waving cookbook mascot on a stack of cookbooks at a kitchen table, with a dill, a marjoram and a cinnamon sprite" />

Rezepte keeps your household's recipes in one place. You run it yourself: one program and one data folder.

- Invite people with a setup link.
- Write recipes with ingredients, steps and photos, or import them from a link.
- Find them by tag and search.
- Share shopping lists, and cook step by step from your phone.
