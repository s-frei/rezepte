This is the first release of Rezepte, published as a release candidate: everything below is what 1.0.0 is meant to ship, and it is out for you to try before it is called stable. Because it is a prerelease, `latest` does not point at it; pull `ghcr.io/s-frei/rezepte:1.0.0-rc1`, or pick an archive from the release page, and follow [Quick start](/getting-started). Try it on a fresh data directory, or [back up](/operations/data-and-backup) the one you use.

## Your recipes in one place

Rezepte is one binary, or one container, that keeps your household's recipes in an SQLite database and your photos next to it in a single data directory. Write a recipe with ingredient groups, preparation steps, tags, times and photos, and find it again by title, ingredient, tag, time, favorite or author. The [Recipes](/guide/recipes) and [Tags and search](/guide/tags-and-search) guides cover it, and `--demo` starts an instance filled with sample recipes to look around in.

## Cook from your phone

Cook mode shows one step at a time in large type, with the ingredient list a tap away and a choice of five text sizes. Steps can link the ingredients they use, so the amount shows right in the step, scaled to the servings you chose. See [Cook mode](/guide/cook-mode) and [Servings](/guide/servings).

## Let programs and AI assistants in

Every member can issue API tokens under Settings → API, each limited to reading, to reading and writing, or to full access that may also delete. The same token connects an AI assistant to the MCP endpoint at `/mcp`, and the token dialog hands you the command or configuration to paste. See [API tokens](/api/tokens) and [Connect an AI assistant](/api/mcp).
