Rezepte 1.1.0 changes nothing you have to adapt: [back up your data directory](/operations/data-and-backup), pull `ghcr.io/s-frei/rezepte:1.1.0` and start it, as [Updates](/operations/updates) describes. Profile photos live in a new `avatars/` folder beside `images/`; if your backup copies only `images/`, switch to copying the whole data directory, as the backup page now shows.

## Invite people with a link

"Add account" no longer asks you to choose a password for someone else. Leave "Send a setup link" selected and you get a one-time link to send them however you like; opening it greets them by name and lets them pick their own password. The link works for a week, and the list of people shows who has not set up their account yet. "Set a password now" is still there for setting an account up together in person. See [People](/guide/users).

## Sign in with Google or your own provider

An instance can name one OpenID Connect provider - Google, or a self-hosted one such as Authelia, Authentik, Pocket ID or Keycloak - and members who connect their account sign in with "Continue with …" instead of a password. Nobody gets an account just by having one at the provider: an existing member connects it under Settings → Sign-in, or chooses it when opening their setup link. Admins see who signs in through the provider and can disconnect it. [Single sign-on](/operations/single-sign-on) walks through setting it up; it takes three new settings and is off until you set them.

## Pass a recipe on as an image

**Pass on** turns a recipe into one picture - cover photo, ingredients at the servings you set, and every step - for WhatsApp, Signal or any messenger. Whoever gets it needs no account and no link to your instance. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a computer you copy or save it. See [Pass on as an image](/guide/sharing#pass-on-as-an-image).

<Screenshot name="recipe-card" caption="Passing a recipe on as an image" />

## Move recipes to another Rezepte

Admins find a new **Import & export** tab in Settings. Tick the recipes to pass on and download them with their photos as one zip; on the other instance, the import lists what is in the file and marks recipes that are already there before anything is saved. It is for moving recipes, not a backup. See [Import and export](/guide/import-export).

## Faces for the household

Everyone can add a profile photo and frame it in a round window; it shows wherever Rezepte shows you, and your initial in your color stays the fallback. Every person circle now opens a person card with name, role and "Recipes by …", which narrows the overview to that person. See [Profile](/guide/settings#profile) and [Person cards](/guide#person-cards).

## API

- Setup links: `POST /api/v1/users` accepts an account without `password` and returns a setup link with it (only with a session; an API token still has to send a password). `/api/v1/users/{id}/setup-link` issues and revokes one, `/api/v1/auth/setup/*` redeems it.
- Single sign-on: `/api/v1/auth/oidc` says whether it is offered, and `/api/v1/auth/me/identity` and `/api/v1/users/{id}/identity` show and disconnect a connected provider. Accounts carry `hasPassword`, `hasIdentity` and `setupLinkExpiresAt` in the list of users.
- Profiles have an optional `email` with `emailVerified`; people and authors carry an `avatarId`, set and removed through `/api/v1/auth/me/avatar` and `/api/v1/users/{id}/avatar`.
- `GET /api/v1/export` and `POST /api/v1/import` move recipes with their photos as a zip; recipes carry `imageCount` and `imageBytes`.
- The OpenAPI document now also describes the photo, profile-photo, public-image, link-preview and MCP routes.

The [API reference](/api/reference) lists every endpoint.

## Also in this release

- **New:** "Shopping list" at the end of the ingredients copies everything you have not ticked off, one item per line, at the servings you chose; "copy" beside the heading copies the whole list. See [Copy a shopping list](/guide/servings#copy-a-shopping-list).
- **New:** Public pages and shared images name Rezepte at their foot; the owner can switch that off under **Name Rezepte when sharing**.
- **New:** An optional email address on your profile. It is not used for anything yet.
- **New:** The theme switch sits in the account menu, in the "You" sheet on a phone and on shared pages.
- **New:** A waving cookbook replaces the onion as the logo, and the login page and the setup link's page are set in a kitchen scene.
- **New:** The demo's people go by made-up pantry names and include one with an open setup link.
- **Improved:** A recipe card shows its time as a label above the title, so it no longer breaks onto two lines on a phone.
- **Fixed:** Dropdown menus stand apart from the page in dark mode.
